Kling AI has released Kling 4.0, adding stereo audio, lip sync in more than ten languages and a faster, lower-cost Flash version. The update also expands the model’s reference inputs and lets users modify elements of an existing video. But a comparison with Wan 3.0 and Seedance 2.5 shows no clear lead on video quality, leaving Kling’s strongest case in this release less about better-looking generations than about control and workflow.
