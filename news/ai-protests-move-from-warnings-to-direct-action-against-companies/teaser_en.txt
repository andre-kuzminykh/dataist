A small group of AI opponents disrupted a technology-industry dinner at London’s Claremont hotel, where a senior Nvidia representative was due to speak. Their protest was modest; its target was not. As AI companies build more infrastructure and make increasingly alarming claims about the risks of their technology, campaigners are moving from online warnings to direct action against the people and institutions behind it. The harder question is whether that shift will widen the movement—or push potential supporters away.
