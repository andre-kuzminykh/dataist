Google updated the Gemini Omni Flash video model to version 1.1. Now the scene continuation function analyzes up to 10 seconds of the original clip instead of just the last second, so the result should be more visually consistent. A scene can be extended in 10-second segments—up to a maximum of 40 seconds. Developers can also upload up to 3 seconds of external video as a style reference, transfer characters and motion patterns from it, and use the initial and final frames to create camera movement between keyframes.
