Nvidia said its investments in private companies are now valued at $65.5 billion, up from $22 billion in January. In the latest quarter alone, the company put $16 billion into equity investments; in the first nine months of the fiscal year, it invested $58 billion. The total includes purchases of shares in both private and public companies. The portfolio’s value has risen through both new investments and the revaluation of existing stakes. But after Jensen Huang’s departure, the future of this investment strategy is unclear: investors expect interim leader Colette Kress to invest more cautiously.
