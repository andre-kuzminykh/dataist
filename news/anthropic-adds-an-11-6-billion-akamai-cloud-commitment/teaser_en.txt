Anthropic has struck an $11.6 billion cloud deal with Akamai, adding another major computing commitment to a spending run that has reached $517 billion in reported capacity deals in 11 months. The announcement matters less as a standalone supplier win than as evidence of how aggressively AI labs are reserving infrastructure ahead of revenue they expect to earn. A month after committing $45 billion to Nscale, Anthropic is making the same financial bet again: capacity first, commercial proof later.
