AI agents turn data governance into an operational problem: companies need to know which agents exist, what they can access and whether their business context is still accurate. Collibra founder Van de Meule argues that the answer is not just better models, but a system of inventories, approval checks and machine-readable contracts that can govern agents as they act. The case is timely: companies are encouraging employees to build agents, while the cost of errors, unauthorized access and repeated model work rises with every deployment.
