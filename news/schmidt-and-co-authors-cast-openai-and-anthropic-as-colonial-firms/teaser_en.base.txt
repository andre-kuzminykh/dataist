Eric Schmidt, Henry Farrell Ferguson and Colin Kahl Klaassen argue that the AI race is becoming a contest over who can afford to build, power and control the technology. In an essay for Foreign Affairs, they compare OpenAI and Anthropic to colonial trading companies: private firms with state backing, but enough influence to shape government policy. The comparison points to a larger vulnerability. AI leadership depends on resources and capital that neither the United States nor China can secure without trade-offs—and a Taiwan crisis could expose how much the race still relies on one island.
