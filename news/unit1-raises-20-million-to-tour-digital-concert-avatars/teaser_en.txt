Unit1, a British startup founded by former Lloyd Webber entertainment executive Barney Ragg, has raised $20 million to build concerts around digital avatars of living and dead musicians. Its pitch is less a new venue than a portable format: combine archival recordings with performances captured from actors and musicians, then put the result on tour without constructing an Abba Voyage-style hall. If the model works, one production could travel to several countries while live musicians share the stage with digital performers.
