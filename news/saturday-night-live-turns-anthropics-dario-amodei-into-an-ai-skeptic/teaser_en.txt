Saturday Night Live used Dario Amodei, Anthropic’s chief executive, as the target of a sketch about the contradictions in AI leaders’ public warnings. The joke was not that Amodei had a new position to announce, but that the people building AI can sound alarmed by the very technology they are selling. The sketch turned that tension into a character: uneasy, divided against herself, and unsure how much reassurance the audience should take from claims about AI’s potential benefits.
