Cerebras CEO Andrew Feldman argued that the AI debate gives too much airtime to extinction scenarios and too little to what the technology could make possible. He called for leaders to discuss both the risks and the potential benefits, including a future in which today’s grandchildren “never meet a person who died of cancer.” His case comes from a company with a direct stake in AI’s growth: Cerebras counts OpenAI, Block, Figma, pharmaceutical group GSK and Lovable among its customers.
