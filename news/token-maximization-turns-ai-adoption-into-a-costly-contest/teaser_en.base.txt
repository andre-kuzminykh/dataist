Token-maximization is turning AI adoption into a spending contest. Companies are tracking, ranking and sometimes rewarding employees for using more tokens, even when that usage has no demonstrated link to productivity, customer experience or revenue. The practice surfaced publicly through Meta’s short-lived Claudeonomics leaderboard, but reports describe similar behavior at Amazon, JP Morgan, Disney and elsewhere. What looks like an AI usage metric is becoming a budget target — and a way to make inefficient work appear productive.
