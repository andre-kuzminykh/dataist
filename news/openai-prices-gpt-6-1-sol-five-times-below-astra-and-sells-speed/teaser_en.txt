OpenAI has added GPT-6.1 Sol, a lower-cost model it says has narrowed the gap with GPT-6 Astra, and introduced Ultrafast, a paid inference mode that can generate up to 300 tokens per second. The two launches put different costs on the same engineering trade-off: Sol makes repeated agent work cheaper, while Ultrafast charges a premium to reduce waiting. For teams building AI into workflows, the choice is increasingly not just which model to use, but how much speed each step is worth.
