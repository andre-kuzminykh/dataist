Autoheal is pitching a shared operating layer for the work that follows AI-generated code: investigating incidents, fixing vulnerabilities, preparing releases and handling complex support cases. The company says its platform coordinates specialized agents across engineering tools, then evaluates and adjusts their performance. Alongside the launch, Autoheal announced a $7.9 million seed round led by Innovation Endeavors. The harder question is not whether agents can take on these tasks, but whether a company can trust them across teams—and know what that work will cost.
