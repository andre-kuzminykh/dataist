One AI does not have to do everything well—if it knows when to call in another.

The authors propose a system that routes each task to the model or tool best suited to handle it, weighing answer quality against time and computing cost. They test this approach across language, images, video, speech, coding, workplace tasks, and robot control, finding that smart routing can improve results while using fewer resources.

In this review we look at how the system chooses among specialized AI models, what kinds of tasks benefit most, and what this approach could mean for building more capable AI.
