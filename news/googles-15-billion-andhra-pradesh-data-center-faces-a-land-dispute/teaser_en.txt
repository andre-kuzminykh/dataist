Google is building a $15 billion AI data-center project in Andhra Pradesh, India, with Adani Group. The plan promises jobs and investment, but residents of Tarluwada say land was taken from them and promised compensation and work have not materialized. The project’s scale is also disputed: public descriptions call it gigawatt-scale, while state environmental permits allow 2.51 GW. The gap matters in a region already facing heat and water scarcity, and where approvals came without public hearings.
