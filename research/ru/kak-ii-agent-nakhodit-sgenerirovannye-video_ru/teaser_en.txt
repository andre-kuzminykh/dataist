A convincing AI-generated video can fool the eye—unless a detector knows where to look.

The authors introduce an AI agent that investigates videos by asking specialist agents to inspect particular moments for visual or audio clues, such as a brief glitch or mismatched sound. It learns both which clues to check and how its specialists can improve, so its verdicts can point to specific evidence rather than rely on an overall impression.

In this review we look at how the system coordinates its inspections, learns from missed clues, and aims to make spotting generated video more reliable.
