Reflection AI’s Beam is an open model built around a different efficiency bet: use fewer tokens to finish a task, rather than compete with the largest models on scale. Released a day after Meta unveiled Muse Spark Open, Beam enters a field where Chinese models still lead on Terminal-Bench 2.1, a benchmark for AI agents working in a command line. Reflection’s case is that comparable performance can cost less when a model uses fewer tokens—but the company has not yet published the price of a completed task.
