Google will extend the retention period for temporary Gemini chats and conversations with history turned off to around two months. The Gemini Apps Privacy Hub previously listed a 72-hour retention period. The change will roll out worldwide in the Gemini app over the coming weeks. Google says the longer period is needed to improve the safety and reliability of its services, respond to users and process feedback. According to Google, temporary conversations are not used to train AI models.

# Google will keep temporary Gemini chats for around two months

## How the settings will change

Google will also separate chat history settings from the use of conversations to train generative models. Previously, both features were controlled by a single toggle, Keep Activity. Users will now be able to save conversations in their history without allowing Google to use them to train models.

If a user previously turned off Keep Activity, both new settings will remain off by default. For users who had Keep Activity on, chat history will stay on. Permission to use conversations for model training can be set separately.

Google did not answer questions about why it is making the change or whether it will affect access to conversations or their review. The company referred to a blog post and the updated Gemini Apps Privacy Hub. They say the longer retention period is needed to respond to users, process feedback, and protect its services and users. Possible system failures are also cited as a reason.

## Retention periods at other companies

For comparison, OpenAI may retain temporary ChatGPT chats for up to 30 days, while Anthropic keeps chats in Claude’s incognito mode for 30 days by default. Companies may retain some data for longer for legal reasons, security purposes, or investigations into rule violations.

Dataist's take: Separating the settings gives users more control over how Google uses conversations to train models. However, temporary Gemini chats will be kept substantially longer than the previous 72 hours, even when history is turned off.
