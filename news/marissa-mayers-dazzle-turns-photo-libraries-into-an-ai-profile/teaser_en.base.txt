Marissa Mayer is betting that a phone’s photo library can tell an AI more about its owner than email can. Her new assistant, Dazzle, builds a picture of a user from photos rather than text-heavy apps, using them to infer interests, tastes and social life. That makes it a different proposition from services such as Muse and Meta’s Instinct—and puts the accuracy of those inferences, and the privacy of the images behind them, at the center of the product.
