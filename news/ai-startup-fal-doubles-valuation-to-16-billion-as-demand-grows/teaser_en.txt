The San Francisco startup Fal, which runs models for generating video, images and audio, raised $1.3 billion in a funding round led by Bessemer Venture Partners. Participants included Nvidia, Sound Ventures and T. Rowe Price. Bloomberg reports that Fal is now valued at $16 billion, twice its April valuation of $8 billion. The company also announced Series E and Series F rounds, which together raised $1.65 billion.
