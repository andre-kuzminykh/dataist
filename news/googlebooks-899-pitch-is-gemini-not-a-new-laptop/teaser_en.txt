Google is launching Googlebook, an $899 laptop designed to make Gemini part of the computer rather than another app or browser tab. It runs Android, adds a desktop version of Chrome and familiar ChromeOS elements, while introducing an AI cursor, voice-created widgets, improved dictation and a tool called Rambler for turning rough notes into readable text. The product matters less as a new laptop category than as Google’s attempt to give Chromebook users a reason to buy new hardware.
