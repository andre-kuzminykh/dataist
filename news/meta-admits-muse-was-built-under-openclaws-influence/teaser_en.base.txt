Meta has acknowledged that Muse was built under strong influence from OpenClaw, after users noticed striking similarities between the two AI agents. Nat Friedman, product lead at Meta Superintelligence Labs, said the resemblance was intentional even though Muse was developed from scratch. The goal, he said, was to make something “like OpenClaw” that could scale to billions of users—a revealing description of Meta’s strategy for turning a developer-loved agent into a mass-market product.
