Supertake has raised $20 million to turn users’ market predictions into stock portfolios with AI agents. Users describe an idea in plain language, and AI refines it through conversation and selects assets. The round included Adjacent, Coinbase Ventures, Union Square Ventures and angel investors, including executives at Meta Superintelligence Labs and Vercel. The service currently works with Coinbase and Robinhood and is available in beta through a waitlist.
