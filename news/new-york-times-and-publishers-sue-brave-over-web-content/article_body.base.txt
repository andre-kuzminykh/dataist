## What the suit alleges

The publishers say Brave’s crawler collected material without giving them a way to prevent it. The Times separately challenges two browser features: one that bypasses paywalls on publications and another that blocks publishers’ ads while displaying Brave’s own.

The lawsuit asks the court to stop Brave from continuing the alleged practices and to award compensation. Brave, founded in 2015, has raised about $250 million, according to services that track private markets. The company did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

The scale of the alleged index is striking, but the account leaves key details unstated: what content it contains, how the crawler operated, and how the disputed features affect publishers in practice. I think those questions matter more than the headline number. The Times has also sued OpenAI and Perplexity, but this case puts a browser’s collection and presentation of web content at the center of the dispute.
