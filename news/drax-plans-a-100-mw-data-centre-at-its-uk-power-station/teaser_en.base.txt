Drax plans to turn its North Yorkshire power station into a data-centre site, with capacity of up to 100 MW expected to come online in the first quarter of 2028. The company says the project could attract more than £20 billion in investment and eventually let it supply the centre directly with electricity. The plan puts a new use for Britain’s largest power station alongside a harder question: whether the country can expand computing capacity without deepening its dependence on carbon-intensive power.
