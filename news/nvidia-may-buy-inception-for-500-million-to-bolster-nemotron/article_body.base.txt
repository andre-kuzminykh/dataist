## How Inception’s models work

Unlike the conventional models behind ChatGPT and Claude, Inception’s models do not generate responses word by word. They gradually improve the response as a whole. The startup’s latest product is Mercury 3.

## Why Nvidia wants the startup

The acquisition is intended to strengthen Nvidia’s open-model lineup, Nemotron, and its physical models for robots. Nvidia is seeking to compete with OpenAI and Anthropic, which are also developing their own inference chips.

Palantir and US government agencies already use Nemotron as an alternative to Chinese models. Acquisition talks began before Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s resignation and after Microsoft expressed interest in Inception.

Inception’s co-founders — professors Stefano Ermon, Aditya Grover, and Volodymyr Kuleshov — are expected to join Nvidia full-time. Initially, neither company commented on the talks.

Dataist’s take: the deal could expand Nvidia’s capabilities in open language models and robotics. But for now, the companies are still in talks and have not publicly confirmed a deal.
