## The product is still undefined

The appointment gives OpenAI a leader whose background is tied to products built around creators, not only to the underlying technology. My read is that this is an attempt to make creator tools a distinct product category inside OpenAI rather than leave them as a side effect of increasingly capable models.

The announcement is quiet about the part that matters most to creators: what will actually ship. It names no product, workflow, release date or business model. The first details may arrive at OpenAI DevDay next week, but for now the company has announced the team before it has shown the product.

That creates a tension at the center of the move. OpenAI is presenting AI as a way to expand human creativity, while leaving unanswered how its tools will fit into the communities and creative businesses Yam has spent more than 13 years serving.
