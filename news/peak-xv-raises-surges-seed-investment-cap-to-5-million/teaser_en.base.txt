Peak XV Partners has raised the maximum investment through its Surge seed program from $3 million to $5 million per company and introduced 18 startups in its latest cohort. The firm, which manages more than $10 billion in assets and invests in India and Southeast Asia, said the average investment per startup has also increased but did not disclose the figure. Surge 12 includes companies aimed at markets around the world, even though more than half of the startups are based in India.
