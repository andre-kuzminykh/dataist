# SEC investigates Nvidia over possible accounting violations

## The market loses ground

Two days after Anthropic became the first AI company to go public, the Nasdaq fell more than 4.5%. The S&P 500 declined for the seventh day in a row.

Against this backdrop, investors are increasingly asking whether the money and debt invested in AI development will pay off. The source says years of concern about this is now intensifying.

## Nvidia under scrutiny, questions about its leadership

Reuters reports that the SEC is investigating Nvidia over “accounting issues.” The commission has not said what it is examining. The investigation could heighten fears of an AI bubble that, according to the source, has supported a significant part of the U.S. economy for several years.

The company and its CEO, Jensen Huang, have already faced scrutiny following reports of his affair with an alleged Chinese spy. Last week, Elon Musk wrote on X that Huang had managed Nvidia’s supply chains well, but that many deals with him were made verbally rather than put into written contracts.

Wired reports that Nvidia’s board was also unhappy that Huang, according to the publication’s sources, had not been sufficiently candid. They said his lies were the main reason the former CEO resigned. The source does not say whether these claims are connected to the SEC investigation.

Nvidia shares have been falling for weeks:

Nvidia shares fell more than 11% on Friday and have lost 40% over the past month. CNBC notes that the price has been falling for seven weeks, the longest losing streak since the 2008 financial crisis. By now, the stock has given back almost all of its gains from the past year and a half.

## Washington's political climate

It is not yet clear what the SEC investigation will lead to. The source notes that the Donald Trump administration has effectively done little to regulate AI.

The political climate in Washington has shifted after this month’s midterm elections. Lawmakers are prepared to summon Huang to Congress for a hearing.

Dataist's take: The SEC probe adds uncertainty around Nvidia at a time when its shares are already in a prolonged decline. The commission has not yet disclosed what specific accounting questions it is examining, so the investigation’s consequences cannot be assessed.
