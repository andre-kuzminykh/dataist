A 30 MW data-center project that Nscale presents as one of its flagship developments is at least six months behind schedule, with only 6.4 MW running. Now the company plans to house hundreds of contractors on The Kalmar, a barge with 500 beds that previously served as a floating migrant detention center. The move addresses a shortage of hotel rooms in Glomfjord, but it comes as workers have described long shifts and inadequate safety practices on the site.
