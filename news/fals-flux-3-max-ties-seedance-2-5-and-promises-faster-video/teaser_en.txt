fal has introduced FLUX 3 Max, a video-generation model built on Black Forest Labs’ FLUX 3 Video. In an image-to-video benchmark, it scores level with Dreamina Seedance 2.5, while fal says it generates video 22 times faster. The benchmark supports a narrower claim than a clear quality win: its uncertainty ranges overlap, and the result says little about text-to-video performance or how the model will behave in production.
