OpenAI plans to combine Chat and Work in ChatGPT under a shared token limit. Tokens measure how much information a model processes and generates, so complex models and tasks will use up the allowance faster. Users who hit the limit will have to wait for it to reset, move to a more expensive plan or buy extra credits. The change keeps ChatGPT on fixed-price subscriptions, but puts a ceiling on how much some users can get from cheaper tiers.
