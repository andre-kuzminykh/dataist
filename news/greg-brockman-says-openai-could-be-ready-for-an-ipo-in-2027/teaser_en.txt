OpenAI co-founder and president Greg Brockman said the company could be ready for an initial public offering in 2027. At a Wall Street Journal conference, he tied his confidence in the business to growth in its enterprise division: in the third quarter, OpenAI’s annualized revenue grew 70% quarter over quarter, while its enterprise business doubled. Brockman said enterprise customers now account for most of OpenAI’s revenue, compared with about 30% previously.
