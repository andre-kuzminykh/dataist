Spotify says it removed about 50 million tracks it classified as AI spam from user recommendations over the past year. The company will keep AI-made music out of editorial playlists when it detects spam, but it does not require individual AI-generated songs to be labeled. That leaves a widening gap: Spotify is building AI tools for listeners and artists even as musicians draw limits around making music with them—and listeners keep choosing songs that sound unmistakably human.
