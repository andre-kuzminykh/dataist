Journalist Olivia Reingold admitted that she used ChatGPT to brighten and sharpen a photo from a Michigan Democrats’ party. She posted it on X, listing what she considered designer items worn by a woman in the image: Valentino shoes, a Chanel bag, a Moncler jacket and a Rolex watch. After the post went up, users noticed signs of editing: detectors flagged the image as AI-generated or AI-edited, and details of the clothing looked disproportionate. Reingold said the woman and the items were real, and that her observation remained accurate.
