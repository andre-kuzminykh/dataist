In South Korea, suspicions of cheating with AI glasses have affected the national tax accountant exam and the eight-hour university entrance test. Before the latter, authorities sent alerts to every phone in the country—a measure usually used in response to a threat of an air strike or nuclear attack. The Ministry of Education reported 111 candidates with electronic devices, 54 of whom were identified thanks to the alerts. The scandal has intensified debate over a system in which a single exam can determine a person’s career and social standing.
