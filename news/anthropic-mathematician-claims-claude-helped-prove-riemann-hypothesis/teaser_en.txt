A mathematician at Anthropic and Harvard University, Levent Alpöge, says he has proved the Riemann hypothesis with Claude. One of mathematics’ most important unsolved problems and a Millennium Prize Problem, it is backed, he says, by millions of lines of Lean code that check the proof. Alpöge has not yet presented a full scientific paper: he claims the proof created with Claude runs to more than 250,000 pages. Other specialists now need to verify the result.
