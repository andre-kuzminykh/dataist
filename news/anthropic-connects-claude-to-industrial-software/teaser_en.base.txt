Anthropic has connected Claude for Manufacturing directly to factory software to automate industrial processes. The plugin works with Autodesk Fusion, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft 365 and Infor through four general-purpose interfaces; other systems can be connected by exporting files to Microsoft 365. Claude handles 17 specialized tasks: preparing estimates from actual inventory, comparing suppliers, creating quality reports and design options, and analyzing sensor data and manuals while equipment is idle. The Infor plugin can also place orders with backup suppliers when deliveries are delayed and reschedule production, subject to human approval.
