OpenAI says GPT-6 Astra helped build a proof connected to a problem physicists had been unable to solve for more than 25 years. The work concerns a lattice formulation of the full Standard Model, the theory of known particles and fundamental forces. Physicist Nathan Craig presented the approach on OpenAI’s blog and in a preprint. Experts in lattice field theory checked the proof, but, Craig says, realistic simulations are still a long way off.
