Google is taking its tuberculosis-screening work beyond a single diagnostic product: it is opening chest X-ray models and adapting them for public-health systems that need to run screening locally. The effort builds on a Google algorithm that has been tested in clinical settings and deployed with partners in six countries. Its importance is practical as much as technical: millions of active TB cases go undetected where radiologists and imaging equipment are scarce, and a model that can be adapted locally may reach places a centralized service cannot.
