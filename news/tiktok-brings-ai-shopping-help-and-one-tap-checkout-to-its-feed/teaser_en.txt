TikTok is adding a conversational AI shopping assistant and one-tap checkout, bringing product questions and purchases into the same app. The assistant can answer questions about product features, delivery, sizing and availability, while remembering a shopper’s preferences during a conversation. Checkout lets users buy from a brand directly in the For You feed, the app’s main algorithmically curated video stream. Together, the features extend TikTok’s shopping push beyond its dedicated marketplace.
