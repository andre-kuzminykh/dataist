A federal judge in San Francisco ordered former OpenAI engineer Alfozan held until trial, rejecting a defense bid for his release on bail. Prosecutors argued that the Saudi government could finance his release and that he might flee to the kingdom; Judge Alex Tse said government-backed bail would not assure that Alfozan stayed in the United States. The decision keeps him in custody as he faces an allegation that he used a device to intercept conversations at OpenAI.
