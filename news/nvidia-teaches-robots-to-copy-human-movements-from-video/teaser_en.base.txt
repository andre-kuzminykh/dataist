Nvidia has introduced GR00T 2, a model that transfers human movements from video to a robotic hand and could reduce the need for remote robot operation during training. Instead of directly turning an image and an instruction into a movement, GR00T 2 predicts the robot’s next action while also generating a short video of the near future. In lab tests, five-fingered robots lit matches, cut bananas and tossed packages onto a conveyor belt; success rates ranged from 10% to 80%, depending on the task. But reliable operation remains limited by factors including the lack of tactile data processing and the slow performance of the language-model planner.
