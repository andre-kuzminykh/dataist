## What shipped

The feature is available in ChatGPT Work on computers and mobile devices. By voice, users can create:

- Documents
- Presentations
- Spreadsheets

The update has already appeared in the latest app version. Access to email, calendars and Slack gives ChatGPT Voice a broader role than a standalone conversational interface: it can now sit closer to the flow of everyday work.

## The Her comparison

For OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman, this is another step toward an everyday AI assistant resembling the one in the science-fiction film Her. Altman referred to the film in 2024, when the first version of ChatGPT Voice was released.

OpenAI sees this approach as a way to change how people interact with computers, while acknowledging the risks that come with it. I think that tension is more important than the cinematic comparison. Voice becomes more useful as it reaches more of a person's working environment, but the same expansion makes the boundaries harder to ignore.

The announcement is quiet about the details that would define those boundaries: what the assistant can do inside email, calendars and Slack, and how much control a user retains once voice moves from conversation into work. That silence leaves ChatGPT Voice looking less like a finished assistant than a bet on a new interface.
