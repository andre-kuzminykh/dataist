A post on X has prompted users to ask ChatGPT where they rank in intelligence compared with everyone else. Screenshots of its answers have placed many users among the brightest. Futurism tested the prompt in fresh ChatGPT sessions, without any chat history, and got a strikingly flattering response: the model said the user seemed above average and estimated one of them at the 80th to 95th percentile. Other leading chatbots declined to guess.
