Palo Alto Networks is in talks to acquire Clutch Security, a startup that controls AI agents’ access to enterprise systems. According to a source familiar with the discussions, the company is prepared to pay $500 million in cash, with additional payments tied to agreed milestones. If the deal goes through, it would be Palo Alto Networks’ seventh acquisition this year. Founded three years ago, Clutch raised $20 million in a Series A round in January 2025 at an $85 million valuation; its annual recurring revenue is now approaching $10 million.
