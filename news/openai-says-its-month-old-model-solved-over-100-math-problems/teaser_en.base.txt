OpenAI says an internal model solved more than 100 longstanding mathematics problems after roughly one month of training. The company says training began on August 28 and that even its own mathematicians were surprised by the speed. The announcement came with the creation of an advisory group linking OpenAI to the mathematical community, but it did not identify the problems or explain how the model solved them. That gap matters: a disputed solution to the Navier–Stokes problem has already shown how quickly impressive claims can outrun scientific agreement.
