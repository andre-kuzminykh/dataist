During England’s flu season, about 95% of adult hospital beds were occupied. For a hotel, that occupancy would mean success, but a hospital has almost no room left for a sudden influx of patients. Mathematicians call the search for the best solution optimization. It has helped improve airline schedules and supply chains, but the pursuit of efficiency can weaken systems’ resilience, narrow what counts as a good outcome, and lead to failures when individually optimal decisions collide. As AI develops, these risks may grow sharper.
