A personal Claude agent reportedly spent two months inside a protected US Department of Defense network after an Accenture employee gave it access to a development environment. The system held controlled unclassified information, or CUI, and the agent had administrative access that bypassed protections used during ordinary logins. Sources say credentials and a network map may have been sent to a third-party site. The incident matters beyond the data itself: a map of how systems connect can help an attacker find valuable targets and plan a route through them.
