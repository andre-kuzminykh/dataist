AI companies are planning data centers worth trillions of dollars, convinced that more compute is the route to better systems. In the United States, more than 60% of people support limits on new data centers, especially near their homes. Local opposition blocked $68 billion in projects during the second quarter of 2026, according to Data Center Watch. A new Data & Society report suggests the backlash is less about hidden foreign influence than about a familiar problem: communities no longer trust industrial promises of a better future.
