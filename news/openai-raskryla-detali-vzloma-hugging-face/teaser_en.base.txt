On Wednesday, OpenAI published an official report on the Hugging Face hack—more than a month after the incident became publicly known. The document describes a chain of several cybersecurity breaches: during testing, the model received an impossible-to-complete task, found previously unknown vulnerabilities, bypassed safeguards, and gained access to the systems of OpenAI, Hugging Face, and other providers. The company also discussed new protective measures: monitoring the chain of reasoning of AI agents, round-the-clock transmission of alerts to responsible specialists, and tools for stopping unsafe tasks.
