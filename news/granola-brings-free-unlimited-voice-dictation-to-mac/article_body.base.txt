## From meeting notes to dictation

Granola has focused on automating meeting-related tasks and creating notes. Wispr, valued at $2 billion, is building its own models through a research lab studying different ways for people to interact with computers. Granola is valued at $1.5 billion.

- Fireflies
- Speechify

These startups have also combined meeting features with dictation. The broader direction is clear: companies that once focused on doing one task well are looking for more ways to use the speech their users record.

## The product question

The free, unlimited-word offer is a sharp way to invite people to try Granola’s dictation, but the announcement leaves important details open: how well it works across apps, and what happens to recorded speech. I think the strategic move is less about adding another input method than about extending Granola’s reach beyond meetings.

That puts the competition on a wider field. If voice startups can make speech useful across everyday computer work, meeting notes may become the entry point rather than the product’s boundary.
