Canva has released Craft, its first image-generation model, built around design templates rather than photographs. The company says that training approach helps the model handle object details and readable text, while allowing users to edit one part of an image without changing the rest. Craft is available to all Canva users in Dream Lab and is also planned for the Canva API and third-party chatbots, including ChatGPT and Gemini, through Canva MCP.
