Google has opened Gemini 4 Argon to a broad set of paying users, 13 days after limiting its initial release to approved cybersecurity specialists. The model is now available through the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Antigravity and eligible Gemini Enterprise plans; individual subscribers can also use it in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search. Its launch price is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, but those rates expire after November 30, 2026.
