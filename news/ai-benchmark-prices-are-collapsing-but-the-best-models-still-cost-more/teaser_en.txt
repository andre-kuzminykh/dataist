Epoch AI says the market price of reaching a fixed AI benchmark result is falling faster than for any earlier technology capable of reshaping industries. Its estimate is striking: reproducing OpenAI o3’s 75% score on GPQA Diamond went from 30 cents per question to four hundredths of a cent in 18 months. But a second MIT study finds that algorithmic efficiency explains only part of the decline—and that the best available model can still cost more per request.
