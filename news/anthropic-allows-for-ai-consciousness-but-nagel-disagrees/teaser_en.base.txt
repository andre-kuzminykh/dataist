Ahead of its planned stock-market debut last week, Anthropic named possible AI consciousness as a risk for investors: if the company concludes that its models can have experiences and matter morally, it may need to impose additional restrictions to protect them. Philosopher Thomas Nagel, whose work has shaped the debate over consciousness, criticized the very idea of caring about models’ well-being. The dispute highlighted a question: why does talking with AI seem like evidence of consciousness, while recognizing images or driving a car does not?
