Google is facing criticism after reports that it may cut off schools from its search engine if they ban AI. Teachers worry that Google’s AI Overviews, which appear above source links and often contain errors, are getting in the way of learning. Users are also asking for a way to search without AI, but Google says it is changing search for schools to support learning: AI Mode and AI Overviews will walk students through problems step by step so they do not get ready-made answers.
