Dropbox is acquiring cloud company Tigris Data for $350 million. The deal is intended to strengthen Dropbox’s AI infrastructure and support the development of DBX Agent Cloud, a data storage platform for companies and developers of models and AI agents. The acquisition is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026. Dropbox also said it does not plan to cut staff as a result of the deal; a month earlier, the company had already laid off 9% of its employees worldwide, the WSJ reported.
