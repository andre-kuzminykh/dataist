OpenAI has created a nine-member mathematics council after an internal model produced a solution to the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize problem and more than 100 other open problems across most areas of mathematics. The council is intended to connect the company with mathematicians and the wider public, but its authority stops well short of overseeing OpenAI’s research. That boundary matters after 25 Fields Medal winners accused AI laboratories of competing to surpass one another with solutions to famous mathematical problems.
