Sakana AI will begin work this month on Sawara, a foundation model. The company aims to train and release a small version in early 2027, followed by a larger one. Sakana AI CEO and co-founder David Ha told TechCrunch that the model is intended not only for national security: the company also wants to support Japanese industry and manufacturing. The smaller version is planned for local deployment, a format that may suit organizations that need to store and process data on their own premises.
