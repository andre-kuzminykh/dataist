Novartis CEO Vas Narasimhan believes AI will reduce the average time to develop a drug from 12 years to eight over the next decade. He disagrees with predictions that AI will help defeat cancer and other diseases within five to ten years: because of limited understanding of human biology, animal studies and clinical trials will still be necessary. Narasimhan also expects the share of drug candidates that succeed in clinical trials to rise from 8% to 12%.
