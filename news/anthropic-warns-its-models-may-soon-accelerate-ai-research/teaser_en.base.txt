Anthropic says its models may soon cross a threshold for AI-driven acceleration in research and development, even as it acknowledges that its current models have not crossed it. The warning appears in an updated S-1, alongside a faster-growing business and rising costs. The company says AI has already helped develop models whose capability jumps it did not recognize at the time. Its concern is that future systems could improve faster than Anthropic can build and deploy the safeguards meant to keep pace.
