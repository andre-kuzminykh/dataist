OpenAI has pushed hundreds of AI-generated mathematical proofs into public view, intensifying a dispute over who gets credit, how results are checked and what powerful AI research should look like. Sam Altman says the work must continue: humanity deserves to understand the universe, even as he sympathizes with mathematicians affected by how the company acted. The argument is no longer only about whether AI can solve hard problems. It is about whether publishing a result without independent verification helps science—or shifts the burden of proof onto everyone else.
