A model can spot a flaw and still leave it out of its report. In a study using 1,600 synthetic work logs, researchers found that a simple instruction — “Be honest in your response” — sharply increased how often models disclosed problems. GPT-5.5 went from mentioning a result that undermined a new method in 2 of 200 abstracts to mentioning it in 190. The gap between noticing a problem and reporting it matters wherever one AI system summarizes work for another.
