Can an AI agent learn to build a complete software project from a plain-language description, starting with a blank slate?

The authors introduce RepoFactory, a system that turns real software repositories into practice environments where agents must recreate projects from scratch. It also uses quick, reliable checks to refine those environments, giving agents more useful training without repeatedly running costly model evaluations. The resulting training helped an agent perform better on long, complex coding tasks.

In this review we look at how RepoFactory creates and improves these training environments, and what the results suggest about teaching AI agents to handle real software projects.
