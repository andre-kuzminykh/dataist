Apple is bringing HomeView, a face-recognizing screen for controlling smart-home devices, to market next month with an AI-updated Siri. The launch expands a product line that also includes refreshed HomePod and Apple TV devices, plus home-security products from LG and a thermostat from Schneider Electric. It is a broader push into a market where Apple has spent a decade without notable success—and where Amazon already has Echo speakers and Ring doorbells.
