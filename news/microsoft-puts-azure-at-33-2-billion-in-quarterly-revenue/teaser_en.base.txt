Microsoft disclosed Azure’s quarterly revenue in dollars for the first time: $33.2 billion. The figure puts the cloud business ahead of Google Cloud’s $30.7 billion for the same quarter, though Google’s revenue grew roughly twice as fast year over year, according to TechCrunch’s calculations. Azure also beat analyst expectations by about $850 million, giving investors a clearer measure of Microsoft’s biggest and fastest-growing business as the three major cloud providers report in dollars.
