Firmus is preparing a $7 billion Australian share offering as its data-center plans face local opposition in Tasmania and its valuation races ahead of its operating business. The proposed IPO could be Australia’s second-largest, after Telstra’s $14 billion share sale in 1997, and among the world’s five largest this year. But the gap between the scale of the ambition and the projects already running is central to the risk: Firmus currently operates only two facilities, in Melbourne and Singapore.
