A team of engineers put OpenAI’s Astra behind the wheel of a Toyota Corolla and sent it to an In-N-Out. The trip was a side project, not a self-driving product test: the model was built to generate text, and the engineers had not prepared it for the drive. Nothing alarming happened. But letting a general-purpose model control a moving car is a striking test of whether AI can reason about the physical world—and a reminder that the ability to act beyond a screen carries risks as well as promise.
