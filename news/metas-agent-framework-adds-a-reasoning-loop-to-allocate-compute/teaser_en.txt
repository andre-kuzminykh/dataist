Meta has proposed giving AI agents a separate reasoning loop to decide what to do next while a task is underway. The approach is meant to help agents spend additional inference compute where it is useful, without changing or fine-tuning the underlying model. In tests across four benchmarks and three models, the framework beat a version that made decisions in a single step in all 12 comparable comparisons at the highest tested budgets. The result points to a different way to scale agents: not just giving them more compute, but letting them decide how to use it.
