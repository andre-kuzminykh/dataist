Australia’s cyber agency is warning that online services may be taken offline without notice as cyber threats grow and AI helps attackers find weaknesses faster. In its annual report, the Australian Signals Directorate (ASD) says it handled 1,500 cyber incidents in the 2025–26 financial year, up 20% on the year before, and alerted outside organisations to more than 4,400 potentially malicious incidents, an 80% increase. The agency says organisations may need to put security ahead of keeping systems available.
