## The launch is also a licensing pitch

The offer is split between discounted API access now and commercial weights for companies that want to operate the model themselves. The open-weights version is promised later, but the announcement gives no release date beyond “in the coming weeks.”

Ideogram announced its 4.5 image-editing model shortly before this launch and also plans to release it with open weights. That makes availability and deployment options part of the competition, not just editing quality.

## What remains untested

The central claim is that Flux 3 Image can edit an image in stages without affecting what should stay unchanged. I think that is the feature to judge this release by: the announcement describes the intended behavior, but gives no examples or measurements showing how reliably the model preserves untouched areas.

The missing detail matters especially for companies weighing commercial weights against API access. Until the open-weights version arrives and the editing behavior can be assessed, the announcement establishes a product and a licensing choice—not yet a demonstrated advantage.
