Alibaba’s Qwen team has published Qwen-Image-2.1, a 7-billion-parameter image model it says outperforms closed alternatives. The model generates and edits RGBA images, handles up to 10 reference images at once, and supports local edits through circles, masks or drawn marks. That combination makes the release notable not just for its model size, but for putting a fairly broad image workflow on hardware such as an RTX 3090.
