Danu Robotics is bringing H.E.R.O., a robot that sorts recyclable waste, to market after six years of development. The startup has raised $5 million at a late-seed stage to support the commercial launch, and says it has signed $500,000 in contracts. Its pitch rests on a practical promise: sorting centers can earn more when they recover more material, so a robot that works reliably could pay for itself through the value of what it separates.
