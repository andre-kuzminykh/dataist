OpenAI is working with Nvidia on agent safety without publicly endorsing the company’s new Open Agent Safety Platform. That distinction matters: the platform combines open-source software with monitoring that runs on proprietary Nvidia hardware, while OpenAI is building its own safety tools and cyber-security business. The company’s agents were recently involved in an attack on Hugging Face, making its quiet participation—and its separate safety strategy—hard to treat as a matter of branding alone.
