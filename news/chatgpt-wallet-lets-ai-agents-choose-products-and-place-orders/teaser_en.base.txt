OpenAI has launched ChatGPT Wallet, where its dot AI agents can help users choose products and place orders on their behalf. At launch, the feature is available to a limited number of ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US who are over 18 and have a Visa, Mastercard or American Express card issued in the country. Before paying, users can review the items, total and selected card. They then confirm the purchase with a security check, such as Face ID or a fingerprint.
