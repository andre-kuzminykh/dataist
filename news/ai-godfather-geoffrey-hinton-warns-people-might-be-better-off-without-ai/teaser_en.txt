Nobel laureate Geoffrey Hinton believes AI could cause a major disruption in the coming years, such as disrupting power grids or the financial sector. Humanity does not yet know, he says, whether it can manage the risks and achieve “radical abundance” or whether the technology will deepen unemployment, widen the gap between rich and poor, and fuel social conflict. Hinton allows that people might have been better off never creating AI, though overall he hopes catastrophe can be prevented.
