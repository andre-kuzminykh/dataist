OpenAI-backed Thrive Holdings has agreed to acquire ADT for $7.6 billion in equity value, giving the investment firm a base in home security as it looks to apply AI across a portfolio of more than 75 companies. The deal’s ambition is clear; the products are not. ADT and Thrive have not said what new AI security offerings they plan to sell, only that they intend to invest in the area and develop new solutions.
