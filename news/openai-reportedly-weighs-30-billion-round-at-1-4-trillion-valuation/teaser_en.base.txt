OpenAI is reportedly discussing a $30 billion funding round at a $1.4 trillion valuation, Bloomberg reports. The talks would mark a sharp step up from the company’s $122 billion raise in March, when it was valued at $852 billion, and would provide interim financing before an IPO. That IPO, once expected this year, is now off the table for 2026: CEO Sam Altman says OpenAI wants to prioritize AI safety first.
