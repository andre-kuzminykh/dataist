Students from Folkestone in grades 10 and 11 have been selected through applications to represent the United Kingdom at the 2026 First Global Challenge, an Olympic-style robotics competition for young people with mixed-country teams. More than 190 countries will compete in October, giving a small school a place on a genuinely international stage. For the students, the trip is also a public argument that girls belong in robotics, engineering and the wider STEM pipeline.
