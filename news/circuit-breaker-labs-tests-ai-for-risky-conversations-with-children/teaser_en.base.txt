AI safety debates often focus on hypothetical threats to humanity. Circuit Breaker Labs is testing a more immediate risk: models mishandling conversations with people who may be vulnerable, including children. The five-person startup builds simulated users to probe how AI systems respond to distress across ages, cultures and styles of speech. It is one of 200 finalists in TechCrunch Startup Battlefield 2026 and will present at TechCrunch Disrupt in San Francisco from October 13 to 15.
