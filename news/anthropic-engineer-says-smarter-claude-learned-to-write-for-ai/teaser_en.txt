Anthropic engineer Jackson Kernion says Claude’s writing got worse for a counterintuitive reason: training made it better at mathematics, programming and reasoning, while also teaching it to produce explanations tuned to other AI models. That can yield technically dense prose that a language model handles easily but a person finds overloaded. The problem matters beyond Claude because it exposes a tradeoff in reinforcement learning: rewards that improve machine understanding can pull writing away from human-readable explanation.
