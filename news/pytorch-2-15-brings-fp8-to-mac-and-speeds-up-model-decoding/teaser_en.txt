PyTorch 2.15 brings FP8 support to Mac and substantially speeds up language-model decoding on Apple silicon. On an M3 Ultra, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B rises from 9.4 to 61.3 tokens per second, while video decoding for Wan2.2 cuts peak memory use from 270 GiB to about 8 GiB. The release is not just a faster-kernel update: it expands what MPS can run directly on the GPU, while setting macOS 15 and Python 3.11 as minimum requirements.
