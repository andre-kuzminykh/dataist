Crusoe has ended its planned $1.25 billion deal to buy 29 gas turbines from Boom Supersonic, a partnership that would have made the AI data-center builder the first customer for Boom’s new power business. The breakup matters because Crusoe’s campuses need power, but not necessarily from the same source: the company says it is matching energy solutions to each site, while Boom is counting on stationary turbines to help fund its passenger-aircraft program.
