People who take part in brain implant trials helped develop a set of recommendations for researchers. The effort was initiated by Ian Burkhart, who received an implant to control his hand in 2014. The recommendations cover 13 areas, from participant compensation and access to brain data to privacy protections and care for the device after a trial ends. The authors propose recognizing volunteers’ contributions, preparing them in advance for possible media attention, and making sure nondisclosure agreements do not prevent them from sharing medical data with their own doctors.
