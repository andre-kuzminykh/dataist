An AI-generated image of College Park Mayor Motley Broom became a warning about how cheaply political disinformation can be made. Six years ago, a political opponent would have needed at least basic image-editing skills to create a compromising picture of her. Now a computer and a prompt can do the job. A mid-June survey found that about 71% of respondents worry AI deepfakes and disinformation will affect elections; two in three fear AI will be used to sexualize women.
