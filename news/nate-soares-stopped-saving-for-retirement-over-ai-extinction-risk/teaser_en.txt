Nate Soares, a leading AI safety researcher, says he has stopped contributing to his retirement account because he believes the chance that AI will cause human extinction in the next decade is greater than 50%. The prediction is stark, but Soares says catastrophe is not inevitable and that he is doing what he can to prevent it. His choice turns a disputed forecast into a personal wager: whether saving for a future is worth it when he thinks that future may not arrive.
