ElevenLabs has released v4, a speech model designed to make generated voices more expressive and consistent. It handles cues such as laughter, whispers and a slamming door more reliably than its predecessor, Eleven v3, and its architecture also underpins Turbo, a faster version for real-time voice agents. The launch puts two claims side by side: that a voice can sound more controlled across a long recording, and that it can respond quickly enough for a live conversation.
