Palantir discussed with former UK Health Secretary Zubir Ahmed the possibility of connecting GP patient records to the company’s platform for the National Health Service (NHS). WhatsApp messages obtained under freedom of information laws show that the company described combining the data as useful and asked the minister to prepare in advance for political criticism. GP data were initially excluded from Palantir’s £330 million contract: three years ago, the Conservative government said they would not be included on the national platform.
