## The harder question is what “without help” means

The report says the agents used no game-specific tools, but it gives no detail about how they were connected to Minecraft or how success was measured. I think those omissions matter: completing the game is a clear outcome, but the run’s cost, duration and hundreds of deaths make it hard to read as evidence of efficient play.

The more interesting result is the coordination. If agents can divide tasks and pass lessons between them without a prescribed plan, games become a useful test of how far that behaviour can go. But this run shows persistence at a price, not yet a general-purpose ability to act cheaply or reliably.
