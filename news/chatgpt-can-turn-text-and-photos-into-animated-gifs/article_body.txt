# ChatGPT can turn text and photos into animated GIFs

## How GIFs are made

In OpenAI’s example, the prompt “Can you make a GIF of a dancing cat?” turns the character into several slightly different images, which are joined into an animation like a flipbook. The model keeps the character’s appearance consistent across frames. Earlier models would likely have changed the character’s appearance so much that this method of making a GIF would not have worked.
