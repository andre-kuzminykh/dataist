Google DeepMind released Gemini 4 Carbon two weeks after Argon, pitching it at engineering work that can keep an AI agent occupied for days. The company says its teams used Carbon on long-running tasks such as tracing root causes and fixing subtle defects across large codebases and low-level software. External evaluations show broad gains over Argon, but also a sharper trade-off: Carbon is more accurate, yet hallucinates more often, and its most demanding tasks can take far longer.
