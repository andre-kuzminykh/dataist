OpenAI has fired three safety researchers after an internal investigation found they handled sensitive information outside the company’s procedures, a spokesperson told The Wall Street Journal. The company said their conduct broke its rules and undermined the trust needed for collaboration. The identities of the researchers, the outside organization involved and the information at issue have not been disclosed. The firings come amid public scrutiny of OpenAI’s safety practices—and leave unanswered whether the researchers had raised concerns internally before the alleged disclosure.
