SpaceX has cut Grok Bot’s usage limits after deciding that the service costs too much to run. The move lands as the recently public company presents AI as a major source of revenue: last month, SpaceX said AI accounted for 52% of its revenue, while its renamed Superintelligence division reported $431 million in profit. But the limits put a qualification on that success: growth and revenue do not, by themselves, show that serving users is economical.
