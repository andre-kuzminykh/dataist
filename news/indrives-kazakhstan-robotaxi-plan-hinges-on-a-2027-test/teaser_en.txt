inDrive plans to test Pony.ai robotaxis in Kazakhstan in 2027, with Nvidia supplying autonomous-driving technology. The agreement is a memorandum of understanding, not a launch commitment: the companies will study adding Pony.ai vehicles to inDrive’s platform. The proposed plan pairs Gen-7 cars with Nvidia’s Hyperion and Halos systems, Alpamayo model and tools for generating synthetic data and testing vehicles across road conditions.
