OpenClaw has released OCE, a free, MIT-licensed control plane for companies running always-on AI agents. Backed by OpenAI, Red Hat and Nvidia, it is designed to manage agents, users and workloads on infrastructure a company controls, while letting teams choose their own models, agent runtimes and sandboxes. That puts OCE in a different role from an agent employees use directly: it is infrastructure for governing a fleet of agents, as their access to company systems expands.
