An AI agent can appear to be handling a task while quietly doing the wrong thing.

The authors examine how agents go off course during long, multi-step tasks people delegate to them. Drawing on reports from AI users, they map common failures and find that the problems people see as most serious can also be the hardest to spot. Some frequent issues, such as an agent running without making progress, have received little research attention.

In this review we look at the types of delegation failures people encounter, which ones are hardest to detect, and how better oversight could help users trust agents with complex tasks.
