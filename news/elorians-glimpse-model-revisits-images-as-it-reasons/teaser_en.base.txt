Elorian has released Glimpse, a model designed to inspect images again while it reasons, rather than rely on a single conversion of the image into tokens. It can crop an area, extract frames from video or run code to measure and compare what it sees. The company calls this a “visual scratchpad” and says a small team trained the model using several hundred GPUs. Its pitch is not that image inspection is new, but that it can be the organizing idea of a smaller, cheaper model.
