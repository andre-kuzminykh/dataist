# Hermes Agent will let employees share AI skills across their company

## Working in Microsoft Teams

Employees will be able to give Hermes tasks and review the results in chat. The Hermes Agent desktop app is available in the Microsoft Store.

The agent automatically turns successfully completed workflows into reusable skills, each made up of a description and code. Collective Wisdom lets employees share these skills with colleagues after review. The knowledge collected stays within the company and is not included in AI providers’ training data.

## Nous Research’s position

Nous Research is part of M12, Microsoft’s venture capital fund. The company says Hermes Agent is the most widely used open-source agent on OpenRouter, processing nearly two trillion tokens a day.

Since the start of this year, Microsoft has also supported OpenClaw, a competing open-source agent designed to run locally on Windows computers. Nous Research describes itself as one of the leading US open-source organizations working in opposition to China’s AI efforts. Recently, the company fine-tuned Alibaba’s Qwen3.8-27B model alongside Arete-27B on “Western values.”

Dataist’s take: Collective Wisdom turns employees’ individual discoveries into shared work skills while keeping them within the company. But skills are reviewed before publication, and the claimed scale of Hermes Agent’s use is based on data from Nous Research itself.
