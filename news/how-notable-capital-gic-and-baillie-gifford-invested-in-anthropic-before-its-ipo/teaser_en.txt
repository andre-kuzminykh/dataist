Notable Capital, Singapore’s sovereign wealth fund GIC and investment management firm Baillie Gifford invested billions of dollars in Anthropic before its IPO. On Wednesday, the company raised $81.3 billion in the largest IPO in history and reached a fully diluted valuation of about $1.5 trillion. By the start of trading the next day, early investors had paper returns of up to about 15 times their investment. Their decisions were based on developers’ interest in Claude, the growing use of models by businesses and Anthropic’s track record of delivering on its plans.
