# US Army proposes private data centers on military bases

## What the Army is proposing

In its announcement, the Army invited private, government and nonprofit organizations to submit competitive proposals. The plan is to lease land for commercial power plants and data centers under long-term contracts.

The sites include Fort Bragg and Fort Hood, as well as the logistics facilities Pine Bluff Arsenal and Tooele Army Depot. The Army prefers projects where the power plant and data center operate as a single complex; proposals to build a data center alone will not be considered.

## Military risks of using AI

The Pentagon has been actively adopting AI in recent years, but its use has already led to serious errors. In September, it emerged that military leaders had nearly sent a capture team to a Chinese cargo ship. An AI chatbot used by an intelligence employee fabricated a cargo declaration listing nonexistent nuclear weapons.

In the early stages of the US war with Iran, an AI system was used to select hundreds of targets for airstrikes. Several months later, it emerged that officials’ excessive reliance on AI had led to a strike on an elementary school in southern Iran. More than a hundred children, teachers and emergency services workers were killed.

Dataist’s take: The plan combines data center construction with the development of on-site power generation at military facilities. But the announcement covers commercial projects on long-term leases, not just infrastructure for the Army’s own needs.
