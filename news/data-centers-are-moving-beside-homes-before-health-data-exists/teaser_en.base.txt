A February 2026 peer-reviewed assessment found no study measuring the health of people living directly beside a data center. That does not make the sites dangerous, but it does make confident claims about safety premature. The evidence instead points to a more ordinary set of exposures: continuous low-frequency noise, diesel exhaust during outages, water consumption, construction disruption, bright security lighting and potentially higher electricity bills. Virginia has now installed seven air monitors around Loudoun County, but the first serious attempt to measure the neighborhood impact is still only testing whether broader monitoring is needed.
