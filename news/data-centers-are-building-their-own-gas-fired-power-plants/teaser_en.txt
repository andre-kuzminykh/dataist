Data centers can wait up to ten years for a grid connection, so developers are increasingly building gas-fired power plants next to their facilities. Cleanview data show that, by October 2026, more than 100 such projects had been announced in the US, with a combined capacity of over 115 GW—more than twice California’s peak electricity demand. Most of the plans emerged since early 2025, but only 4.9 GW of capacity is operating so far, with another 15.8 GW under construction. On-site generation can get a data center up and running faster, but it may also increase greenhouse gas emissions and affect energy prices.
