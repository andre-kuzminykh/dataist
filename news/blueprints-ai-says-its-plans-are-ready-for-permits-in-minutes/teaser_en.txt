Blueprints AI says its software can turn a sketch or PDF into building plans ready for permit submission in minutes. The Santa Monica startup, founded by architect Al Dram and data specialist Coco Tsao, is bringing a second version to TechCrunch Disrupt this week. It promises to generate drawings across a building’s major disciplines, update the full project when one part changes, and check plans against local codes. The pitch is less about replacing architects than shifting their time from drafting toward design.
