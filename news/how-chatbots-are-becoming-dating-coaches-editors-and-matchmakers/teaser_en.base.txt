Google Gemini began as a confidence boost for Sam, a 27-year-old from Stoke-on-Trent, after his ex-girlfriend joked that her sister thought he was unattractive. Now he uses it to encourage him and check his dating messages. A Match Group survey published this month found that 39% of 3,000 single people in the UK have used AI for dating. The figures are higher among younger users, but the stories point to a more complicated role for chatbots: they can help people navigate online dating while making the exchange feel less like their own.
