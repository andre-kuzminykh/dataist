Tenstorrent has raised $1.5 billion at a $6.2 billion valuation, choosing to fund its next stage as an independent company. The chipmaker says the money will expand production of processors designed to run open-weight AI models and generate video. The raise follows a demonstration that its Galaxy servers could run DeepSeek models, CEO Jim Keller told Bloomberg. It puts Tenstorrent among the startups seeking to challenge Nvidia in AI hardware, but the announcement offers no production targets or timeline for the expansion.
