Google Research introduced DeepCrossAttention (DCA), an architecture that changes how information passes between transformer layers. Rather than summing the outputs of all previous layers equally, DCA uses trainable weights and attention across network depth to select which representations to use for each token. In experiments, the model matched a standard transformer’s performance with up to five times fewer training steps and achieved lower perplexity under the same training budget. The number of parameters increased by less than 0.2%.
