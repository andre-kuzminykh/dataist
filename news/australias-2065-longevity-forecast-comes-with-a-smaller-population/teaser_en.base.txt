Australia’s Treasury has published an excerpt from a 40-year forecast that points to a longer-lived but slower-growing country. By 2065/66, life expectancy at birth is projected to reach about 90 years for women and 86 for men, while the population is expected to reach 39.3 million—1.8 million fewer than forecast in 2023. The same outlook says the budget will face sustained pressure from an ageing population, with much of the upside resting on whether Australia can turn AI into productivity.
