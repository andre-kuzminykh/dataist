OpenAI is reportedly discussing a new $30 billion funding round at a $1.25 trillion valuation, below the $1.4 trillion figure Bloomberg reported a month ago. CNBC and Bloomberg say the terms are still being negotiated, and OpenAI declined to comment. The talks come as the company prepares for a possible IPO: it filed confidentially in June, after raising money in March at a post-money valuation of $852 billion.
