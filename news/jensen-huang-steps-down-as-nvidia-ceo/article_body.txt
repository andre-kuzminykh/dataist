# Jensen Huang steps down as Nvidia CEO

[FIGURE:0]

## Why the board opened an investigation

Nvidia announced the investigation on November 8, less than two weeks after The New York Times reported that Huang may have been targeted in a Chinese intelligence operation. The newspaper said the operation was carried out through a relationship with Alice Huang, also known as Huang Le. She and Jensen Huang are reportedly not related. They have been seen together in public at least twice: in June 2024 and May 2025.

On November 11, The New York Times reported that Huang may also have had a relationship with an Nvidia employee who reported to him. A source familiar with the situation told WIRED that this relationship, along with Huang’s business ties to Singapore-based Megaspeed, where Alice previously held a senior position, likely weighed more heavily in the board’s decision to seek his resignation than the espionage allegations did.

Media reports said that last year US intelligence agencies told President Donald Trump about Huang’s relationship with Alice, but he took no action. On November 6, The New York Times and the Financial Times reported that Alice was a spy for China’s Ministry of State Security and had been recruited to gather information about Nvidia’s leader. The Chinese government rejected the claims.

On Sunday, Huang sent employees a letter acknowledging the affair, apologizing to his family and colleagues, and promising to cooperate with the internal investigation. He has been married to Lori Huang since 1985. Both of their children work at Nvidia.

## Business ties and control over public statements

On Monday, WIRED reported that Huang had hired a crisis communications firm last year to manage his personal statements. Starting in October 2025, consultants began working on all press inquiries concerning Huang, both personal and professional.

Around the same time, The New York Times reported that the US Department of Commerce had been investigating whether Megaspeed was helping Chinese companies circumvent US export restrictions through a network of subsidiaries. Media reports said the investigation was halted and handed over to the Department of Justice after Nvidia general counsel Tim Teter asked about it.

Huang has publicly criticized export restrictions. In his view, they unnecessarily shrink the market for US chips, divide the technology industries of China and the US, and push China to develop competitive chips of its own. He cultivated a relationship with Trump while seeking decisions favorable to Nvidia, including opening the Chinese market to the company.

## How Huang turned Nvidia into a market leader

Huang founded Nvidia in 1993 with his friends Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem. According to available accounts, they came up with the idea for the company at a Denny’s diner and wanted to create realistic 3D graphics for personal computers. In its early decades, Nvidia was best known in the gaming industry for chips capable of producing high-quality graphics in games.

In 2012, the AlexNet neural network won a well-known computer vision award, and Nvidia shifted its focus from high-speed computer graphics to chips for artificial intelligence. AlexNet was created by engineers Alex Krizhevsky, Ilya Sutskever, and Geoffrey Hinton. Huang has repeatedly compared its arrival to a Big Bang for computing.

Nvidia’s new graphics processors proved well suited to running AI models, which require substantial computing power. As the AI boom began in 2023, the company’s shares rose sharply in 2023 and 2024. In the summer of 2025, Nvidia became the first company in history to reach a market value of $4 trillion.

By September 2026, Nvidia controlled about 90% of the graphics processor market. The company’s growth was attributed to its advanced chips, its CUDA software ecosystem, which keeps developers within the platform, and Huang’s leadership style: fast-paced, controlling, charismatic, and often sharp-edged.

Dataist’s take: Huang’s departure affects more than Nvidia’s leadership. His personal style was part of how the company built its business and held its position in the graphics processor market. The board has said only that it will consider internal and external candidates; it has not named a permanent successor.
