# Google updates Project Genie with Genie 4 and a worlds gallery

## What has changed in Project Genie

Genie 4 is designed to generate interactive 3D worlds. Text prompts let users change the weather, characters and storylines on the fly, while audio is generated in real time and adapts to the scene.

Genie 3 has also been updated: a session can now last up to three minutes, and worlds support 360-degree panoramic views. They can also be linked together.

An AI agent builds worlds from voice descriptions. In the community gallery, users can share worlds they have created and adapt other participants’ work.

All generated content undergoes safety checks and receives SynthID watermarks. Google AI Pro subscribers can join the waitlist.

Dataist’s take: the update expands Project Genie from generating individual worlds to changing them, linking them and sharing them among users. Access to Genie 4 is open to Google AI Ultra subscribers, while Google AI Pro members can currently only join the waitlist.
