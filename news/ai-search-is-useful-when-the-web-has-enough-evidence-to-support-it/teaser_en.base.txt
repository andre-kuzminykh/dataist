AI search works best when the web has plenty of clear, reliable information to draw from. For familiar facts or a quick overview, it can save time; for a more complex request, it can compare sources and turn them into a coherent answer. But when the question is obscure, the information time-sensitive or the stakes high, a confident summary can hide a weak source—or no reliable source at all. Knowing when to use AI search is less about trusting the answer and more about knowing what to verify.
