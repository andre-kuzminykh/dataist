Mistral released Large 4’s open weights on October 29, after about three more weeks of reinforcement learning and feedback collection following its preview. Artificial Analysis scored the final model 26 on Intelligence Index v5, up from 25 for the preview, while estimated task costs fell 63% to $2.06. The release makes a case for lower operating costs and more control over deployment, but it does not put Large 4 at the top of the open-model rankings: several models score higher, and the results depend on the agent software and settings used.
