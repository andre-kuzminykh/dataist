Artemis Security has released Orion-1, a model built to investigate cyberattacks by connecting activity across authentication services, cloud infrastructure, company devices, SaaS apps and network equipment. The launch targets a growing divide in security work: general-purpose models can help attackers, while safeguards on leading models can also obstruct defenders. Orion-1 is available to selected Artemis customers in a closed preview, with human approval required by default for actions with serious consequences.
