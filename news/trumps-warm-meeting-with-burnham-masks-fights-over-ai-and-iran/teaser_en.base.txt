Trump’s first face-to-face meeting with Andy Burnham ended with warmer words than many British officials expected. He praised the British prime minister as a “born businessman,” said he had “many advantages,” and suggested he could be an excellent head of government. The cordial tone matters because the meeting was meant to repair ties strained by disagreements over Iran, while the two leaders remain far apart on AI regulation, the Chagos Islands and the war in Ukraine.
