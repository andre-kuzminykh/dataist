AI is already helping Wall Street banks, hedge funds and individual investors analyze markets, assess risks and make decisions. Financial institutions are adopting generative AI, machine learning and AI agents to process large volumes of data faster, automate workflows and expand research. These tools could give smaller firms easier access to analysis that was once available only to organizations with large teams and substantial computing resources. But similar models and signals can also make investors’ actions more aligned, leaving markets more vulnerable during periods of stress.
