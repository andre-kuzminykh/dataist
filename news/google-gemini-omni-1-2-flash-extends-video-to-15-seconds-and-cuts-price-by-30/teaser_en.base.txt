Google has opened Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash video generation to everyone. The model creates clips from 3 to 15 seconds long—5 seconds longer than its previous version—and lets users edit them through a dialogue without regenerating the entire clip. The price is down 30%, to $0.07 per second of 720p video. The model is available through the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Google Cloud Console, Flow, Google Vids, the Gemini app for Google AI Ultra subscribers, and Google Ads. Every finished clip contains an invisible SynthID watermark.
