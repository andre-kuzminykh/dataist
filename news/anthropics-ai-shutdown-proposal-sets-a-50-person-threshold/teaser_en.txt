Anthropic is privately discussing a bill that would let the federal government halt an AI model only when it poses an imminent threat: a risk of killing or seriously injuring at least 50 people, or causing more than $1 billion in property damage. The proposal, reported by Politico, would limit intervention to three scenarios and put the final decision in the hands of a federal judge. That narrow threshold matters: it offers a way to stop a model while leaving labs considerable room to operate.
