Moment Energy is turning used EV batteries into grid storage, giving packs that may still hold up to 80% of their original capacity another job before recycling. The Vancouver company has commissioned 11 systems since its 2019 founding and is building factories in Canada and Texas. The opportunity is growing as data centers need more power and outages expose the limits of existing supply. But Moment’s model faces a two-sided test: it must process batteries at scale while competing with new units that can already cost less.
