## OpenAI accuses Apple of stealing trade secrets and pressuring suppliers

## Mutual accusations

OpenAI’s lawsuit says a former employee shared information about product and supplier plans after returning to Apple. OpenAI alleges that Apple is pressuring shared suppliers and obstructing competition.

The new lawsuit is a response to a case Apple filed over the summer. At the time, the company accused several former employees of passing confidential information to OpenAI.

## From partnership to dispute

The companies also have a shared history: just two years ago, they announced a partnership under which ChatGPT was supposed to appear on the iPhone. OpenAI later completed its $6.4 billion purchase of io, a studio founded by former Apple chief designer Jony Ive. The company is now preparing its own devices to compete with Apple’s products.

Dataist’s take: The legal dispute is unfolding as the companies compete for suppliers, employees and the device market. The allegations in the lawsuit remain OpenAI’s position for now; Apple previously made its own claims against former employees.
