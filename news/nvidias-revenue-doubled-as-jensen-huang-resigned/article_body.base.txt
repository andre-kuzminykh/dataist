# Nvidia’s revenue doubled amid Jensen Huang’s resignation

## Financial results

Nvidia reported revenue of $115.84 billion and earnings per share of $2.68. Bloomberg’s analyst consensus had expected revenue of $109.15 billion and earnings per share of $2.47.

Kress said the company was maintaining its pace of development, sticking to its product release plan and setting higher goals.

Nvidia develops powerful computing chips for artificial intelligence. The company controls about 90% of the market for these chips, and OpenAI, Anthropic and Google use its technology. With Wall Street holding significant investments in Nvidia, changes at the company could have a notable effect on the US economy.

## Huang’s resignation and the market reaction

Huang founded Nvidia with partners in 1993. The company began as a small maker of video game chips and grew into a trillion-dollar corporation at the center of the AI boom. As CEO, Huang directly managed about 60 employees and oversaw much of the company’s day-to-day work.

Last week, after reports that Huang had a romantic relationship with an alleged Chinese agent, he admitted to having an affair. A few days later, reports emerged of two more alleged relationships with Nvidia employees. On Thursday, the board announced Huang’s resignation, effective immediately.

Amid these events, Nvidia shares fell more than 10%, and the company’s market value dropped by hundreds of billions of dollars to $3.7 trillion. In October, it had reached a record of nearly $6 trillion. Since then, the shares had been gradually losing value amid a broader slowdown in the AI market.

## Calls for Huang to return

Donald Trump and Silicon Valley figures, including Elon Musk, Uber co-founder Travis Kalanick and prominent venture capitalists, called for Huang to return as CEO. On Monday, before traveling to Asia, Trump told reporters he would not object to Huang’s return and added that he believed Huang had learned an important lesson.

On Tuesday morning, Huang spoke publicly for the first time since resigning. He apologized for his mistakes and thanked friends and colleagues for their support. He also said he was willing to return if the board decided that doing so was in the interests of shareholders, employees, the company and the United States. According to several publications, the board had not considered his return.

On Tuesday, Huang agreed to give transcribed interviews to the House Foreign Affairs Committee, the Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party and the House Intelligence Committee. They are scheduled for early December. The US Securities and Exchange Commission is also investigating Nvidia’s activities in China following Huang’s resignation, The Wall Street Journal reported on Tuesday.

Dataist’s take: Nvidia’s financial results remain strong, but its CEO’s resignation and declining market value come amid a slowdown in the AI market. According to published reports, the board is not considering Huang’s return, while the US regulator’s investigation into the company’s business in China continues.
