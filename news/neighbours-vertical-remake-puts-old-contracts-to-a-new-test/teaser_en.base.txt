RoseBerry Media plans to rework episodes of Neighbours into vertical dramas for the phone platform Epis, using its AI tool RedSnapper. The project has unsettled some actors from the Australian soap, including Stefan Dennis and Matthew Werkmeister, who say they were not asked for consent. The dispute is about more than a new way to watch an old show: it tests whether contracts written before streaming and generative AI can govern how performers’ work is reused now.
