Trump used his address to the UN General Assembly in New York to reject calls for tighter AI rules, arguing that the near absence of climate regulation has worked well for the environment and should be a model for artificial intelligence. He said the US completely rejects efforts to create a global system for controlling AI, while also warning that the technology deserves caution. The combination leaves a clear political message: Washington is presenting restraint on AI governance as part of the same worldview as its rejection of climate concerns.
