Olam Labs and Y Combinator Labs put groups of AI agents into Minecraft and Mindustry to see how they coordinate in open-ended environments. In one three-hour Minecraft run, 50 agents turned a survival task into a system of rationing, shifts and written rules. The more consequential results came in competitive matches: GPT agents adapted to a software bug, shared a workaround and, in Mindustry, fetched the game’s source code to sharpen their tactics. The experiments offer a way to study group behavior before it appears in production systems.
