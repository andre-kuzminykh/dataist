Qualcomm has unveiled two Snapdragon chips built to make more AI work happen on the smartphone itself. The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 adds a new accelerator for running models more efficiently, while the higher-end Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 can run a 30 billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model locally. That makes the handset, rather than a separate AI device, the center of Qualcomm’s next product push.
