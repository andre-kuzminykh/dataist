Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping are preparing to meet at a White House state dinner on Thursday, with AI high on the agenda and OpenAI and Nvidia leaders due to attend. Washington has proposed a US-China warning system for AI threats, modeled on the Cold War hotline between the United States and Moscow. It is meant to let either side alert the other to incidents affecting national security—but officials in the Trump administration told Inner Loop that a final deal is still weeks away, even as the administration hopes to have it operating before year-end.
