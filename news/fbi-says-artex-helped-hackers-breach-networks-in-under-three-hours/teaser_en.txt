The FBI warns of cyberattacks using autonomous AI tools, including Artex, an open-source program for testing network security. The agency says Artex was used against ConnectWise and Barracuda, as well as financial organizations in South Korea. In one case, attackers went from searching for vulnerabilities to breaching a network in less than three hours. Among those affected were ConnectWise customers: hackers gained access to the Microsoft 365 cloud environments of at least 2,250 organizations.
