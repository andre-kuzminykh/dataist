Octen, founded by a former Alibaba engineer, is expanding search for AI agents: its Pulse model finds web pages in a median of 10 milliseconds on the server. The company also offers a $20-a-month subscription with unlimited web search and is adding image search. But Pulse’s speed excludes network transfer and answer generation, and the downloadable version of the model is limited to non-commercial use. Companies will therefore need to assess the quality of results, access terms and operating costs for their own workloads.
