Agility Robotics is set to become the first public company focused entirely on humanoid robots. Through a reverse merger with a SPAC, it will raise $465 million from institutional and retail investors. The company already uses Digit robots at nine customers’ facilities, but earned only $174,000 from them in the first six months of 2026 and posted a loss of $362 million. Now it must scale production of its new Digit 5 model and find customers for it.
