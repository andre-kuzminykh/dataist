Uber gives engineers four Claude Code settings: compact context after 400,000 tokens, use medium reasoning, cache the prompt for an hour for the main agent and five minutes for subagents, and avoid loading MCP tool descriptions at startup. The company introduced these limits as AI costs rose: Uber CTO Praveen Neppalli Naga said the company had already exhausted its 2026 AI budget by the start of the year. Yet from February to August, the number of agent requests across Uber services grew 9.4 times, while overall AI spending, according to Uber engineer Uday Kiran Medisetti, had stayed roughly flat since April.
