Apple has won a fast-track look at OpenAI hiring materials as the companies’ trade-secrets case moves into discovery. A federal judge ordered OpenAI to turn over interview records involving former Apple employees who sought roles in its consumer hardware and robotics groups, along with messages and depositions tied to people named in the lawsuit. The order arrives ahead of the expected launch of OpenAI’s first hardware device, making the hiring process part of a dispute over what knowledge crossed between the companies.
