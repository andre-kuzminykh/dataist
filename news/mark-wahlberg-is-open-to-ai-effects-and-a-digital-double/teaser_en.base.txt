Mark Wahlberg is open to using AI in films, but not to handing the work over to it. At TechCrunch Disrupt, the actor said AI could help with parts of his expensive biopic about baseball player Pete Rose, such as creating a stadium. He rejected the idea of asking ChatGPT to write a script and making a film without live actors. He is also willing to consider a digital replica of himself—one his children or grandchildren might someday use in films.
