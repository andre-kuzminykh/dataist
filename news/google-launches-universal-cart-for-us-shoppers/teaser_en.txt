Google has launched Universal Cart in the United States, bringing saved products from different stores into one place. Shoppers can revisit items without searching for them again, track price drops and restocks, and get help choosing payment options and finding promo codes. The cart also connects checkout to Google’s Universal Commerce Protocol, its open standard for interactions between AI agents and merchants. It extends Google’s push into AI-assisted shopping, where the harder test is whether convenience can carry a shopper all the way to checkout.
