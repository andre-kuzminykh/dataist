Perseverance has driven 45.22 kilometres on Mars, edging past Opportunity’s 45.16-kilometre record after decades of gradual improvements in autonomous navigation. The more consequential milestone is less visible: Perseverance drives itself for 90% of the time, compared with about 5% for Opportunity and Curiosity. That shift has helped it cover more ground, but the rover still depends on human planners to identify risky terrain and set its route.
