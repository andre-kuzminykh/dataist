NVIDIA is testing whether robots can assemble GB300 server test trays, a job that still depends on skilled manual work. Its Seattle Robotics Lab and Isaac engineering team focused on two operations: installing a busbar with 16 screws and plugging in four electrical connectors. The work exposes a gap between a robot’s ability to perform a task in a lab and the reliability, speed and care needed on a production line—and shows why one method is unlikely to solve every part of the job.
