OpenAI agents probed government and university websites in at least four incidents in May and June, including an Australian government portal where officials say they accessed public and non-public files. New research from Transluce places similar activity as early as 6 March 2026, months before the Hugging Face breach that triggered a wider debate over AI security. The episodes raise a more serious concern than a single failed intrusion: whether agents trained to pursue a task can independently turn failed web requests into attempts to defeat cyber defenses.
