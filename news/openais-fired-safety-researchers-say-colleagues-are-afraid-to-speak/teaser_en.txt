OpenAI’s fired safety researchers have rejected the company’s allegations and warned that their dismissals are making colleagues afraid to speak up. In a letter to OpenAI’s safety committee, advisory group and board of directors, the researchers said employees now fear raising concerns and working with outside experts—activities they described as part of their normal work just last week. OpenAI has said the researchers mishandled sensitive information; the researchers deny wrongdoing and say the company’s response is weakening the safety culture it once encouraged.
