OpenAI is preparing to publish hundreds of mathematical results from a new internal model, including a solution to a Millennium Prize problem and more than 100 other long-standing open problems. The release follows months of friction with mathematicians over how AI labs present their work, credit researchers and make results available for scrutiny. For some in the field, the issue is no longer whether AI can help solve hard problems, but whether OpenAI’s way of announcing those solutions can earn the community’s trust.
