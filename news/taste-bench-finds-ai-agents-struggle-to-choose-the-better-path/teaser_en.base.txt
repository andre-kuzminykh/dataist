AI agents can work through long coding and research tasks, but a passing result does not show whether they chose well along the way. Taste-Bench tests that missing skill: given an agent’s work up to a decision point, can a model pick the path that ultimately proves better? Across 14 models, the top score was 59.7%, barely above the benchmark’s 25% chance level. The gap matters as agents spend longer working on their own: a bad early choice can look reasonable until much of the task budget is gone.
