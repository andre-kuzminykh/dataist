Google’s internal tests suggest that AI is not yet speeding up AI research. Across 1,389 runs of its Argon agent, success without human help fell sharply as tasks became more complex. Argon solves about half of DeepMind’s RE-Bench tasks—well ahead of Gemini 3.7 Flash, but far from the reliability that full automation would require. The results point to a gap between automating bounded work and taking over the open-ended research that could improve AI itself.
