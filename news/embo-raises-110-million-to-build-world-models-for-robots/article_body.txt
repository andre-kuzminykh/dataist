Embo, a robotics startup, has raised $110 million in seed funding to develop models for household robots. Andreessen Horowitz led the round, which also included Khosla Ventures, DST Global and Nvidia. Embo trains its model on large collections of online videos so it can learn how objects work and how physical interactions unfold. It then practices movements in simulations, before learning from the experience of a fleet of humanoid robots.
