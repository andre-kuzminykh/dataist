# Nvidia clarifies terms of financial guarantees for data centers

Huang supported the AI industry not only by promoting the technology but also by financing companies that build its infrastructure. A petition calling for his return as Nvidia CEO had gathered 197,000 signatures over the weekend, including Elon Musk’s.

Nvidia has substantial funds available to invest: on Tuesday, the company reported net income of $64.9 billion for the third quarter, up from $59.7 billion in the previous quarter. But Nvidia’s continued growth depends on companies building and expanding data centers and buying its AI chips and systems.

Under Huang, Nvidia invested heavily in AI startups and cloud providers that rent out computing capacity. It later began guaranteeing large loans for data center construction. Its largest such agreement is a $105 billion guarantee for OpenAI’s PORTS-Pike campus in Ohio, announced in August. OpenAI committed to using only Nvidia equipment at the campus.

## When the guarantee takes effect

Huang’s sudden departure last week raised questions about whether Nvidia would continue financing customers at the same scale. Reports said that Colette Kress, now acting CEO, had tried to rein in Huang’s increasingly large deals. According to those reports, the guarantee for the OpenAI project was reduced from $250 billion to $105 billion.

In comments accompanying its quarterly results, Nvidia’s finance chief explained that a loan guarantee does not mean the company itself provides money for construction. First, the customer must secure the necessary financing through equity or debt, build the facility, prepare it for operation, and buy and install Nvidia equipment. The guarantee takes effect only after these conditions are met. If a project does not clear these stages, Nvidia’s commitment ends.

Kress cited OpenAI’s PORTS-Pike campus as an example of a project covered by these terms. She also said this was not a change in Nvidia policy, but a clarification of rules that were already in place. The company had not previously disclosed them publicly.

## Which companies Nvidia will support

Kress signaled that Nvidia is not stepping away from financing AI infrastructure, particularly new data centers, but will be more selective. She said that land, access to electricity, and completed buildings are key parts of these projects, and that helping secure them could become one of the company’s main priorities. Nvidia will also scrutinize more closely which companies it helps finance.

Huang faced criticism over circular deals: Nvidia invested in customers that then bought its products. Kress will have to strike a balance. The company’s investments helped fuel the AI boom, which generated substantial revenue for Nvidia, but its previous approach to dealmaking may be coming to an end.

Dataist’s take: Nvidia remains interested in expanding the data center market, but is now setting out the terms of its support more clearly. Guarantees do not themselves give customers money for construction: to activate them, customers must first secure financing and meet the project requirements.
