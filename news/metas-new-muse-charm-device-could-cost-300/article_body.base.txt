Meta plans to sell the Muse Charm for $300, The Information reports. The device, which the company showed at its Connect event, is designed as a portable version of its Muse AI assistant: people will be able to talk to it, ask questions and give it commands. That would put its price above Amazon’s Bee Frontier wearable AI device, but close to the price of a device from startup Friend.

## Meta may price its pocket-sized Muse Charm AI device at $300

[FIGURE:0]

A Meta spokesperson did not deny the reported price. In response to a query from TechCrunch, the spokesperson said the company was glad to see interest in its AI devices and would share more about them soon.

Meta has already released Muse, an AI assistant, along with a set of avatars that let users give the system a personality. The Muse Charm would be an attempt to put the software assistant into a dedicated physical device. It could be carried in a pocket or possibly worn around the neck.

The device is not yet on sale, and it is unclear whether buyers will be interested. Wearable AI devices have yet to find notable success; the Humane AI Pin is one example. Companies continue to refine the idea in hopes of attracting a broader audience.

## How the price compares with competitors

At $300, the Muse Charm would cost more than some similar devices. Friend’s wearable AI product, which lets users text and talk with an AI persona, currently sells for $250.

Amazon’s Bee Frontier records conversations and helps users track tasks. Its regular price is $75, and it is being offered for $52.49 during a Black Friday sale, a 30% discount. A $99-a-year subscription lets users connect Bee Frontier to their apps, including photos and email. Even with the subscription, Amazon’s device costs considerably less than the Muse Charm.

Dataist's take: At $300, the Muse Charm sits alongside higher-priced wearable AI devices, while Bee Frontier offers some features for less, with a separate subscription. Meta has not yet disclosed details about the Muse Charm’s capabilities or release timing, so the devices cannot be compared on functionality.
