Leading the Future, a super PAC network backed by major AI investors, has sent millions of dollars to four little-known Nevada companies, according to federal campaign-finance filings reviewed by WIRED. The payments raise a basic question about political spending: who is doing the work, and for whom? A complaint to the Federal Election Commission alleges that two groups in the network used shell companies to conceal the actual recipients. The commission currently lacks the quorum needed to act on the complaint.
