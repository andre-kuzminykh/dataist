OpenAI is releasing GPT-6.1 Sol as a lower-cost alternative to its delayed flagship, GPT-6.1 Astra. The company says Sol comes close to Astra on AI-assisted coding, computer use and office work, while costing a fifth as much. That makes the launch more than a cheaper model release: with Astra held back over safety concerns, OpenAI is putting a less capable but more deployable option in front of customers.
