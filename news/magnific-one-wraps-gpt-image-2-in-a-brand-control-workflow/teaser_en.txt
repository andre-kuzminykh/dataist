Magnific has launched Magnific One, an image-generation product built around a practical promise: make images feel less like AI output while keeping them within a company’s visual style. The product uses OpenAI’s GPT Image 2 with Magnific’s creative-guidance system, which interprets prompts, applies references and brand rules, and checks generated images for problems. The announcement’s strongest case is not that Magnific has a better underlying model, but that it can make image generation more predictable for professional teams.
