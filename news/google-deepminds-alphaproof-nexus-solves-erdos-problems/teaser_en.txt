Google DeepMind says AlphaProof Nexus has solved nine of 353 Erdős problems without using its specialist proof tool, AlphaProof. The system also proved 44 conjectures from the OEIS integer-sequence database, solved an open problem in algebraic geometry and improved a known bound in minimax optimization. The work has been submitted for scientific peer review, giving the results a different kind of scrutiny from a collection of AI-generated proofs published outside journals.
