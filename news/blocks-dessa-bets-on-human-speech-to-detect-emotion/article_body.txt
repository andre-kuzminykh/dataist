## What voice systems are trying to do

Dessa is not the first attempt to bring emotional expression to AI speech:

- Hume AI launched EVI, an “emotional intelligence” voice interface, in March 2024.
- Kyutai introduced Moshi in September 2024.
- LAION’s Open Empathic project has worked since late 2023 on training empathetic speech-synthesis models using an open audio dataset.
- OpenAI updated ChatGPT’s Advanced Voice Mode in June 2025 and has continued improving its intonation, pauses, and expression of empathy and sarcasm.

A more natural voice, however, does not show that a system feels emotion or has intelligence. The Russian article argues that capabilities such as reasoning while responding matter more to answer quality than how natural the voice sounds.

## The gap between expression and understanding

I think the useful question is not whether Dessa can detect emotion in speech, but what the model does with that signal. The announcement offers no detail on how the model is evaluated or whether its emotional recognition improves its responses. Without that, “recognises emotions” describes an intended capability, not evidence of better communication.

Training only on human speech gives Dessa a distinct premise, but it does not settle the harder question: whether a voice model can respond appropriately to emotion without merely performing empathy. The field’s progress may be judged less by how convincing its voices sound than by whether they reason better when a conversation gets complicated.
