OpenAI is using discounts to pull customers away from Anthropic, with some cuts reaching 90%. Replit switched after receiving an offer of more than 30% off a contract worth over $100 million a year. DataCamp’s CEO says OpenAI’s pricing cut the company’s costs by 80–90%. For customers, the pitch is clear; what is less clear is how much of the price difference comes from cheaper infrastructure and models, and how much OpenAI is willing to give up to win the account.
