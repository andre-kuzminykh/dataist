Researchers at Microsoft have found a way to implant a backdoor in a large language model without retraining it. The Lazarus method rewires fewer than 10 neurons that are usually inactive: when a hidden trigger appears, an agent can carry out an attacker’s command. The model otherwise behaves normally and passes standard safety checks. The attack is especially dangerous for agents with access to tools or a terminal: they could download and run malicious code, steal secrets or execute destructive commands.
