AstroForge plans to put an AI control stack in charge of its next spacecraft, rather than relying on continuous commands from Earth. The asteroid-mining startup says Solo will eventually run the spacecraft with limited onboard autonomy, and its Autonomy-1 mission is planned for 2027 on Stoke Space’s first rocket. The move matters because spacecraft autonomy still mostly depends on traditional control algorithms, while AstroForge’s own two prototypes have already suffered failures that ground teams could not fully fix.
