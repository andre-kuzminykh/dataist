Samsung is expanding its chip campus in Pyeongtaek, South Korea, betting that demand for memory will keep rising through 2028. The construction site is already part of a vast industrial complex, where finished factories stand across the road from new steel frames and crane crews. The push comes as SK Hynix has pulled ahead in high-bandwidth memory used in Nvidia’s AI accelerators, while TSMC remains the leader in contract chip manufacturing. Samsung is trying to regain ground in memory, and the scale of the work makes that ambition visible.
