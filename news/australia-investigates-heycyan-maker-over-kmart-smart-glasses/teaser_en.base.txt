Australia’s privacy commissioner has opened an investigation into Shenzhen Qingcheng, the Chinese company behind the HeyCyan app used with Kmart’s Anko smart glasses. The inquiry follows questions about the app’s technology and privacy policy, and the company’s failure to answer the commissioner’s requests. The case is narrower than the public debate around the glasses: the commissioner says she will not investigate the other companies contacted, while Australia’s privacy law may not cover people who use the devices to record others.
