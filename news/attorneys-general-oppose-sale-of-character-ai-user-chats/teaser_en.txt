A coalition of attorneys general from 42 states and the District of Columbia is seeking to stop Character.ai from auctioning off users’ personal data as part of its bankruptcy case. The service’s archives contain conversations from more than 21 million US accounts, including over 2.1 million accounts belonging to minors, as well as IP addresses. Smirks and Pangs Acquisition LLC submitted the opening bid for the auction, scheduled for December 1. The attorneys general warn that the buyer could use the data without restriction, despite Character.ai’s previous promise not to sell users’ personal information.
