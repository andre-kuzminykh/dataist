A team of AI models can solve a problem none of its members can solve alone—but only if the models learn how to challenge and correct one another. A new framework called SAT trains fixed groups of models to develop reusable collaboration strategies, then applies those strategies to unfamiliar tasks. Its strongest results came on math and physics benchmarks, where a diverse team outperformed single-model and multi-agent baselines. On knowledge benchmarks, however, the team often generated a correct line of reasoning but struggled to identify it.
