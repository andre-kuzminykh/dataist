Meta hired lettering artist Jessica Hische to refine the M and wordmark for Muse, its new AI agent and personal assistant. Hische finished the letterform in two days, producing 32 options, then spent two weeks on the wordmark. She expected criticism for working with Meta, but the response on Threads grew into personal attacks, including posts about her home and finances. The backlash exposed a fault line for designers: even work done without generative AI can be treated as complicity with the companies behind it.
