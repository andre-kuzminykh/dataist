OpenAI has started combining Chat and Work modes in ChatGPT. The update is currently going to a small number of Pro subscribers. Under the new system, both modes will use a shared limit, while conversations with the Instant model will remain unlimited and will not count toward it. An OpenAI representative told TechCrunch that most users should not run out of their limits more often, but the most active may reach them sooner. A wider rollout is planned for December.
