Choosing an AI for healthcare is not as simple as picking the model with the highest score.

The authors introduce InclusionMed, an open effort to compare medical AI across different healthcare roles and kinds of work. Their results show that models can rank very differently depending on whether they are helping patients communicate or supporting clinical decisions, and that similar scores can come with very different costs. The project also reveals how much of healthcare remains missing from current evaluations.

In this review we look at how InclusionMed assesses medical AI, what its first comparisons reveal, and why the right model depends on who will use it and for what.
