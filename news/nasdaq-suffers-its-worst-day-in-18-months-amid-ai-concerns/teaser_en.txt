Nasdaq fell 5.5% on Thursday, its steepest drop in 18 months. Investors fear that Democrats’ election wins in the US will bring tighter controls on data-center construction and slow AI development. By the close, the index had lost more than $2 trillion in market value and entered correction territory, more than 10% below its recent record. The sell-off spread to other markets: chipmakers’ shares plunged, and the VIX volatility index rose 61%.
