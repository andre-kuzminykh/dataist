# Satya Nadella: AI agents will change the economics of the internet

[FIGURE:0]

## From visits to transactions

Nadella spoke at the summit on Wednesday. He said a shift to an internet where AI agents handle some tasks could change who pays for what: companies, for example, may distribute money differently for attracting traffic and completing transactions.

Business models built around visits from people will have to adapt. Companies that earn money from ad impressions may switch to charging for clicks or tie revenue more closely to purchases. If one platform does not let agents use its services, users may turn to another that does.

## The Amazon and Shopify example

The meeting covered Amazon’s decision to block AI agents from its online store. As a result, agents cannot browse its catalogue or buy products for users.

Nadella suggested that companies defending their familiar business models risk losing to more open competitors. If Amazon continues to block agents, another marketplace, such as Shopify, could let them make purchases and gain an advantage.

Dataist's take: The changes will affect more than advertising. The Amazon example shows that access to catalogues and purchases by AI agents could become part of competition between platforms. But Nadella’s remarks do not indicate when companies will start changing their business models, or how quickly that change will happen.
