The US has restricted Chinese companies’ remote access to advanced Nvidia chips in overseas data centers, while OpenAI and Anthropic have made progress in curbing distillation of their models. According to The Information, since September this has complicated work at Chinese developers including Deepseek, Moonshot AI and Alibaba: current projects are delayed, and the gap with American models could widen. Companies are looking for workarounds: renting computing capacity through intermediaries, switching to Chinese chips and investing in data for reinforcement learning.

## US restrictions are slowing the development of Chinese AI labs

## Projects delayed

According to the publication, the pressure has already affected the timelines of many ongoing projects. Last month, Deepseek researchers estimated internally that without distillation, the gap with leading American models could grow from three to six months to nine.

Moonshot AI has already canceled a $150 million deal to buy Nvidia Blackwell chips in Indonesia.

## Seeking alternatives

Chinese companies are trying different ways to maintain access to computing resources and continue training models:

- Rent computing capacity through intermediaries in other countries.
- MiniMax, Mihoyo and Alibaba are using Chinese chips more actively.
- Increase investment in data for their own reinforcement learning.

<strong>Dataist's take:</strong> The restrictions affect several stages of development at once: access to chips, project timelines and model training methods. Chinese companies are seeking alternatives, but according to Deepseek researchers’ assessment, abandoning distillation altogether could significantly widen the gap with leading American models.
