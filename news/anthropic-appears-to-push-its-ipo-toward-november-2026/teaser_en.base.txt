Anthropic appears to be pushing its IPO from October toward November 2026, reportedly to show investors a stronger third-quarter report. The company’s second-quarter numbers already make a credible listing case: revenue rose from about $4.7 billion to more than $11.5 billion, while adjusted operating margin turned positive. But the timing also exposes the pressure behind the launch. Anthropic is spending heavily on infrastructure, relies on non-standard profit measures, and may need public-market access before its cash reserves run low.
