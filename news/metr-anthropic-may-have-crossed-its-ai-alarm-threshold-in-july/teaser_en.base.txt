According to the nonprofit METR, since October 2025, Anthropic’s model performance has been improving about 1.9 times faster than it did between March 2024 and October 2025. Auditors attribute around 80% of the acceleration to the use of AI in the company’s own research. METR believes Anthropic may have crossed the alarm threshold in its policy as early as July 15, 2026, but the company removed a significant portion of the key data from public materials.
