Bharat Semi, an Indian startup, has raised $24 million in seed funding to develop programmable image sensors with built-in processing and AI capabilities. The nearly five-year-old startup has yet to generate revenue, but says it has agreed development projects with US defence contractor General Atomics and India’s Kaynes Semicon. The sensors are still in development: Bharat Semi expects to begin its first commercial shipments in 2027. The company plans to sell its products in the US and other markets.
