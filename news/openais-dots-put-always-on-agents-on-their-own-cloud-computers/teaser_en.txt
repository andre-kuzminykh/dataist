OpenAI introduced Dots, always-on AI agents that can monitor work and carry tasks through to completion, at its DevDay 2026 developer conference. Each Dot runs on its own cloud computer and can work across connected apps, chat and voice, with the aim of acting before a user asks. The launch comes three weeks after Meta introduced its similar Muse agent. OpenAI is also releasing the less expensive GPT-6.1 Sol, while delaying GPT-6.1 Astra over safety concerns.
