## Nvidia keeps its open-model strategy after Jensen Huang’s departure

## Hugging Face deal plans

In September, Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face, a platform for open models. The $12.9 billion deal would be the second-largest in Nvidia’s history. More than 118 million developers and 200,000 companies now use the platform.

Kress said the plan to combine Nvidia’s and Hugging Face’s platforms had not changed despite Huang’s departure. Huang had strongly advocated for open technologies. The acquisition was not raised directly during the question-and-answer portion of the call.

Kress said the companies intend to expand the Hugging Face platform and make AI more open, capable and accessible. Hugging Face’s CEO called Huang’s departure a sad event and pledged to advocate more actively for open AI.

## Nvidia’s open models

Huang promoted Nvidia’s Nemotron family of open models, designed for AI agents that operate over extended periods and for tasks requiring reasoning. Kress said Nemotron had become the most widely used open model from the US on OpenRouter. Developers use it as a foundation for building their own intelligent systems, she said.

She also noted that open models help accelerate AI adoption. Weekly token volume on OpenRouter was up 35-fold year over year and 3.5 times over the past three months.

## Huang’s departure and investigations

Last week, Nvidia founder Jensen Huang stepped down as CEO after reports of extramarital relationships. These reportedly included an affair with Alice Huang, an executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed, which is described as an Nvidia customer and a suspected Chinese spy. Huang has also been accused of relationships with two direct reports. He is due to testify before Congress by Thanksgiving.

Meanwhile, the US Securities and Exchange Commission is investigating whether Nvidia’s disclosures may have misled investors, The Wall Street Journal reported.

Dataist’s take: Kress confirmed that Nvidia’s strategy remains unchanged, but the call did not clarify the status of the Hugging Face deal: it was not discussed separately. The company links its plans for open models and the platform to the development of Nemotron and broader developer access to tools for building their own systems.
