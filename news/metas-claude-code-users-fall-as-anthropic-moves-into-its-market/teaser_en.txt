Meta has cut the number of Claude Code users at the company from about 60,000 to 30,000, as it promotes its own coding tools and Anthropic expands into products that compete with its partners. The shift is notable not just for the scale of Meta’s spending on Claude Code—more than $105 million in a single 28-day period—but for the changing relationship between the companies: Anthropic is no longer just a supplier Meta needs to accommodate.
