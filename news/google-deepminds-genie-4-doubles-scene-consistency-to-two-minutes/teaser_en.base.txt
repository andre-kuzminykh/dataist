Google DeepMind has released Genie 4, a world model that adds audio, more realistic scenes and real-time control over what happens inside them. The update arrives as rivals build interactive worlds of their own and companies look to simulation for applications from games to robotics. Genie 4 can keep a scene consistent for up to two minutes—twice as long as Genie 3—but Google says the system is still early, with no settled product plan and unresolved problems with audio accuracy and readable text.
