Wabi, founded by Replika creator Eugenia Kuyda, raised $55 million at a $1.25 billion valuation as it shifts from letting users build apps with prompts to becoming an AI messenger. The company says its personal agent will create interfaces for everyday tasks. The financing puts a striking valuation on a pivot whose product is still taking shape: Wabi’s examples range from meal planning to local restaurant tracking, but the announcement says little about how people will use those tools in practice.
