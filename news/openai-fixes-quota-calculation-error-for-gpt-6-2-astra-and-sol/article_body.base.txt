# OpenAI fixes quota calculation error for GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol

## What changes for subscribers

The issue affected only GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol; other models were not affected. After the fix, Astra users will use up their quota 40% faster, and Sol users 25% faster than last week. Quotas for both models should still be higher than for GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol.

To offset the change, OpenAI will give Go, Plus, Pro and Business subscribers an additional limit reset. Business subscribers will also get free access to Fast Mode through the end of the year. The company will not retroactively charge users for the difference.

## OpenAI combines Chat and Work modes

OpenAI is testing a merger of Chat and Work modes with some Pro users. Work is more geared toward independent AI-agent operation: it is slower than Chat, but has more capabilities and likely costs OpenAI more because it uses more tokens.

Anthropic has already taken the same approach with Cowork. All conversations now take place in a single cloud environment: the model decides whether to reply immediately or start a full AI-agent workflow.

Dataist's take: the fix makes quota use for Astra and Sol less favorable than it was last week, but the additional resets and compensation for Business subscribers soften the transition. Combining Chat and Work shifts the choice between a standard reply and an agent workflow to the model itself; OpenAI is only testing this approach with some Pro subscribers so far.
