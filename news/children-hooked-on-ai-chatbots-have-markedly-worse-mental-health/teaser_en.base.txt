A new survey by the JED Foundation found that 90% of children aged 9 to 17 had used AI chatbots. 58% turned to them for help with schoolwork, and 57% when they felt sad, stressed, lonely or confused. Participants who used AI while emotionally distressed were more likely to report mental health problems and suicidal thoughts or attempts. The researchers stress, however, that the data do not prove chatbots caused those problems.
