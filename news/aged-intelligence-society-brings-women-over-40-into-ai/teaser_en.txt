Aged Intelligence Society, founded by former WIRED editor Shoshana Berger and Nina Willdorf, brings women over 40 together to try AI tools on the problems filling their lives: a 300-page insurance packet, an underpriced consulting proposal, the administrative load of caring for children and aging parents. The club’s appeal is growing beyond its San Francisco Bay Area dinners. Its founders are betting that a social setting can make AI feel less like a distant industry debate and more like a practical skill.
