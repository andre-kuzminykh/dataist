Four current and former students at a school in Baltimore County have filed a lawsuit after police detained them outside the school a year ago when Omnilert’s weapon-detection system mistook a bag of Doritos for a gun. The teenagers were made to kneel, handcuffed and searched. They accuse Omnilert, the county police department, the school board, several officers and the school principal of unlawful detention and search caused by a system error and the actions of adults.
