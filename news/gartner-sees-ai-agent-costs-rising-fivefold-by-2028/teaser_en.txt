Gartner expects AI-agent costs to rise fivefold by 2028, even as token prices fall. The forecast, attributed to Gartner senior director analyst Will Sommer, points to a mismatch: older AI capabilities are getting cheaper, while frontier models and the workloads built around them are becoming more expensive. For companies adopting agents, the question is not simply what a token costs, but how many tokens a task consumes and whether the result justifies the bill.
