Anthropic says Zhipu AI’s open-weight GLM-5.3 can build working cyber exploits at a level close to Claude Mythos Preview, but with a crucial difference: anyone can download it. The comparison comes five months after Anthropic introduced Mythos Preview, which it has kept to selected cyber-defense specialists through Project Glasswing. Anthropic says those specialists have found more than 10,000 vulnerabilities in critical software. The gap between restricted access and public availability is now the story.
