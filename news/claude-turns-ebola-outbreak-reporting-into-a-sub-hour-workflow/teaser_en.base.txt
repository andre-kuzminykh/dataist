Claude is now being used to speed outbreak response in eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo, where health workers visit homes every day to trace symptoms, recoveries, deaths and referrals. The work is part of a partnership organized by CEPI with WHO AFRO, INRB and other organizations, alongside Anthropic teams working on applied AI. The immediate payoff is operational: reports that once took a full day can now be prepared in less than an hour, while the same tools are being tested for disease modeling, vaccine research and viral-genome analysis.
