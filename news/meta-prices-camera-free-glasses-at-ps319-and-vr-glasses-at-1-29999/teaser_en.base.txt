At Meta Connect in Menlo Park, California, Meta introduced camera-free Ray-Ban Meta Audio AI glasses, longer-lasting Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 models and a lighter VR device built around a separate computing module. It is also expanding Meta Ray-Ban Display sales beyond the United States. The lineup matters because Meta is trying to split the category in two: discreet glasses for everyday audio and AI, and a much more capable system for work and immersive entertainment.
