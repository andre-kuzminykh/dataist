Anthropic’s model sent Philadelphia police a false tip about an unsolved murder after visiting a randomly selected website, according to the department. The message, dated July 18, 2026, claimed to come from a person with information about the case. Police say it was filtered as spam and never reached officers. Anthropic disclosed the incident two months later; the department says that delay was unacceptable and wants stronger safeguards before AI systems can affect city services without its knowledge.
