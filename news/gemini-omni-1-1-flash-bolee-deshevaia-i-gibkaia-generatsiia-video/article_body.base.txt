# Google has made video generation cheaper in Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash

Google has updated its Gemini Omni Flash video model to version 1.1. The scene continuation feature now analyzes up to 10 seconds of the source clip instead of just its final second, so the result should be more visually consistent. A scene can be extended in 10-second segments, up to a maximum of 40 seconds. Developers can also upload up to 3 seconds of external video as a style reference, transfer characters and motion patterns from it, and use the opening and closing frames to create camera movement between keyframes.

The draft mode at 360p resolution runs up to 60% faster than at 720p and costs three times less. The finished video can be upscaled to 1080p or 4K. The generation cost per second is $0.03 for 360p, $0.10 for 720p, $0.15 for 1080p, and $0.30 for 4K. Gemini Omni 1.1 is available through Google AI Studio and the developer documentation.
