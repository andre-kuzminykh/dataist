OpenAI has released GPT-6.1 Sol, a cheaper model that the company says comes close to GPT-6 Astra on several measures. It is aimed at difficult work such as coding, debugging, document understanding and multi-step workflows. The launch also narrows the gap between Sol and Astra on factual errors, while leaving the more consequential question unresolved: how much confidence should users place in those safety claims when OpenAI’s reported tests are the evidence?
