Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge has published a proof of the Riemann hypothesis created with Claude, one of mathematics’ most important unsolved problems. His Lean code runs to 17 million lines. The nonprofit Lean Focused Research Organization and Mathlib said they trust the proof’s correctness: the problem is stated correctly, and the code compiles. But to understand the reasoning and use the result in further work, mathematicians will have to grapple with ideas that are new to them.
