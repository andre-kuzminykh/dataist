Autonomous delivery robots from Starship Technologies were removed from the streets of Sheffield just five months after the launch of a pilot project. The six-wheeled machines appeared in certain areas of the city in March as part of a partnership with Uber Eats. During the trials, the robots repeatedly had their signal flags broken off, were covered in graffiti and prompted complaints that they were obstructing pedestrians. Starship said it had “suspended the service” after the trials naturally concluded, and that vandalism did not affect the decision.
