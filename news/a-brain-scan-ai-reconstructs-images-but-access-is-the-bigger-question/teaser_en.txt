A team led by Michal Irani at the Weizmann Institute of Science has built an AI system that reconstructs images from brain scans, aiming to reproduce what a person saw more accurately than earlier methods. The work, recently presented at two conferences, combines a model that predicts an image’s structure with one that predicts its content. For now, it depends on high-resolution fMRI scans from large, expensive machines—but the prospect of extracting information from brain activity has already raised concerns about where the technology could lead.
