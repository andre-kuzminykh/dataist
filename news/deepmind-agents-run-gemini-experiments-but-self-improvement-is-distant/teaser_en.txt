Google DeepMind has tasked AI agents with independently managing some computing resources and running experiments for the next generation of Gemini. Koray Kavukcuoglu, the company’s vice president of research and head of Gemini development, discussed this on a company podcast. He said the lab has moved from separate teams for projects such as AlphaFold and fusion research to a single general-purpose algorithm used in both science and products. Recursive self-improvement remains a long way off.
