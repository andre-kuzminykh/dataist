Anthropic is discussing a draft law that would let a court halt the release of an AI model if it poses a predictable, immediate risk of killing more than 50 people or causing more than $1 billion in property damage. The proposal, reported by Politico, would put outside experts in the assessment process and require the Commerce Department to seek a judge’s order. It matters because Anthropic is asking lawmakers to turn catastrophic-risk warnings into a route to stopping a product before release.
