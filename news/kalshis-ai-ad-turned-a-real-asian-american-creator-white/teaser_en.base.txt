Kalshi used a video by tech blogger Elliot Choi in a YouTube advertisement without his permission, then used AI to turn the Asian American creator into a white man who does not exist. The ad promoted Kalshi, a platform tied to betting and insider-information trading, and appeared just as scrutiny of cheap AI-generated advertising was intensifying. It is a particularly clear test of whether brands treat a person’s image as material they can quietly rewrite and reuse.
