Ahead of the Victorian state election, the Labor Party published a video featuring an AI-generated image of Liberal leader Jess Wilson, attributing to her the claim that treatment under a Liberal government would cost patients tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars under an American-style system. The Liberals called the video a smear campaign, even as they circulated AI videos of Premier Ben Carroll. Experts and the Greens warn that such material undermines trust in politics and are calling for rules requiring political ads to be truthful, like those in South Australia.
