ChatGPT has been generating cartoons that carry the signatures of real New Yorker cartoonists, including longtime contributors Harry Bliss, Emily Flake, Joe Dator, Pat Byrnes, Peter Vei and Jason Adam Katzenstein. Cartoonist Loper told Nieman Lab that seeing his name on an image he had not made was “surreal.” The episode is more than a false credit: for artists who use a signature to identify work as their own, a model can turn a familiar name into a counterfeit mark.
