Navigation enables a robot to turn its perception of the surrounding world and movement into autonomous task execution. Unlike locomotion, which is responsible for stable movement, navigation must constantly determine the robot’s position, account for changes around it, choose a route, and avoid obstacles. The material demonstrates the COMPASS workflow: an AI agent checks dependencies, prepares scenes, launches training, diagnoses failures, and compares checkpoints. The process was tested on Boston Dynamics Spot, an integrated warehouse, and the SAGE-10K scene.
