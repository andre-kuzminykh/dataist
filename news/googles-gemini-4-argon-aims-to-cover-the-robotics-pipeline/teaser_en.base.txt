Google is asking Gemini 4 Argon to do more than direct a robot: the model can build simulations, generate training tasks, inspect results and revise its instructions. In one demo, Boston Dynamics’ Spot learns in a virtual copy of a pedal bin before trying to drop a soda can into the real one, with two other AI agents checking its foot and grip. The pitch is a single model spanning much of the robotics pipeline, rather than a model limited to one task.
