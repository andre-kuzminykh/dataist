## From research to review

The new features support tasks such as:

- Checking links and quotations
- Comparing laws across countries
- Managing case materials
- Tracking proposed legislation
- Checking contracts against internal rules

Claude for Legal’s catalog includes more than 60 integrations, including Bloomberg Law. Users can also connect their own databases without manually uploading files.

## The platform’s wider pitch

Goodwin, Harvey, Foley & Lardner and PwC also offer their own tools on the platform. Anthropic says built-in settings protect data and preserve client-defined boundaries between teams that must not share information.

I think the source trail and lawyer review are the most consequential parts of the announcement. They make the system’s role legible: it prepares work for a human to check. What I’d want to know is how reliably it catches faulty citations or mismatches in legal text; the announcement describes the workflow, but gives no accuracy results. Without those, the promise is about process, not proof that the checks are dependable.
