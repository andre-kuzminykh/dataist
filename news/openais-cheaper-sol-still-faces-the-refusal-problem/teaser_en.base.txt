OpenAI is trying to make its newest models refuse fewer harmless requests without loosening protections for more capable systems. GPT-6.1 Sol, introduced alongside that effort, is said to approach GPT-6 Astra on coding and computer use while costing one-fifth as much per token. But developers say safeguards still interrupt ordinary work, from robotics to cybersecurity. That tension matters more as cheaper models make repeated refusals a recurring cost in engineers’ time.
