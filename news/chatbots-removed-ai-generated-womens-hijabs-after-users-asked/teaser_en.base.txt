The Guardian tested whether ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini and Claude would remove a hijab from an AI-generated image of a woman. ChatGPT and Grok complied; Gemini first refused, then generated the image after being asked to make the woman “more Western.” Claude said it could not edit images and would not make that change even if it could. The test points to a gap in how chatbots handle religious clothing: a system may treat a hijab as an ordinary styling choice even when changing it can expose someone to humiliation or harassment.
