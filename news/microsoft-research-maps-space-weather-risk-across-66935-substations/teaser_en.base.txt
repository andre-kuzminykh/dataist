Microsoft Research built a machine-learning system to estimate how geomagnetic storms could affect 66,935 substations across the continental United States. It combines solar-wind observations and forecasts of geomagnetic activity with local geology and grid data to estimate risk 30–60 minutes ahead. The aim is more specific than warning that a storm is coming: operators could see where its effects may be strongest. But the system has not been tested as part of live grid operations.
