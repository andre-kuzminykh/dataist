OpenAI has hired a former US Army secretary as it expands its bipartisan support ahead of the congressional elections. The company hopes to strengthen ties with the military and Congress and compete for new government contracts. Dan Driscoll oversaw the Army’s adoption of AI and will now help OpenAI work with government agencies and understand their practical needs. The appointment continues the company’s push into defense: in February 2026, OpenAI won a Pentagon contract to use its models on classified networks after the department barred rival Anthropic.
