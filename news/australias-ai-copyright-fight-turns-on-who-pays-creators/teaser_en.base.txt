Australia’s copyright debate has become a test of whether AI companies can train on creative work without paying for it. The International Confederation of Music Publishers says companies are pressing the government to weaken copyright rules, while the parliamentary committee on artificial intelligence calls the current situation a deadlock. The committee’s dispute is concrete: AI models are trained on copyrighted work in the United States, but companies say they do not train frontier models in Australia under its current laws. The gap between those claims is where the fight over creators’ control and compensation now sits.
