Australia’s population is set to enter a slower, older phase, with deaths outnumbering births for the first time in the 2060s. Treasurer Jim Chalmers presented the finding at the Australian National University after the release of a 40-year intergenerational report covering demographics, public finances and the country’s exposure to global risks. The report’s underlying trade-off is clear: Australia expects living standards to keep rising, but it will need faster productivity growth just as the share of working-age people begins to shrink.
