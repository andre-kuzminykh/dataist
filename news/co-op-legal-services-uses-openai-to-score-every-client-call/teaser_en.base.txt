Co-op Legal Services is using an OpenAI model to record, analyse and score every client call handled by its legal-services staff, according to an employee who spoke to the Guardian. The system checks more than 50 aspects of each conversation and gives managers a pass-or-fail assessment to use in reviewing staff performance. The calls concern wills, probate and property, and many clients have recently experienced a bereavement. The rollout puts a sensitive service under a form of monitoring that unions and workplace experts say can make employees’ working lives more stressful.
