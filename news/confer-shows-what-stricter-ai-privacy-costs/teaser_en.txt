Most consumer AI services offer privacy as a policy, not a technical guarantee. OpenAI, Anthropic and Google provide zero data retention for some paid corporate and developer accounts, while ordinary users generally get weaker promises. More private options do exist—from trusted hardware environments to models running on a laptop—but they narrow model choice, reduce capability or raise the price. The trade-off is no longer abstract: Confer’s cheapest paid plan costs $34.99 a month.
