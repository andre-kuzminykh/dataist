Space Daily, a long-running space publication, used fictional experts to publish AI-generated articles, according to an investigation by Futurism. One of those bylines belonged to “Dr. Katherine Chen,” supposedly a former NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory systems engineer who had spent 15 years designing autonomous systems for deep-space missions. The people behind the site later removed the profiles and many of the articles, then acknowledged that their publications had used author names that did not correspond to real people. The episode matters because the deception was not limited to bad prose: it manufactured expertise as part of the publishing system.
