# Tencent releases Hy4, a model that sees images and controls software

## Images, software and game development

Tencent introduced the full version of Hy4 about two months after its preview. The company says it trained the model on a much larger volume of data than its predecessor. It has not announced a date for publishing the weights.

Hy4 can recognize fine details in photographs and complex diagrams. In one demo, the model found a component about two meters across on the roof of a warehouse shown in a large aerial image.

The model can also operate software independently. Tencent trained it to work with professional tools, including Blender and computer-aided design systems, as well as on tasks that require using several applications in sequence.

The company also links image analysis to programming, including website and game development. For game development, Tencent worked with its own game studios and adapted Hy4 for Unreal, Unity and Godot. In Honor of Kings, the model handles parameter configuration with almost no errors and, according to the development team, finds the causes of errors much more often than its predecessor.

## Finance and office tasks

Tencent also fine-tuned Hy4 for financial and office work. In a trial, more than 200 finance professionals rated the model on nearly 600 real-world tasks. Tencent says Hy4 outperformed the Chinese models Deepseek V4.1 Flash, Kimi K3.1 Pro and GLM-5.3 Flash.

The company also says that after an update to its search function, the model was less likely to make up information when working with web sources: in internal tests, the share of such answers fell from nearly 16% to about 5%. Hy4 can work with a person in real time on Word documents, Excel spreadsheets and PowerPoint presentations.

Tencent’s partners report similar results, but base their assessments on their own trials.

- The Comein platform considers Hy4 the only Chinese model to match Claude Opus 5.5, ranking second; in the company’s assessment, it substantially outperforms OpenAI’s GPT-6.1 Sol on data queries.
- The AI agent platform Manus says Hy4 successfully completes almost all document-related tasks.

An independent assessment by Artificial Analysis paints a more restrained picture. On the Intelligence Index, which combines assessments of knowledge, coding, science and general knowledge, Hy4 scored 35 — above the median score of 30. That puts it level with Grok 4.7 and in second place among models from Chinese labs. Kimi K3.1 Pro ranked higher, with 46 points. In Tencent’s own financial trial, by contrast, Hy4 outperformed Kimi.

Without web search, results are worse. On the AA-Omniscience factual accuracy test, Hy4 answers about half of questions correctly using only its training data. When it does not know an answer, it makes one up 89% of the time instead of acknowledging uncertainty.

## The model helps train other models

In an internal trial, Hy4 ranked first among open models on research tasks across seven fields. Together with the Hyra 2.0 AI agent system, it also helped optimize training pipelines for Tencent’s smaller models.

Hy4 then independently trained its own version of Jev, a decision-making model from US startup TypeSafe AI. Tencent says this version outperformed the original in several tests, but did not disclose their names.

Tencent is releasing specialized biology and physics models created this way as open models. The company plans to use Hy4 in future to support and accelerate its own AI development.

## Safety, pricing and limitations

Ahead of the planned release of the weights, Tencent outlined its safety measures. During training, the company deliberately filtered out material related to hacking. Following reviews by its cybersecurity teams, Tencent found no elevated risk of misuse; its assessment of risks in biology also remained below the threshold it had set. The company says it will publish a detailed safety report alongside the weights.

Pricing is unchanged from the preview: about $0.83 per million input tokens and about $2.50 per million output tokens. Tencent acknowledges that Hy4 still trails the best commercial models by a considerable margin on difficult math, extended programming projects and analysis of complex images. The company plans to release a faster, cheaper version called Hy4 flash next.

Last July, Tencent released Hy3, with 295 billion parameters, under the Apache 2.0 license. Since then, Chinese competitors have moved ahead: Zhipu AI released GLM-5.3, Deepseek released V4.1-Flash-1017, and Moonshot AI released Kimi K3.1 Pro.

In late October, Artificial Analysis updated its index: closed test sets now account for 60% of its score. According to the source, the gap between open models and the leaders has widened from 3 to 12 points.

Tencent’s plans to release the Hy4 weights come amid tighter rules. In October, China’s ministries of commerce and science published a draft export regulation covering weights for models in the global top 10 or models with more than two trillion parameters. For open releases, the draft provides for an expedited process with automatic approval.

Dataist’s take: Hy4 combines a broad set of capabilities with notable limitations. In particular, without search it often replaces uncertainty with a made-up answer, and it trails commercial leaders on difficult tasks. How much the model can strengthen Tencent’s position will become clearer after the weights and detailed safety report are published.
