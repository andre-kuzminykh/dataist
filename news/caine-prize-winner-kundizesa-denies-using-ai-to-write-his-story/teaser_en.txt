Simbarashe Kundizesa, a Zimbabwean writer, denies using generative AI to write “The Blue Jockey,” which won the Caine Prize. The dispute began after a writer questioned the story’s authorship using an AI detector and stylistic clues. The prize’s organizers say they are investigating, while its chair has challenged the idea that a detector can settle the question. The case lands amid other recent accusations against literary award winners, but the evidence described so far points to a harder problem than a score can resolve: how to distinguish suspicion from proof.
