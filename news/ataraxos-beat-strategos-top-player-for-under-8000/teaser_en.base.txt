Ataraxos beat Stratego’s most decorated player, but the result is only part of the story. The system learned through self-play with less than $8,000 in computing costs, a fraction of what DeepMind’s earlier attempt reportedly required. It also won under a handicap: the human could adapt to the AI across their match, while Ataraxos could not adapt to him. The achievement is therefore both a result in a difficult game and a test of how far a much cheaper approach can go.
