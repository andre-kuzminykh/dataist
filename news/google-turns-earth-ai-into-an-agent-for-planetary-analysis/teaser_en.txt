Google is turning Earth AI from a collection of geospatial models into an experimental agent that can plan multi-step analyses and train forecasting models. The system brings Gemini together with satellite, weather, population and mapping data, and is being tested by organizations working on health, disaster response and infrastructure. Google is also opening limited access through Google Earth and an experimental API. The shift matters because the hard part of geospatial work is not just finding data: it is choosing the right tools, combining them and checking what the result can support.
