Voice AI still has not had its ChatGPT moment, according to leaders from Otter and another voice-AI company. At the HumanX conference last month, Wen argued that models can already listen while speaking; the harder step is fast reasoning, so replies arrive without long pauses. For Otter, whose meeting-notes service is also developing digital twins to represent people in meetings, the test is broader: voice systems must understand what was said, preserve the meaning and earn users’ trust.
