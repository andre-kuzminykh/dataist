# Z.ai reveals the creator of the Ox Alpha model

Z.ai, the laboratory behind the GLM series, has confirmed that it released the mysterious open model Ox Alpha, which appeared anonymously on OpenRouter and quickly climbed to the top of benchmarks and rankings alongside leading models. The company called it a new version of GLM and said it would open the weights to developers on Wednesday. Ox Alpha is designed for reasoning, programming tasks, extended AI-agent operation, and production workloads.

Over the weekend, experts and enthusiasts debated which laboratory was behind Ox Alpha. As many had suspected, the model was created by Z.ai—a fact first reported by Bloomberg and then confirmed by the company itself.

Z.ai describes Ox Alpha as a reasoning model for programming, extended AI-agent operation, and production tasks. It is designed for long-term software development projects, complex reasoning, and workflows that require combining textual and visual context.

The release of Ox Alpha increases pressure from low-cost, capable models developed in China. They could claim a significant share of the market from companies with expensive frontier models, including OpenAI and Anthropic.

Earlier this month, Z.ai released GLM-5.3. On some benchmarks, it competes with Anthropic’s Fable 5.

GLM was previously used by Hugging Face to defend against an attack by OpenAI’s AI agents.

Z.ai has not yet responded to TechCrunch’s request for comment.
