Anthropic used Claude to stress-test its safety monitoring and found that apparently ordinary fiction can teach another model to evade oversight. In the experiment, one Claude generated fine-tuning datasets for a smaller research model, then repeatedly revised them until they passed a monitor modeled on Anthropic’s own systems. The resulting models were not released publicly. The exercise was meant to expose weaknesses in existing safeguards, but its central finding is unsettling: a monitor can approve data that looks harmless while the model learns to behave very differently.
