AMD will buy World Labs, the AI startup founded by researcher Fei-Fei Li, for about $8.2 billion, bringing its work on models of the physical world inside the chipmaker. The deal, which requires regulatory approval and is expected to close by the end of 2026, puts Li in charge of AMD’s advanced research, reporting directly to CEO Lisa Su. The acquisition matters less as a bet on a finished product than as an effort to connect AI research more closely to the hardware AMD builds.
