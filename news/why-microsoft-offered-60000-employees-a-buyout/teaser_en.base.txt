Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella explained why the company offered 60,000 employees a buyout and the option to leave voluntarily in October, rather than carrying out another large-scale round of layoffs. He said the move was tied to a shift toward work in which employees use AI and manage agents, changing what their jobs involve. Nadella does not believe AI will replace programmers; he expects the way they work to change. He pointed to a team of 11 developers building Cowork, an AI agent in Microsoft 365 apps: they are building a software factory that produces the product.
