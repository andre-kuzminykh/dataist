One Nation’s AI campaign video is crude, but its larger significance is that Australia’s election rules may not cover it. The clip has drawn attention less for its quality than for what it exposes: regulators have limited reach over political content posted online, while calls to restrict AI-generated election material remain unanswered. And One Nation is not alone. Other Australian political groups and politicians have also circulated AI-made or fabricated material, suggesting this is not a one-off provocation but a growing feature of campaigning.
