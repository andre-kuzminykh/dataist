Nvidia is close to a deal to acquire Inception for at least $500 million, The Information reported, citing three people familiar with the talks. The deal also includes performance bonuses for more than 30 employees of the San Francisco Bay Area startup, which was founded two years ago. Inception develops diffusion-based language models: they refine an entire response at once rather than generating it one token at a time. The acquisition is intended to strengthen Nvidia’s open Nemotron lineup and its work on robotics.
