Ando has launched a workplace messenger built around a premise Slack and Teams were not designed for: AI agents should be participants in team discussions, not applications humans consult on the side. The company, which came out of stealth on Thursday, wants its platform to replace conventional work messengers for teams using agents. It has raised $20 million across pre-seed and seed rounds from Accel, Index Ventures and Emergence, giving that argument a financial runway—but not yet proof that teams want another place to work.
