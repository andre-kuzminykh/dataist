OpenAI is turning background agents into named characters. Its new Dots product lets users create an agent, give it a name and customize its identity, with the company’s longer-term plan to group multiple agents into teams working on a user’s behalf. Dots arrive Tuesday in ChatGPT for Pro and Business Premium users in supported markets, and can also be launched from Codex. The product matters less as a new agent capability than as a new way to package and manage agents that already exist across Codex and similar systems.
