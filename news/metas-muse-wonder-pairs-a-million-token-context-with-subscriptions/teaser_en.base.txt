Meta has brought Muse Wonder to consumers as a subscription product, pairing a one-million-token context window with Active Memory and Active Focus for longer-running work. The model can plan tasks, use browsers and desktop or mobile apps, and delegate work to subagents. That makes it more than a chatbot pitch: Meta is selling an agent that can keep working across steps, while its benchmark score puts the larger Muse Wonder Full alongside Claude Sonnet 5.5.
