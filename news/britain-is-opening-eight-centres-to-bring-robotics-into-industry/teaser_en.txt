Britain is setting up eight robotics adoption centres across sectors including agriculture, food production, surgery, energy and waste management. The programme also includes £2.5 million for 12 projects in practical robotics training and apprenticeships. Its test is not whether the country can build impressive machines, but whether businesses can put them to work: the government’s case is that wider adoption could support reindustrialisation, while the announcement offers no measure yet of how much adoption the centres will deliver.
