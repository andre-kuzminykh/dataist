SKF has used AI to bring Greta Garbo back for a 99-second television ad about bearings. The Swedish company’s choice has a historical hook: Garbo appeared in an SKF advertising film in 1921, when she was 15 and still known as Greta Gustafsson. The new spot turns that brief connection into a pitch for the smooth rotation of SKF’s industrial product—and makes a famous screen presence sound like a lifeless sales presenter.
