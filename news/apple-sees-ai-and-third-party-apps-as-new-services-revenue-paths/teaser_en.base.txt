Apple is looking for new ways to grow its services business as App Store commissions face regulatory pressure and mobile gaming weakens. On Monday’s call, Apple finance chief Parekh pointed to AI as a possible source of future growth: it could encourage customers to upgrade their devices, while third-party apps and partnerships may offer another route. He did not explain how Apple might make money from those apps, leaving open the question investors are asking: whether the company could take a cut when AI agents distributed through the App Store generate revenue.
