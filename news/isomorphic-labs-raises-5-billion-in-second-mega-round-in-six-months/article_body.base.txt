# Isomorphic Labs raises $5 billion to develop drugs with AI

## Platform and drug development

In May 2026, Isomorphic Labs raised $2.1 billion. Founded in 2021, the company is developing IsoDDE, the Isomorphic Drug Design Engine. The company says the system goes beyond AlphaFold 3 and is designed to predict more accurately how drugs bind to targets and where they interact.

Isomorphic Labs works with Novartis, Eli Lilly and Johnson & Johnson. The company does not yet have an approved drug.

[INFO:facts] $5 billion :: new round | $2.1 billion :: May 2026 round

## Leadership and the AlphaFold team

Isomorphic Labs is led by Demis Hassabis. In August 2026, he stepped back from day-to-day leadership of Google DeepMind and became Alphabet’s chief scientist. In 2024, Hassabis and John Jumper received the Nobel Prize in Chemistry for their work on AlphaFold.

In July 2026, DeepMind disbanded the original AlphaFold team. Many researchers moved to Isomorphic Labs or took on other projects, while nearly a quarter left DeepMind. In early November 2026, however, the company released AlphaGenome 2: a model that predicts how DNA variants affect gene activity in specific cell types.

Dataist's take: The new round will allow Isomorphic Labs to keep developing its platform and preparing drug candidates for clinical trials. But the company still has no approved drug, so the funding does not yet mean its work has reached patients.
