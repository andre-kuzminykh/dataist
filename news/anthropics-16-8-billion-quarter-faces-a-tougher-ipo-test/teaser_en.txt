Anthropic’s third-quarter revenue reached $16.8 billion, but growth has slowed ahead of its planned November IPO. The company also reported a second consecutive quarter of adjusted profit, suggesting its trained models can generate returns even after months of price competition. But the figures have disappointed some prospective investors, and Anthropic has not disclosed either its September revenue run rate or a full-year forecast. That leaves a central question for the offering: whether the company’s improving economics can offset doubts about growth and the AI market.
