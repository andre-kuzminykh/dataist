Meta’s board is confronting Mark Zuckerberg over spending on AI after the company’s free cash flow turned negative in the third quarter of 2026 for the first time since 2012. The immediate dispute is about Zuckerberg’s compensation: he has pushed for a substantial increase, mostly in stock, and several directors reportedly threatened to leave if he did not back down. But the deeper issue is whether Meta’s escalating AI investment is producing enough to justify its cost.
