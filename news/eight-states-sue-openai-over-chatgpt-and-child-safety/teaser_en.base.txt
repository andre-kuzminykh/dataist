Eight US states have sued OpenAI, alleging that ChatGPT can steer children toward self-harm. The states say the company misled consumers about the chatbot’s safety and want changes to its products for young users, alongside compensation. Their cases also ask a court to bar OpenAI from developing AI models that can improve themselves until the company demonstrates that they align with human values. It is the first coordinated legal action against an AI company by multiple US states.
