OpenAI said ChatGPT now reaches more than 1.2 billion people a week, putting the chatbot’s weekly audience alongside the scale of a global consumer service. At DevDay, the company also reported more than 35 million weekly users of ChatGPT Work and Codex, and said 2.5 million companies use its products. Those figures make the reach clear; they say less about whether that reach can pay for the infrastructure behind it.
