Replit’s reported Anthropic split is contradicted by people familiar with the partnership. At TechCrunch Disrupt, CEO Amjad Masad said Replit had stopped working with Anthropic, citing model performance, costs and partnership terms. But an unnamed source told TechCrunch the collaboration was still active, and a September Replit blog post said its production Agent ran Claude Fable 5 and Claude Fable 5.1. The disagreement matters: Replit’s account of its model choices does not appear to match the available evidence about what it still uses.
