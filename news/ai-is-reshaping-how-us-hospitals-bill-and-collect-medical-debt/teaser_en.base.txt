Medical debt is becoming a growth market for AI: as more Americans face higher out-of-pocket costs, hospitals and debt buyers are using algorithms to decide how much patients might pay and what discounts to offer. A 2025 survey found that more than a quarter of U.S. adults reported medical debt, including roughly one in four people with insurance. The technology promises to make billing more efficient, but it also brings a sharper question: when an algorithm personalizes a patient’s bill, whose interests is it optimizing?
