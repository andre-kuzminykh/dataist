OpenAI’s head of global affairs, Chris Lehane, is urging policymakers to prepare AI policies ahead of the 2028 presidential election. In a new essay, he compares AI’s potential economic impact to Franklin Roosevelt’s New Deal and outlines five priorities, from worker protections and education to AI safety and data center construction. Lehane acknowledges Americans’ concerns but is betting on wider AI adoption; critics say his proposals include few commitments that would help distribute the technology’s benefits or limit corporate power.
