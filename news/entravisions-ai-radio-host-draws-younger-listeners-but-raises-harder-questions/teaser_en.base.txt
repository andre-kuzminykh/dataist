Entravision has turned an AI radio character into a weekday morning co-host, then syndicated the show to five more markets. Coyoteque speaks Spanish to a Latino audience and addresses immigration, racism and human rights—subjects the station’s executives once worried could put human hosts at risk. Nielsen figures point to a sharp audience increase, especially among younger listeners. But the experiment’s harder test is not whether an AI voice can attract an audience; it is whether a station can build trust with one while its workers question what automation is replacing.
