ChatGPT can now turn text prompts and photos into animated GIFs. The update runs on OpenAI Images 2.6 and is gradually rolling out to all ChatGPT and Codex users on the web, desktop and mobile apps. After generation, users can adjust the animation speed and edit individual frames. OpenAI has also released two API models for developers: GPT-Image-2.6 Flare for fast image generation and Sunburst for higher-quality results.
