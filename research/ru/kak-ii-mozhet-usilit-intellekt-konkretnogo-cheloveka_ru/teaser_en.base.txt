AI has spent years learning to act on its own—but what if its most powerful role is helping one person think and remember better?

The authors propose an AI companion that stays close to a person’s daily life, remembers useful context over time, and offers help when it matters. Rather than simply doing tasks on someone’s behalf, it could help bridge the gap between what a person knows and what they can bring to mind in the moment. That promise also raises questions about privacy, fairness, and pressure to use such a system.

In this review we look at how this kind of partnership might work, what it could make possible, and how to protect the people who rely on it.
