Runware wants to build data centers on a factory line, with a target of producing 30 MW of capacity a day by 2028 at its plant in Romania. That would amount to nearly 1 GW a month, or about 10 GW over a year—roughly the capacity expected to be added across the United States this year. The ambition is not just to make data centers modular, a model already sold by companies including Crusoe and Schneider Electric, but to manufacture and deploy them faster.
