At least 25 court cases in England and Wales were affected in 2026 by undisclosed use of AI. In published rulings, judges described fabricated legal precedents and statutes, as well as witness statements prepared with AI. The cases include a police officer who asked Microsoft Copilot to draft a statement designed to secure the longest possible sentence for a defendant and make the judge “cry”. An investigation by the Bureau of Investigative Journalism, published jointly with the Guardian, identified the cases through court rulings.
