The head of the International Monetary Fund, Kristalina Georgieva, warned that demand for AI equipment and related technologies is fueling inflation and could push major central banks to raise interest rates. Along with the energy shock caused by the war in the Middle East, she expects it to bring global inflation to 4.7% in 2026. At the same time, rapid AI adoption is supporting economic growth, but countries need digital infrastructure and workers prepared for new technologies.
