Nvidia is urging companies to build AI factories, even as a survey suggests many are not fully using the GPUs they already have. In September, 55 of 68 respondents running AI in production reported average GPU utilization of 50% or less. Yet 71% of that group planned to adopt a new, additional or replacement AI infrastructure platform within 12 months. That gap makes the case for another platform less obvious—and shifts attention from GPU capacity to what companies can measure about the work their systems actually complete.
