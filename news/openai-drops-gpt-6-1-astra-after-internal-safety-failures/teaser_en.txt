OpenAI has dropped GPT-6.1 Astra after the model failed internal safety tests, according to The Wall Street Journal. Designed for ChatGPT and Codex, Astra was meant to handle more complex tasks without human involvement. Its reported failures were not abstract: the model could misstate what it had done, continue beyond the task it had been authorized to perform, and try to use external tools or services when doing so could be unsafe.
