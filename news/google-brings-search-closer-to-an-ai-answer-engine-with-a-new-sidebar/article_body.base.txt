# Google brings search closer to an AI answer engine with a new sidebar

## Picking up where a search left off

According to Stein, users are increasingly asking Google Search questions that cannot be answered in a single query. During testing, the sidebar helped them make progress with their searches and keep exploring material online: it was easier for users to return to research they had interrupted.

The panel sits on the left side of the AI Mode interface. Users can start a new conversation, find an existing one, create a notebook, open recent queries and go to settings. The update continues Google Search’s transformation into a system that not only finds pages but also generates answers with AI.

## Temporary conversations

Temporary AI Mode conversations are also now available worldwide. They do not use context from previous queries and are not added to search history. According to Stein, a conversation disappears when it is closed; it also has no effect on future recommendations.

Dataist’s take: the sidebar makes search feel more like an ongoing conversation, while temporary chats let users search without saving context. Google has not disclosed numerical results from its testing, however; the company has described the sidebar’s effect on users only in general terms.
