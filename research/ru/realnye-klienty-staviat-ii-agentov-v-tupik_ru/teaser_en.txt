An AI agent can ace a neatly written task and still leave a real customer stuck.

The authors introduce a testbed where agents must do more than follow instructions: they have to ask customers the right questions, uncover hidden requirements, and make changes in realistic software systems. Across projects modeled on real customer work, even the strongest agent often failed to deliver a solution that meaningfully improved the customer’s results.

In this review we look at how the testbed works, where AI agents stumble, and what it takes to move from completing tasks to solving customers’ real problems.
