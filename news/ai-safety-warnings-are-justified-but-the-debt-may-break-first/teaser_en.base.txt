AI safety warnings deserve attention, but the nearer threat to the industry may be financial. AI companies are spending billions on data centers while the price customers pay for model access keeps falling. Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta and Oracle are expected to raise $132 billion (£99 billion) for infrastructure this year, even as the economics of each AI service remain unsettled. If revenue growth slows, the commitments behind that expansion could become a problem far beyond Silicon Valley.
