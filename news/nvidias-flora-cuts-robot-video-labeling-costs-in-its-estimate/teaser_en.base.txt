Nvidia has released Flora, an open set of models for labeling robot-training videos across three tasks: describing actions and when they happen, scoring progress for trial-and-error learning, and flagging successful outcomes after deployment. The company says Flora is already used to train GR00T and can process up to 200,000 hours of video a day on 512 GB300 GPUs. Nvidia’s cost estimate puts an hour of labeling at about 72 cents, versus about $88.50 for GPT-6 Astra. The release matters because robot training needs more than video: it needs labels detailed enough to turn footage into data models can learn from.
