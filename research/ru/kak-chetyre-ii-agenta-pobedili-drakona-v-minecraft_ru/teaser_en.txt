What happens when four AI agents enter Minecraft with no assigned roles, only one shared goal: defeat the dragon?

The agents planned, wrote, and debugged their own code while coordinating through in-game chat and shared memory. Over more than a day, they tackled thousands of linked tasks, learned from setbacks, and helped recover one another’s gear—offering a glimpse of how AI teams might handle lengthy, complex work beyond a game.

In this review we look at how the experiment unfolded, how the agents divided and coordinated their work, and what their victory reveals about the limits of autonomous AI.
