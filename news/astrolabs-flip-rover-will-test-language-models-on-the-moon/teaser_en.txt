Astrolab’s 480-kilogram FLIP rover is set to carry a high-performance HPE computer that will run large language models on the Moon for the first time. The models will analyze camera and lidar data to help plan routes and extract useful findings, potentially reducing what the rover must send back to Earth. The goal is not just to put AI on the lunar surface: it is to test whether onboard computing can help robots work with less reliance on delayed, limited communications.
