Snapchat has introduced Easy Lens CLI, a tool for creating AR Lens effects with AI coding assistants such as Claude Code, Cursor and Codex. Creators can describe an idea in plain language, and an assistant will build the effect, prepare a preview and help publish it on Snapchat. The tool offers more than 100 augmented reality building blocks and 50 AI asset generators. Developers enrolled in the Lens+ Payouts program can earn money from the effects they create. Last month, Snapchat paid $1.8 million to members of the Lens+ creator community.
