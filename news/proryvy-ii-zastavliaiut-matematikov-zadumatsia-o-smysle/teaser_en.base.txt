Recent mathematical breakthroughs by AI still look like an intelligent rearrangement of already known ideas, rather than the creation of a truly new theory. But if this changes, mathematicians will have to answer what the purpose of their research is. This month, OpenAI's Astra model solved a key problem in group theory—it proved the existence of non-sofic groups. Its proof is mainly based on a small modification of the theorems of Gabor Kun and Andreas Thom. Dr. Henry Bradford believes that it would now be rash to rule out the emergence of superhuman abilities in AI across all areas of mathematical reasoning in the coming years.
