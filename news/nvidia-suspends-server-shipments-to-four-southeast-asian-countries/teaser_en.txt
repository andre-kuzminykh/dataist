Nvidia has asked partners, including Dell and Supermicro, to halt server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia. The company is responding to new US export restrictions designed to prevent Chinese companies from accessing AI chips through overseas cloud providers. Amid the new rules, sellers have put an unusually large number of Nvidia GPUs on the market, including thousands of B300 chips. Cloud providers in Southeast Asia have 90 days to obtain a license; banks have already suspended about $500 million in loans to Singaporean company Aolani.
