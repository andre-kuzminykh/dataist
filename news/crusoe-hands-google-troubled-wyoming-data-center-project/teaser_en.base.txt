Crusoe sold Google an unfinished data center project in southeastern Wyoming. Google, which had previously declined to become the site’s customer, will now develop it itself. Reports said the company was concerned about the project’s cost and construction timeline under Crusoe’s management. Google did not disclose the sale price, but announced plans to invest $17 billion in the campus. Crusoe announced the project last year with Tallgrass, an infrastructure company backed by Blackstone. The site was expected to eventually reach 10 GW of capacity.
