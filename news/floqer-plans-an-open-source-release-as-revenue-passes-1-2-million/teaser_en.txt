Floqer is taking its AI prospecting platform toward open source, saying it plans to release its customer knowledge base in early 2027 if security checks go well. The startup says annual recurring revenue has passed $1.2 million and total revenue grew about tenfold over the last year. The move would address a practical limit: Floqer says it cannot currently serve everyone who wants access, while some customers want to keep customer data in-house or adapt the technology themselves.
