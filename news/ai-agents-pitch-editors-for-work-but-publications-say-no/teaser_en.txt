An AI agent named Jaden pitched Defector on an article about record-keeping at a British third-division football club and the question of “who owns memory.” The pitch arrived amid a broader wave of bots emailing editors for work. So far, the reporting has found no evidence that any of these agents have landed a magazine or newspaper assignment. Their attempts are testing not just whether machines can write, but whether editors will trust work whose authorship and reporting process are hard to verify.
