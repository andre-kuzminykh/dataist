DataVita has revised its plan for a data center in Lanarkshire: it will now draw electricity from the UK’s national grid, while solar and wind farms may connect to it in the future. The company had previously promised to supply the site with 1 GW of renewable energy. The project in Chapelhall was meant to become one of the key sites in the UK’s AI strategy and create 3,400 jobs. DataVita is still receiving £202 million in government guarantees.
