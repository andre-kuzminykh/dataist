A research idea can look novel until someone finds the paper that already tried it. LitPivot, a system for developing research proposals alongside relevant literature, is designed to make that collision part of the drafting process: researchers can test a proposal’s problem, contribution or evaluation plan against selected papers, then revise the idea and reconsider which sources matter. In a study with 17 researchers, proposals developed with LitPivot were rated as better supported by relevant literature than those made with a text editor and an AI tool for querying papers.
