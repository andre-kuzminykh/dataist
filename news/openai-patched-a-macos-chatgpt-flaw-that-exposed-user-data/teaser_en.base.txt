A flaw in OpenAI’s ChatGPT app for macOS could have let an attacker take control of the app and reach users’ conversations and other data, including browser sessions. OpenAI confirmed the vulnerability and said it had issued a fix in a system changelog on September 25. The incident points to a risk beyond AI tools being used in cyberattacks: the AI apps themselves can become targets, with the access they need to work making a compromise more consequential.
