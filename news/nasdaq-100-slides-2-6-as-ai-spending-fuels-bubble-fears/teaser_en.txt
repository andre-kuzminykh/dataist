Friday’s selloff in US stocks sharpened doubts about whether the AI boom can justify the spending behind it. The Nasdaq 100 fell 2.6%, with Amazon and Alphabet among the companies under pressure as investors weighed vast infrastructure costs against uncertain returns. The index was moving toward a $1 trillion loss, NBC News reported. The market’s concern is not simply that AI stocks have run up; it is that the largest companies may be committing more capital than the business can yet support.
