Amazon has agreed to buy a significant part of Israeli startup Decart. Bloomberg puts the deal at $7.5 billion. Amazon will acquire Decart Optimization Stack (DOS), a technology for optimizing software stacks, along with the company’s founders and AI specialists. The software helps models run more efficiently on hardware from Nvidia, Amazon and Google. The deal could make it easier for customers to switch to Trainium chips: Amazon is preparing to sell them to outside customers as an alternative to Nvidia.
