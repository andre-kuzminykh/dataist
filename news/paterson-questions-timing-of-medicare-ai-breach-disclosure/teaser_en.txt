Senator Paterson has questioned whether the timing of the Medicare AI breach disclosure was accidental. Speaking on ABC’s 7.30 the previous evening, he said the government still had to explain when it learned about the incident and why Prime Minister Albanese chose to disclose it in New York, in that way and at that moment. Paterson linked the setting to Albanese’s political effort to show he was willing to confront major technology companies over AI. The dispute is therefore about both the accessed data and the government’s account of its response.
