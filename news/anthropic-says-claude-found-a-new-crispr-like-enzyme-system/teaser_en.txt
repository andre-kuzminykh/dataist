Anthropic says Claude has identified a previously undescribed enzyme system in bacteriophages, with a repeating DNA structure reminiscent of CRISPR. The system, called ART, includes a reverse transcriptase, a neighboring partner gene and a long array of evenly spaced repeats. Its function remains unknown, but the discovery is an early test of Anthropic’s larger claim: that AI agents can search biological data, generate hypotheses and help decide which ones deserve experiments.
