Activists from the Dutch group Geef Tegengas entered a Microsoft data center construction site in Amsterdam’s Westpoort district and clashed with police. Some climbed a crane, lit signal flares and tied helium balloons to equipment. Separately, more than 100 groups gathered outside the site as part of the pan-European Stop Dirty Data Centres campaign, calling for a two-day halt to construction of new data centers. The protests are linked to the environmental and social effects of expanding AI infrastructure.
