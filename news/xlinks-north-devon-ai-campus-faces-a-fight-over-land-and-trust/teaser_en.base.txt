North Devon’s proposed AI campus puts land, water and trust on the line. Xlinks wants to build a 1.5 GW data-centre campus in a UNESCO biosphere reserve, alongside a 1.8 GW battery-storage system. Residents say they learned of private talks between the company and local officials only after obtaining documents through information requests. The dispute is about more than the scale of the development: it is also about who gets to shape it, and when.
