Oregon and Virginia have filed separate lawsuits against SpaceX and its subsidiary X Corp. They accuse the companies of violating consumer protection laws by misleading users about Grok’s safety and safeguards against the spread of child sexual abuse material and non-consensual intimate images. The lawsuits follow a surge in requests for the chatbot to create sexualized images of women and minors, as well as the discovery of videos with similar content on Grok’s website.
