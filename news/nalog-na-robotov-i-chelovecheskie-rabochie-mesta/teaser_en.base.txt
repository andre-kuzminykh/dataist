Bill Gates published a lengthy essay on the social consequences of AI on the Gates Notes website. The Microsoft co-founder supported the idea of slowing the development of advanced models, but questioned whether such an approach would be sustainable. At the same time, he noted the benefits of AI for science and healthcare and proposed two new tools to protect the labor market: a tax on robots that would fund retraining and social support, and a list of professions in which the use of AI would be prohibited. In Gates’s view, such measures could significantly reduce the profits of large AI labs.
