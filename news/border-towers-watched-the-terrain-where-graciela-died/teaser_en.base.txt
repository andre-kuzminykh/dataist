Graciela Gómez Hernández died on September 14, 2025, after crossing into Southern California through the mountains east of Tijuana. Her family’s last messages came from a landscape watched by a General Dynamics tower about a mile away, part of a border surveillance network costing more than $1 billion. An investigation by MIT Technology Review and the Times of San Diego found at least 138 migrant deaths from 2022 to 2025 within the nominal coverage areas of border towers, including at least 81 where the terrain likely offered cameras a direct view.
