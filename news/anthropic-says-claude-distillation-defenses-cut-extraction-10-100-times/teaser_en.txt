Anthropic says it has cut the number of Claude reasoning tokens extracted in distillation campaigns by 10–100 times, using a mix of tighter access controls, classifiers and account enforcement. The result matters because reasoning traces can teach a smaller model how a stronger one reaches an answer, not just what answer to give. But Anthropic’s account also describes a costly, ongoing contest: the company says it has blocked tens of thousands of accounts, while acknowledging that attackers have not yet mounted their most persistent effort to defeat the new defenses.
