Bank of England Governor Andrew Bailey says authorities must retain the ability to intervene as advanced AI models pose growing risks to financial stability. He argues for testing new models and building standards before deciding where intervention is needed—not rushing into tighter regulation. The warning comes as the Bank’s Financial Policy Committee flags a surge in AI-related borrowing: major AI companies borrowed $450 billion from January to September, more than the UK government plans to raise through bonds in all of 2026.
