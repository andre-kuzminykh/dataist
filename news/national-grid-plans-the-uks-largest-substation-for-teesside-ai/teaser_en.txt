National Grid plans to build what it describes as the UK’s largest substation at Teesside, next to a proposed AI data centre. The deal addresses a basic constraint on the project: getting enough power to a site quickly. The data centre, planned for Teesworks, would cover almost 500,000 square metres, while the substation would sit on 29.5 hectares of land. The project could give the region access to 3.2 GW of power, according to Tees Valley mayor Ben Houchen.
