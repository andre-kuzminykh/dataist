Trump’s push to replace “AI” with “SI” is finding echoes among tech leaders. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella has framed the coming era as one of “superintelligence,” while Elon Musk has adopted the new initials in public and renamed his AI company SpaceXSI. The shift matters less as a change in terminology than as a visible response to a president who has moved from proposing a new name to ordering its use in government documents.
