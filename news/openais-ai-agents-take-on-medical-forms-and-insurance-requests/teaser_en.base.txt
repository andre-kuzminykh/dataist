OpenAI is proposing GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6 Luna as digital assistants for clinics. They work in medical software and insurance portals, clicking buttons and entering information as a person would. In tests, both models handled administrative tasks; according to the company, processing a task with GPT-6 Luna costs about 8.5 cents. CVS Health is already using agents like these to process referrals. For now, the offering is aimed at the US market and takes its data-protection requirements into account.
