Claude routed a teenager toward a 518-metre rock face in British Columbia, and North Shore Rescue had to bring him out by helicopter. The teenager, who had indoor climbing experience but lacked the preparation and gear for the route, asked the chatbot for directions on a hike from Grouse Mountain to Crown Mountain and back. He drifted off course and became stranded below Widowmaker Arete, where rescuers had to lower two team members to reach him.
