Apate.ai sends AI agents into scam calls, messages and online groups posing as trusting victims. The Australian startup says its agents have spent a combined 17 years talking with scammers across 2.5 million conversations, gathering details such as bank accounts, crypto wallets, phone numbers and phishing sites. Banks, telecom companies, government agencies and utilities use that intelligence to act before a real customer is defrauded. The model turns time-wasting into data collection: instead of merely keeping scammers busy, Apate aims to learn how their schemes work.
