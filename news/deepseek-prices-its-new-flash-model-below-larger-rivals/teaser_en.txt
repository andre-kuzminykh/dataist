DeepSeek has updated its Flash model, positioning it as a cheaper option for coding and AI-agent work while claiming stronger benchmark results than several larger models. V4.1-Flash-1017 is available on the company’s website, app and API, with weights on Hugging Face and ModelScope. Alongside the release, DeepSeek opened its Search and Fetch web-search tools to outside developers through an API—a move that extends the announcement beyond model performance to the infrastructure around it.
