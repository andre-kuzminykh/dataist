The $31 billion data center planned for rural Queensland is being sold as an AI investment and a source of jobs. For people near Dalby, it also revives memories of the coal seam gas boom: promises of prosperity, rapid change and businesses left exposed when the industry moved on. The project would put Australia’s largest data center on a 725-hectare cattle feedlot, about 15 minutes from Denver Kanowski’s home. Its 2.16 GW peak demand would equal roughly a quarter of Queensland’s total energy use.
