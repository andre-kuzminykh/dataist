Google has launched Gemini 4 Neon, a mid-sized language model for complex tasks, including programming. It is available through the paid Gemini API in Google AI Studio and Android Studio, as well as in the Gemini app and Google’s AI development platform Antigravity for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers. Neon costs $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens: 25% less than Opus 5.5’s standard rates. On two engineering benchmarks, it scored within about one percentage point of Anthropic’s flagship model.
