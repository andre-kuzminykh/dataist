# Gemini Tasks can call businesses and make purchases

## What Tasks can do

In regular Chat mode, Gemini answers informational queries. Tasks lets users ask the app to act on their behalf: for example, contact a business to arrange a meeting or place an order. Google also says Gemini can sign in to websites securely at the user's request.

Gemini vice president Josh Woodward wrote in a blog post that he recently asked the feature to call local car dealerships and book servicing for his family's minivan. He also used Gemini to analyze an electricity bill.

The feature launched in May for a select group of testers under the name Gemini Spark, as an assistant that could work around the clock. Google AI Ultra subscribers in the US later got access. Gemini Spark was then renamed Task Mode, and now Google is beginning to offer the feature for free to hundreds of millions of users of the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model.

To turn on the mode, users simply switch from Chat to Tasks in the Gemini app. They can send requests as text or speak them by calling the app.

Dataist's take: Tasks moves Gemini beyond answering questions to carrying out errands, including calls and purchases. The source does not specify exactly which actions are available to all users or when the feature will ask them to confirm an operation.
