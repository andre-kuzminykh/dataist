Manus 2.0 adds video editing and game development to its desktop app, now called Manus Studio. A separate app, Cue, gives each agent its own email address, wallet and computer, extending Manus beyond one-off tasks toward agents that can stay online and act remotely. The launch matters because it puts Manus in direct contrast with Meta’s Muse, which also gives each user a cloud computer, after Meta’s attempted acquisition of Manus fell apart.
