OpenAI plans to train its models on data from Daybreak Blue cybersecurity sessions, material it had not previously used for training. The change has alarmed researchers worried about a repeat of a recent dispute over work submitted to Codex: mathematicians Tristan Buckmaster and Levent Alpöge uploaded draft Navier–Stokes equations, then OpenAI presented its own proof while denying it had used their material for training. The concern is not proof of misuse. It is that the new policy could make the distinction harder to trust.
