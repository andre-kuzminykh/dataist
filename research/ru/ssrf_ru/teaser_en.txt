What happens when an AI agent can act on the web, but a link leads somewhere it should never reach?

The paper examines server-side request forgery, a security flaw that tricks a web service into fetching information on an attacker’s behalf. It reviews how this weakness can expose private systems and data, and how to spot and prevent it.

In this review we look at how the attack works, why it is difficult to contain, and what defenses can help keep web services from reaching unsafe destinations.
