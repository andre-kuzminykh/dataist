A former OpenAI researcher and University of Texas at Austin professor, Scott Aaronson, believes AI’s mathematical breakthroughs matter less for their individual results than for what they show systems can already do. A proof of the Riemann hypothesis is unlikely to change everyday life, though ideas from this kind of research may eventually find applications in science and engineering. But Aaronson says AI’s ability to make mathematical discoveries could affect the global economy and even threaten all life on Earth.
