## What each tier gets

Google described the changes on its support page. The new lineup leaves free users with Flash-Lite, removes Pro from AI Plus, and reserves all three models for AI Pro and AI Ultra.

[INFO:compare] Free :: Flash-Lite | AI Plus :: no Pro | AI Pro and Ultra :: all three

## The audience may barely notice

Most people who use Google’s AI occasionally may not know which model answers their prompts. Experienced users make up only a few percent of the audience and are likely already paying for subscriptions, according to the report.

I think the sharper question is whether this is mainly a pricing change or preparation for a more expensive model. Google may be getting its plans ready for the larger Gemini 4 Argon, whose operation could cost more. That possibility is not confirmed, but it would make today’s tier changes look less like a simple feature cut and more like groundwork for a costlier generation.
