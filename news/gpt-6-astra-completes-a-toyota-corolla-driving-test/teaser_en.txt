Three frontier models took control of a Toyota Corolla in a small driving test. Only OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra completed the course, on its second attempt—and the car covered less than 500 feet. The result is a narrow success, not evidence that general-purpose AI is ready for the road. But it offers a useful look at where these models stumble: not at high-speed decisions, but at interpreting a simple line of cones.
