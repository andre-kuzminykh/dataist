A faulty software update has reportedly disabled Samsung refrigerators in South Korea, leaving owners with expensive appliances that could no longer keep food cold. The failure arrived just before Chuseok, the three-day mid-autumn harvest holiday, when some families had stocked up on special foods. Samsung customers were told that a service technician could not arrive before October 1—after the holiday had ended—turning a software problem into spoiled food and a broken household appliance.
