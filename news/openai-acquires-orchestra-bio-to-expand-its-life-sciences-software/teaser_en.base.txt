OpenAI has acquired Orchestra Bio, a developer of AI tools for biotechnology companies, and plans to bring its team and technology into Rosalind, its life-sciences research model, and Rosalind Workbench. The deal adds software for the day-to-day work of drug development to OpenAI’s life-sciences effort: Orchestra’s agents track finances and help resolve clinical-trial scheduling problems. OpenAI says Rosalind already has Moderna and Novo Nordisk among its customers.
