Forty researchers at the Technical University of Darmstadt argue that the rise of generative AI makes computer science more important and calls for changes to what it teaches. In a joint position paper, they propose developing it as the “science of computers and AI”: teaching students not only to write code, but also to check and manage AI-built systems. The authors also point to a new approach, “generative computing,” in which systems create behavior and code while running. This raises questions about safety, energy use and social responsibility.
