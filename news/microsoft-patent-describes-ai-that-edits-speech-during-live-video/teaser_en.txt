Microsoft has patented a system that could alter speech and a speaker’s face during a live video, letting viewers hear a version tailored to their preferences. The patent describes AI agents that detect specified words, then replace or remove them while adjusting the speaker’s mouth and face to match. It also allows edits to the audio and video around each change, so the result sounds and looks smooth. The filing covers live events and augmented reality, but a patent describes a possible system, not a product Microsoft has announced.
