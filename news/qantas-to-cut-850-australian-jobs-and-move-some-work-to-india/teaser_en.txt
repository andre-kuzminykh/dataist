Qantas plans to cut 850 jobs in Australia and transfer some functions to an Accenture team in India. The restructuring is tied to a multiyear agreement to modernize the airline’s internal systems, processes and data, and to increase investment in technology and AI. The first changes are expected by the middle of next year. This is the third major job-cut plan announced by an Australian company in three weeks: Canva and National Australia Bank previously announced restructurings.
