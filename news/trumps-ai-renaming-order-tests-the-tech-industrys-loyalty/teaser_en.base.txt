Donald Trump has turned a terminology dispute into a loyalty test. At a White House lunch with leaders from the biggest AI companies, he signed an order telling the government to avoid the term “artificial intelligence” and call the field “superintelligence” instead. The executives present appeared to accept the change without objection. That matters less as a language policy than as a glimpse of how readily the industry’s most powerful figures will accommodate a president who expects the private sector to follow his lead.
