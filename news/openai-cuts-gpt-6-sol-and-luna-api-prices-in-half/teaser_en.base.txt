OpenAI has released updated versions of GPT-6 Sol and Luna, extending the GPT-6 generation beyond the flagship Astra model launched earlier this month. The new models are aimed at the more practical end of the lineup: Sol for difficult work such as programming, and Luna for high-volume administrative tasks. OpenAI says they cost half as much to access through the API as their 5.6-series predecessors, while GPT-6 Sol makes about half as many errors as the previous version.
