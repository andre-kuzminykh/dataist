Xiaomi has released the MiMo-V2.6 family, putting its larger MiMo-V2.6-Pro at the top of Artificial Analysis’s ranking of affordable open models. The model scored 46 points while charging $0.435 per million input tokens and $0.87 per million output tokens. Xiaomi says a benchmark task costs about $0.13, giving the model an unusually strong quality-to-price position. The launch also lands alongside Anthropic’s allegation that Xiaomi extracted training data from Claude through its own models, making the company’s public emphasis on openness harder to ignore.
