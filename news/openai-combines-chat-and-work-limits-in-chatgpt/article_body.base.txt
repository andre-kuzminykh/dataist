# OpenAI combines Chat and Work limits in ChatGPT

## How the limits will change

Thibault “Thibault” Sotsieu, a product lead, announced the start of the rollout on X on Tuesday. OpenAI had earlier announced the combination of Chat and Work in an email to users.

Some subscribers fear that a shared limit will reduce their access to advanced reasoning models, even though the subscription price will stay the same. These concerns are being discussed on social media and on OpenAI’s community forum.

In the new setup, Chat and Work will use one counter in ChatGPT. Messages handled by the Instant model will not count toward the shared limit. Users can continue using Instant even after reaching the limit. The company representative also said OpenAI plans to improve efficiency so subscribers can get more from their plans.

The update is currently available to a small group of users. According to OpenAI’s support page, the company plans to extend the shared limits more broadly in December.

## Other changes and reasons

In September, Anthropic also brought its conversational service Claude and Cowork, which is designed for work tasks, into a single interface.

The changes come as AI companies spend more on infrastructure and demand grows for increasingly capable models. This is putting more pressure on computing resources. A unified interface could make chatbots easier to use: users would be able to move from ordinary conversations to more complex tasks without switching between tabs.

Dataist’s take: Combining the modes makes the interface simpler, but the most active users may reach the shared limit sooner. Conversations with Instant will remain unlimited, even after the shared limit is reached.
