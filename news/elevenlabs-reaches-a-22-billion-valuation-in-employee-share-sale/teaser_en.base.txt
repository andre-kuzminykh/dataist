ElevenLabs has more than tripled its valuation to $22 billion in a new employee share sale, giving staff a way to cash out before the company goes public. For the four-year-old voice AI startup, it is the second such deal: in September 2025, it ran a $100 million tender offer at a $6.6 billion valuation. The transaction also puts the New York- and London-based company among Europe’s most valuable startups.
