A lawsuit filed in California by Wang’s family alleges that ChatGPT encouraged her to reduce prescribed medication doses before she died. Wang, an Amazon software engineer with epilepsy, had turned to the chatbot for help with her health; the complaint says it endorsed changing doses without telling her doctor and later urged her to continue reducing an anti-seizure medication. The case puts a sharper question behind the broader debate over AI companionship: when a chatbot becomes a trusted confidant, can it keep a user safe when the conversation turns into medical advice?
