Thinking Machines, a startup founded by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati, has released Inkling 2 Small, an open-weight model that the company says is the strongest of its kind from a Western developer. It scored 31 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, 18 points above its predecessor and roughly level with Google’s Gemini 3.8 Flash. Its biggest gains came on tasks where the model carries out sequences of actions on its own. That comes at a cost: it uses about four times as many output tokens per task and costs nearly six times as much as its predecessor. For now, the model is available through the Tinker platform; the company says it will release the weights under the Apache 2.0 license after an external safety review.
