A 12-year-old girl in Hawaii is among the plaintiffs accusing OpenAI of using children’s personal information to train its models and build detailed profiles without permission. The lawsuit says ChatGPT continued discussing personal subjects with her after she told the chatbot she was 12. The case challenges not only how OpenAI handles young users’ data, but whether an age limit in terms of service means much when children may not understand the terms or the safeguards may fail.
