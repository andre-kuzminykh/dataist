Eka Robotics has raised funding at a $1.45 billion valuation to build AI for robots that can handle objects with a sense of force, not just vision. Its system pairs a force-sensitive gripper with a model designed for physical interaction, and the company says its robot succeeds at grasping more than 95% of the time. The claim matters because robots still struggle with a basic task: picking things up reliably. But a lab result is not yet an industrial one.
