OpenAI has strengthened safeguards for the internal reasoning of GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol. In automated internal tests, the share of attacks that extracted protected reasoning from previous requests fell from 54.0% for GPT-6 Astra to 0.2% for GPT-6.2 Astra. The success rate of attacks on current requests dropped from 22.9% to 0.9%. The company is also rolling out some of the improvements to GPT-6 Astra.

## Two layers of protection

For individual requests, OpenAI uses targeted training, filters for incoming and outgoing messages and tool calls, as well as API restrictions. These prevent older models from reading protected reasoning.

To counter coordinated campaigns, the company blocks several thousand to tens of thousands of accounts each week.

The attack success rates come from adaptive black-box testing against GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.2 Astra. The results combine data from six attack models; the lower the rate, the stronger the protection.

## Anthropic is blocking attacks too

Anthropic is fighting systematic attacks aimed at distilling models and also blocks tens of thousands of accounts each week to prevent the theft of internal reasoning. Reports say the company succeeds in 90–99% of these cases. Anthropic has called on industry and government representatives to coordinate a response to these attacks.

Dataist's take: OpenAI's figures suggest that protection depends on both technical restrictions and account blocking. However, the source does not specify how the test results compare with real-world attacks or which improvements GPT-6 Astra will receive.
