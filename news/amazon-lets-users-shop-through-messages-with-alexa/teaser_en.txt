Amazon has launched a feature in the US that lets people shop through text messages with its AI agent Alexa. Selected users will be able to search for products using everyday language, add them to their cart and place orders, as well as compare offers, track prices and check delivery status. The feature follows Amazon’s decision in September to block Meta’s Muse agent from accessing its website. The company is starting the rollout ahead of the holiday season and plans to expand access in the coming weeks.
