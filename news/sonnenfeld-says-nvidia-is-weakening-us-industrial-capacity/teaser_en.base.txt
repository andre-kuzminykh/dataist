Yale professor Jeffrey Sonnenfeld believes Jensen Huang’s return as Nvidia CEO and to its board could expose the company and its executives to substantial legal costs. He says the board reinstated Huang under pressure from the Donald Trump administration without completing its investigation into his ties to Alice Huang, a suspected Chinese spy. Sonnenfeld also alleges that Nvidia’s advanced chips reach China in violation of export restrictions and help build models based on work from US labs. The company denies reports that its most advanced chips have been smuggled into China.
