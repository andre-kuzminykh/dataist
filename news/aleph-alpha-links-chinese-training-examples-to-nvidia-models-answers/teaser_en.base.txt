A German AI company says political framing can travel through model training data, not just through a model’s own policies. Aleph Alpha found that Nvidia’s Nemotron Cascade 2 echoed China’s official position in 17% of its answers, and links the pattern to a small set of examples generated by Chinese models. The result matters beyond China: governments and businesses are competing to deploy AI systems, while the values embedded in their training data may travel with them.
