A shakeout among AI cloud providers would not be a sideshow for Nvidia. The company’s exposure runs through customers building data centers, buying its chips and, in some cases, relying on its help to secure power, financing and customers. After a week of falling shares and a withdrawn IPO filing, industry executives expect consolidation. Two executives in data centers and cloud computing told The Information that only seven to ten of today’s hundreds of providers may remain in a few years.
