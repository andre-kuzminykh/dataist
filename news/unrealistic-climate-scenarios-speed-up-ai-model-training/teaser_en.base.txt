Researchers at MIT, ETH Zurich and Imperial College London have developed a way to train AI climate models faster and with less data. Instead of many realistic scenarios, the method uses synthetic data with abrupt changes — for example, a sudden spike in CO₂ emissions in 2050 or a drop in methane levels in 2037. The team says a single climate simulation run is now enough for training. The models also make more reliable estimates under unfamiliar conditions and of the effects of local environmental policies.
