Airbnb plans to launch its own AI travel agent in the first half of 2027. It will adapt the interface to each user’s request, showing maps, photos, comparisons, calendars and itineraries rather than relying on chat alone. The agent will help users not only choose a trip but also handle issues while traveling, such as changing a booking, resolving a payment problem or requesting a refund. The company also reported that third-quarter revenue rose 19% to $4.87 billion.
