Nvidia has released Nemotron 3 Diarization, a free 100-million-parameter model that identifies up to eight speakers in real time. Its strongest benchmark result comes with a trade-off: users can choose an audio buffer as short as 0.32 seconds, but shorter buffers generally reduce accuracy. The model leads VoiceArena’s Diarization-Bench with a 14.72% error rate, while its predecessor comparison points to a less demanding setting: at a 1.04-second buffer, it cuts errors by an average of 41% across eight test scenarios.
