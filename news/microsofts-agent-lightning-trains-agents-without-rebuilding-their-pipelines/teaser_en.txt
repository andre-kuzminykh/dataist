Microsoft Research Asia has released Agent Lightning v1.0, an open-source framework for training AI agents with reinforcement learning while keeping their existing pipelines intact. The framework is about 3,500 lines of code and puts a model proxy between the agent and the training system, rather than requiring developers to rebuild the agent inside a training framework. In a coding-agent example, training Qwen3.5-9B on about 6,000 samples raised its SWE-bench Verified Pass@1 score from 41.8% to 56.4%.
