Anthropic says habits from the era of earlier models are keeping Claude Opus 5.5 from performing at its full potential. The company recommends removing requests to “think hard” or “think carefully” from prompts, starting at the Medium effort level, and stating the goal and desired output format upfront. Opus 5.5 is now the default in the Pro, Max, Ultra and Team plans; in Anthropic’s internal tests, at Medium it matched Opus 5 running at High.
