EA Sports will create a digital version of John Motson’s voice for the football simulator FC27. The late BBC commentator’s family gave permission to use recordings of his broadcasts. For almost a decade, from 1996 to 2006, he commentated on games in the series, then called Fifa. Motson’s voice, known to fans as “Motty,” will appear in the game after a software update. The company reportedly paid his family, but did not disclose the amount.
