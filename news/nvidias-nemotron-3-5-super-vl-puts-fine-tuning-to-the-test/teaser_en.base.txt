NVIDIA has released Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, an open 120-billion-parameter model built to handle documents, images, charts, tables and web pages alongside language tasks. It is part of a three-model release: Super VL targets multimodal work, while two smaller Bolt models are aimed at local AI agents. The distinction matters because NVIDIA is pitching not just a model, but a way to adapt and run specialized systems on company data and infrastructure.
