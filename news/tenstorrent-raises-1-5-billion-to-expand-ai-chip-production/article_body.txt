## Who is backing the expansion

The round was led by Trace Capital, a new firm founded by Quiet Capital co-founder Ben Mahdavi, and Innovation X. Other investors include:

- Cadence Ventures
- Eclipse
- Fidelity
- SanDisk
- T. Rowe Price

Keller said Tenstorrent chose independence and sought more capital after demonstrating that Galaxy servers could run DeepSeek models. The company plans to use the funding to increase chip output for open-weight AI models and video generation.

## The production question

The raise puts Tenstorrent in a crowded effort to take share from Nvidia. Earlier this month, TechCrunch reported that AI chipmaker Etched had received investment offers at a valuation of $40 billion or more. That figure is far above Tenstorrent’s $6.2 billion valuation, though the two companies’ fundraising situations are not directly comparable.

I think the more consequential detail is not the valuation but the gap between funding and output: Tenstorrent has named the workloads it wants to serve, but not how many chips it expects to ship or when. Without that, the raise signals ambition more clearly than it shows how quickly the company can turn demand into production.
