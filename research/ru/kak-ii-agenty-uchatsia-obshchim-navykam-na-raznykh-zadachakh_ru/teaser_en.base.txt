An AI agent may face a dozen different-looking tasks that all depend on the same underlying skill.

The authors introduce a framework that lets AI agents learn those shared skills across tasks, rather than treating every new setting as a separate problem. It creates simple, testable simulated worlds for practice, then checks whether the learned abilities carry over to the original tasks, including real-world ones. As the system improves, it also builds reusable skill modules and chooses which combination to use for each task.

In this review we look at how the framework creates and tests environments, helps agents build skills that transfer, and selects the right modules for unfamiliar challenges.
