OpenAI has introduced a set of measures to combat child sexual abuse material created or distributed with the help of AI. They include an image classifier that selected companies will be able to access for free through an API, warnings and temporary suspensions in ChatGPT, guidance for developers, and tools for law enforcement. In early tests, the classifier found as many images containing abuse as previous methods while flagging 48% fewer images; flagged images were twice as likely to contain abuse.
