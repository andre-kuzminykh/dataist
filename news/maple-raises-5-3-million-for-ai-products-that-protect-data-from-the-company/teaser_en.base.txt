Maple has raised $5.3 million to build AI products designed to protect user data even from the company itself. The funding will support, among other things, a cloud-based AI agent that can keep working while a user’s computer is off. Maple already offers a chatbot and Maple Work, a desktop app that can control a computer, work with files and applications, and carry out tasks in a browser. Data is processed in a secure hardware environment, and the startup plans to begin corporate pilot projects next year.
