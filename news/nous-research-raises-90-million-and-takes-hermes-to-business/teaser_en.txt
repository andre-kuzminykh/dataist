Nous Research raised $90 million at a $1.5 billion valuation and launched Hermes for Businesses, a product for companies to deploy AI agents tailored to their work. The move takes Hermes beyond its developer and consumer following: Nous says the agent has been copied more than 24 million times and accounts for about 2.5% of global AI token usage. The company had about $36 million in annualized revenue by mid-September 2026 and expects to exceed $100 million before year-end.
