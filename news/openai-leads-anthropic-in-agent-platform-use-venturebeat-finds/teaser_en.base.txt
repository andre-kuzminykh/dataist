VentureBeat’s survey found that 43% of companies using platforms to manage AI agents named OpenAI, compared with 22% for Anthropic and 21% for Google. The gap matters, but it is not a simple market-share tally: respondents could name multiple platforms, and a company can pay one vendor for models while running agents on another’s platform. The same survey suggests the harder question is not which platform companies have chosen, but how costly it is to change what they have already put into production.
