Blackstone’s AI infrastructure bet is built on a simple premise: the GPUs and data centers behind the boom are not just expensive assets, but collateral for lending. Jas Khaira, who leads the firm’s AI hub in San Francisco, says concerns about the sector’s growing debt are overstated. The argument matters because Blackstone is both financing AI infrastructure and investing in companies that depend on it, including newer cloud providers whose borrowing has come under scrutiny.
