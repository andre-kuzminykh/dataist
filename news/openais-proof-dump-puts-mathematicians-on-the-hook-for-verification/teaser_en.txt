A dispute over more than 700 AI-generated proof files has turned into a boycott call. The Association for Mathematical Help (AHM) says OpenAI ignored basic norms of research and urges mathematicians to stop collaborating with the company. The argument is not only about whether the proofs are correct: it is about who gets to set mathematical questions, who has to make machine-produced results usable, and whether a field can absorb discoveries faster than its researchers can understand them.
