Serve Robotics, a maker of autonomous delivery robots, is launching a Physical AI Services business to sell connectivity and data tools to other robot developers. One customer is Bedrock Robotics, which is building autonomous control systems for construction equipment. Serve is also working with T-Mobile to bring AI computing closer to robots. The company will continue developing its own delivery services and hospital robots, while aiming to supply technology for robots operating on farms, in warehouses and in other challenging environments.
