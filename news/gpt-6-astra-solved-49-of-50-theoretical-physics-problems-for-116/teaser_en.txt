GPT-6-Astra solved 49 of 50 theoretical physics problems in TheoPhysEval, a new benchmark built from unpublished research tasks with checkable answers. The result came through OpenAI’s Codex, at an average cost of less than $3 per problem and just under $116 for the full test. The benchmark’s harder question is not whether an agent can carry out a calculation, but what that ability means when choosing the calculation is still part of doing science.
