Belgian authorities ordered Google to halt construction of a data center being built without permits. In southern Belgium, the company was building the facility without approval for a modified design. Regional official Raphaël Stockis said the building was nearly complete by the time of the inspection, with its frame already finished. Three hundred construction workers received an oral order to stop work, which Google later confirmed in writing. The dispute concerns a plan to replace air cooling with liquid cooling and combine two data centers into one. A decision on Google’s appeal is expected on January 7.
