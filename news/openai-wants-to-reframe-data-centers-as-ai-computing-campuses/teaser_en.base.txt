OpenAI is preparing a campaign to shape the US debate over AI after Tuesday’s midterm elections, including a proposal to call its data centers “AI computing campuses.” The effort comes as Democrats are projected to retake the House, and possibly the Senate. Chris Lehane, OpenAI’s head of global affairs, told Axios that the midterms would be a “trailer” for 2028, when he expects AI to become a central issue in the presidential campaign.
