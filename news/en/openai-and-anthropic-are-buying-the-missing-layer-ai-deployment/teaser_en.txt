The most valuable AI engineers may no longer be the people who build models. OpenAI and Anthropic are assembling businesses around the harder final mile: placing engineers inside customer organizations, connecting models to existing systems, and turning demonstrations into working applications. OpenAI’s planned acquisition of Tomoro, Anthropic-linked Ode’s acquisition of Fractional AI and Casper Studios, and the expansion of deployment teams across the industry all point to the same commercial problem: companies can buy access to powerful models long before they know what to do with them.
