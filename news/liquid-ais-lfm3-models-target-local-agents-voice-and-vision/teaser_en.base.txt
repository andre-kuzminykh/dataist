Liquid AI has released the LFM3 family, a set of compact models for text, vision, audio and structured decisions that can run locally or through its cloud API. The launch is aimed at repeatable business tasks, from extracting fields from documents to handling browser workflows and voice commands. Its pitch is not that a small model can do everything: it is that a model tuned to a narrow job may reduce reliance on remote AI services, if its accuracy, deployment costs and license terms hold up in practice.
