Deel has introduced two specialized language models, Pangaea K1 for employment rules and workplace procedures, and Pangaea P1 for payroll questions. The company says smaller models trained for these tasks can answer faster and more cheaply than general-purpose systems. K1 scored 70 on Deel’s own benchmark, close to its top result of 75, with a median response time of 7.7 seconds. The launch also brings Emet, a business that prepares expert training material and evaluates AI systems. Together, the products give Deel a way to build its own assistant while selling the expertise behind it to other AI developers.
