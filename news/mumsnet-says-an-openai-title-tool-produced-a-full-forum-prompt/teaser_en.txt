Mumsnet says it does not use AI to write forum posts, after a prompt asking an AI to draft a whole response appeared beneath a user’s request for advice. The site’s explanation changed as users challenged it: founder Justine Roberts eventually said the prompt came from Mumsnet’s system for suggesting thread titles, which sends drafts to OpenAI. The incident landed on a forum whose users value advice from other parents—and whose audience and post count have both fallen.
