Decrypt Ads has identified about 2,100 domains that appear to be part of a network of AI-generated recipe sites, many likely run by the same operators. The sites pose as personal cooking blogs, publish invented recipes and promote them through search and social media to earn advertising revenue. One uses an AI image resembling actor Emily Blunt as its fictional baker. The report’s larger concern is not bad recipes alone: it says deceptive publishers can mislead readers, waste advertisers’ money and expose users’ browsing data to ad partners in Russia and China.
