Abliteration AI removes safeguards from open models and gives users access to them. Its founder, Devon Thomas, believes people should set their own rules for how AI works. Supporters see this approach as a way to use models more freely, including to find vulnerabilities, while security experts warn of the risk of abuse. The technique for removing safeguards is called abliteration: more than 12,000 such models are tagged on Hugging Face, and the seven most popular ones were downloaded more than a million times in the past month.
