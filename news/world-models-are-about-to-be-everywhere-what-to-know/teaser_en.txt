In 2016, Pokémon Go players scanned the world around them while hunting virtual Pokémon. Data from those recordings is now helping develop world models: AI systems that build digital environments and learn to predict how objects change within them. Niantic, the company behind Pokémon Go, sold the game and shifted its focus to this technology. World models could become an important part of the development of home robots and self-driving cars. A robot, for example, could practice tasks in a virtual copy of a room, while a car could learn from simulated hazardous situations.
