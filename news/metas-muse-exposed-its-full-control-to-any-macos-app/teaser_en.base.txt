Meta’s Muse was built to act across a user’s apps, accounts and Mac, but a zero-day flaw gave any application or terminal command a path to take control of the assistant. Security researcher Patrick Wardle showed that an attacker could redirect Muse’s speech-decryption server, capture an authentication token and use the agent’s permissions to write malicious files or take photos, often without a warning. Meta released a fix more than 12 hours after Ars Technica published the findings.
