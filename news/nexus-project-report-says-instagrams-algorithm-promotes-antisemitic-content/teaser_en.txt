According to a report by the research group Nexus Project, antisemitic posts have become more common on Instagram since the platform scaled back moderation, and many are AI-generated. The authors say the platform’s recommendation algorithms show this material to users who engage with antisemitic conspiracy theories and propaganda. In a Nexus Project test, a new account received a feed made up almost entirely of antisemitic content in less than an hour; more than half of the posts came from verified accounts.
