Symantec says attackers used a layered prompt to turn language models into operators for cyberattacks, pairing them with 74 attack modules and instructions to run for up to 100,000 steps without asking for confirmation. The company’s internet-facing decoy captured the prompt in summer 2026, but did not execute its tools. The episode matters less as proof that commercial models can be reliably hijacked—the analysis does not establish that—than as a glimpse of an attack design built to keep searching for exposed models, credentials and new targets.
