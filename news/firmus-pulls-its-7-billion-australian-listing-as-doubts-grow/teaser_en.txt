Firmus has withdrawn a planned listing on the Australian Securities Exchange after investors questioned the data-center company’s valuation and revenue forecasts. The offering was expected to raise $7 billion at $11 a share, ahead of an October 23 debut, and would have been Australia’s largest new listing since Telstra. The retreat leaves Firmus looking to private markets to fund its liquid-cooled AI data-center buildout in Australia and elsewhere in Asia.
