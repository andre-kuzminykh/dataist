SemiAnalysis has introduced GDPx, a benchmark that tests AI agents on 206 work tasks using professional software and simulated work environments. Some tasks allow up to eight hours to complete. Claude Opus 5.5 posted the top score at 12%; Gemini 4 Carbon scored 10.9%, and GPT-6 Astra scored 9.6%. The methodology has drawn criticism over closed tasks, the effects of safety filters and differences in the conditions agents work under.
