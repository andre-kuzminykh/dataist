Rabbit has introduced OS3, a cross-platform operating system for AI agents that can be used through a browser, Telegram or iMessage. It is also coming to the Rabbit R1, but no new hardware is required. The move reverses Rabbit’s original premise: instead of asking people to buy a dedicated AI device, the company is putting its agent system on computers and phones they already own. For a company that once struggled to make its gadget useful, OS3 is both a broader bet and an implicit admission that software is now the better route.
