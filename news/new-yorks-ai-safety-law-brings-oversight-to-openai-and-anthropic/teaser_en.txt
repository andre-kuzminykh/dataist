New York has opened a registration portal for major developers of advanced AI models. OpenAI, Anthropic and other companies with annual revenue above $500 million must register by January 1, 2027. The RAISE Act, which took effect earlier this year, requires them to publish safety policies and model reports, and to report critical incidents within 72 hours. Companies that violate the law face fines of up to $1 million for a first offense and up to $3 million for subsequent offenses.
