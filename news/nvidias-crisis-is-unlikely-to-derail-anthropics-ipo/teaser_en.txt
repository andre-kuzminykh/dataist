Anthropic is preparing for an IPO, and Jensen Huang’s departure as Nvidia’s CEO has raised questions about whether the chipmaker’s crisis could hurt the startup’s valuation. Nvidia planned to invest $10 million in Anthropic as part of the offering, and Anthropic relies heavily on its GPUs. But the company has other sources of computing capacity, and investor interest in the IPO reportedly exceeds the shares on offer by about six times: 825 million shares are expected to be sold at $70–78, which could value Anthropic at up to $1.405 trillion.
