A video circulating online appears to show a Chinese operation converting thousands of Nvidia RTX 5090s into server cards for AI. The cards’ original circuit boards are replaced and their cooling systems changed to blower-style fans, making them easier to fit densely into AI server racks. The footage offers a glimpse of how demand for AI hardware may be diverting scarce consumer GPUs into computing infrastructure — though the video alone does not establish where the cards end up or how many are involved.
