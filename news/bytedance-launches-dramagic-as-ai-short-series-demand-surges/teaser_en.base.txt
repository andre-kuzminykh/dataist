ByteDance has launched Dramagic, an AI platform designed to take short series from script to screen. The move places the company inside China’s rapidly expanding short-series market: around 128,000 projects were released in the first quarter of 2026, three times as many as during the entire previous year, and 95% were created with AI. With AI-generated video estimated at $90–120 per minute—about one-tenth the cost of traditional production—the launch is as much about production economics as software.
