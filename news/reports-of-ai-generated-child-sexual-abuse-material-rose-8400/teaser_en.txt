The number of reports involving AI-generated child sexual abuse material rose from about 4,700 in 2023 to more than 400,000 in 2025 — an increase of over 8,400% in two years. The figures come from Childlight, the Global Child Safety Institute in Edinburgh. The material may include deepfakes made with apps that digitally remove clothing from photos. Although the images are fake, they are used to blackmail children for money or new images. The researchers urge governments to recognise the sexual exploitation of children as a preventable public health emergency.
