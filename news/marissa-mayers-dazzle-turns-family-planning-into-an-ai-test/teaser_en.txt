Marissa Mayer asked her AI assistant Dazzle to help identify what makes her 10-year-old daughter Marielle special. The task came from a teacher: name three qualities. Mayer, the former Yahoo chief executive, said she struggled to choose from the many possible answers, so Dazzle suggested that Marielle is compassionate, creative and loves animals. The anecdote offers a more intimate view of Mayer’s startup than a product demo: Dazzle is meant to take on family planning and other repetitive work, but its usefulness depends on how well it reads the person asking.
