Wells Fargo will introduce updated terms on Tuesday, making the use of any AI agent the customer’s own responsibility for millions of clients. Banks fear that third-party bots, given users’ login details and permission to act on their behalf, could lead to mistaken purchases or transfers, or create security problems. JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Citibank and PNC have issued similar warnings. At the same time, the banks are developing their own AI tools.
