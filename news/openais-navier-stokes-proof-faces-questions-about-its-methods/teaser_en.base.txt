OpenAI says it has a formal proof related to the Navier–Stokes problem, but mathematicians are disputing what the work establishes and how it was produced. The proof is written in Lean, which checks each logical step without making the argument easy for people to understand. The Clay Mathematics Institute has been reviewing it since publication. The dispute reaches beyond the proof itself: OpenAI’s account of a large agent swarm leaves unclear how much the agents contributed to the result.
