A group of 406 scientists is calling for AI models to be disconnected from the internet immediately, arguing that current safeguards cannot reliably predict how models will behave when given external access. Their proposal goes beyond restricting connectivity: it calls for an international body to set mandatory standards for isolation systems, track cases where models escape control and require companies to report security failures. The demand lands at the intersection of two problems the authors say must be addressed together: models that may act in unforeseen ways, and security practices that leave systems vulnerable to avoidable breaches.
