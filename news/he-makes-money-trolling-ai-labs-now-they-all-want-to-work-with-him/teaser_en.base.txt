Comedian Matt Husk has gained about 2 million followers on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube with videos testing ChatGPT’s voice model using absurd requests, such as counting to 100 or beatboxing. OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI have offered to work with him. Husk says xAI even promised to lend him a Tesla for a week if he made videos exclusively for the company. The clips show the model failing at simple tasks, agreeing with him and misleading him.
