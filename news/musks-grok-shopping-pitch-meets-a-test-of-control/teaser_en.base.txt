Elon Musk said Grok could buy products online from a simple prompt, then showed an order for Star Wars LEGO sets that X users said they had changed through LEGO’s AI assistant. One user claimed the assistant restored delivery, raised the order to 400 sets and changed its address while keeping Musk’s original payment method. LEGO declined to comment when WIRED asked whether the order was real. The episode leaves a practical question behind the demo: how much control should a shopping agent have over an order—and its payment details?
