Nvidia’s Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe combines transcription, translation and speaker identification in one open model. It handles speech in 40 languages, translates 31 languages to and from English, and identifies speakers in 13 languages. The model can process recordings up to an hour long with as many as eight participants. Nvidia is pitching it as a single tool for tasks that often require separate systems, though some of its headline accuracy claims come from benchmarks the company cites.
