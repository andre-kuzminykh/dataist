Tokyo court has recognized an actor’s voice as part of his right of publicity, finding that unauthorized use of a performer’s voice can violate that right. The case, brought against TikTok by voice actor Kenjiro Tsuda, concerned AI-generated videos using a voice he said was unmistakably his. The judge declined to order the videos removed because the account owner had taken them down after the lawsuit was filed, but the ruling gives Japan’s performers a legal basis to challenge commercial voice imitation.
