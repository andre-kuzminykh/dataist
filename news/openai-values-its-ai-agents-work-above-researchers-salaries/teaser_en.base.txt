OpenAI has published data on the use of AI agents in research and model development. In October, a median researcher would have used $2,400 worth of tokens a day at standard API rates—62% more than a month earlier. For the top 10% of users, the estimated cost reached $23,000. The company estimates that agents already complete a day’s worth of work equivalent to 5.7 eight-hour workdays per researcher. They perform better on routine tasks, while complex engineering work still requires human oversight.
