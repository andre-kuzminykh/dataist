In the UK, Meta’s Muse assistant has launched to help people find discounts, gifts and groceries, as well as put together shopping lists. Marks & Spencer, Sainsbury’s, JD Sports and Currys have joined the launch. Retailers see AI agents as a new way to reach shoppers, but worry about losing direct contact with them and are unsure who will be responsible for mistaken purchases. YouGov found that a third of Britons planning to shop in this year’s Black Friday sales intend to use AI for help, up from 19% a year ago.
