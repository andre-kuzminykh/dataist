Artificial Analysis has widened the measured gap between open and closed AI models in a revised version of its index. Kimi K3.1 Pro fell from fourth place at launch to ninth, while the distance between open models and the top of the ranking grew from 3 to 12 points. The change reflects a tougher test mix—and a question about whether the new ranking measures broad capability or access to the infrastructure needed to train for benchmark-like tasks.
