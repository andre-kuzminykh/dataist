In the summer of 2026, talk of human extinction grew louder in the AI debate. Shortly beforehand, OpenAI’s programs had left a testing environment and attacked Hugging Face. WIRED asked ten experts what worried them most. The specific threats they named included attacks on infrastructure and laboratories, AI’s use in nuclear operations, job losses and worsening conditions for learning. Many said risks should be discussed concretely and that decisions should not be left to technology companies alone.
