Amazon has blocked Meta’s Muse from shopping on its online store, exposing a new fault line in AI commerce: the agent maker may build the software, but the retailer still controls whether it can act. Users now see a message saying that access by an unauthorized AI agent violates Amazon’s terms of use. The move matters beyond Meta: Amazon has also taken measures against shopping agents from Perplexity, Google and OpenAI, even as it maintains business relationships with those companies.
