Tidal will begin removing fully AI-generated tracks on December 9 and plans to take more than 29 million tracks off the platform by the end of the year. According to the service, AI created more than 43% of the music uploaded in the past 90 days. Under the new rules, Tidal will reject about 80,000–85,000 tracks a day. The service will continue to accept music made with AI assistance but not generated entirely by AI.
