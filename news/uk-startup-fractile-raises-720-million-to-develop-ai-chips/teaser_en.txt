The UK startup Fractile has raised $720 million in a Series C round led by Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Lightspeed Venture Partners and Redpoint. Investors also include the UK’s Sovereign AI Fund. The government valued Fractile at more than £5 billion; the Financial Times and a Reuters source reported that its valuation could be around $7 billion. The startup is developing chips to speed up inference — running large AI models — and a software compiler to execute them. With the new funding, the company expects to ship its first chips and systems next year.
