# ElevenLabs launches an app for meeting notes and dictation

## Tools for teams

In recent months, Fireflies, Wispr Flow and Granola have released similar features. ElevenAI combines capabilities already found in note-taking and dictation apps: it records meeting conversations and notifies participants. Users can ask questions about information from different meetings, and get suggestions during a chat—for example, a reminder of a detail from an earlier conversation.

Companies can connect ElevenAgent and assign it follow-up tasks. For example, based on meeting notes, the agent can draft an email.

Dictation turns speech into edited text that can be pasted into any app. It supports more than 90 languages. ElevenLabs says ElevenAI can also recognize specialized terms from specific industries, both in dictation and in meeting transcripts.

## A bet on the enterprise market

ElevenLabs is using the app to showcase the capabilities of its own model and connect its agent platforms for more accurate processing of context. The company may be aiming less to compete in the personal productivity app market than to offer teams an alternative to buying licenses for separate note-taking services.

Meeting-tool providers are trying to turn their products into companies’ central knowledge repositories. They are also developing ways to automatically extract information from conversations and carry out follow-up tasks.

## Other ElevenLabs launches

Over the past week, the company has introduced other products and features:

- A series of speech-to-text models, Scribe v3, with real-time transcription;
- A tool that lets companies fine-tune models on their own data;
- A tool for choosing the right language model for a given task;
- A workflow builder that automates tasks based on transcription.

Dataist’s take: ElevenAI connects conversation transcription with search and follow-up actions in a single app for teams. The service is still in preview and available by sign-up, so the scale of its adoption by companies remains unclear.
