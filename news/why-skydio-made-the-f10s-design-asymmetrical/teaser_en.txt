Skydio has introduced the F10 Lightrunner, a fixed-wing drone with a camera mounted on the wingtip and an asymmetrical layout for its wings and motors. It is designed for autonomous launch, landing and high-speed flight: it can reach 100 miles per hour and stay airborne for up to two hours. The asymmetry helps keep the wing from blocking the camera while the drone tracks a target, and the MegaDock system can launch, catch and charge up to five of these drones.
