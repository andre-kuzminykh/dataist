Yelp is adding an AI agent that can call a restaurant and ask how long the wait is. Its new Check for Me feature will appear on restaurant pages in the Yelp app: users enter their party size and phone number, then receive the restaurant’s estimate in a message. Yelp is also expanding its AI assistant and launching a $30-a-month tool for businesses, extending its AI push from helping diners choose where to go to handling customer inquiries.
