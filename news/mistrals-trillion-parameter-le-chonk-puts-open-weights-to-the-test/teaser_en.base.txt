Mistral has introduced Large 4, a text-output model with one trillion parameters that the company says can compete with leading open-weight systems. It is also a product of a joke: ML4’s internal nickname is Le Chonk, a nod to the fictional Le Chaton Fat model that circulated online in June. Mistral plans to release ML4’s weights after three weeks, putting the announcement’s central claim to a practical test: whether a large, open model can help companies keep control of the systems built around it.
