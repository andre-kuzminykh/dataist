Agile Deliberation is a way to build image classifiers for subjective categories whose boundaries depend on context and people’s views. Instead of asking users to define all the rules upfront, the system helps refine them through dialogue: it breaks a concept into subcategories, presents ambiguous images and adjusts the prompt based on human feedback. The method was tested in 18 user sessions lasting an hour and a half each. According to the authors, participants achieved more consistent labels and better results than with conventional approaches, while experiencing less fatigue and stress. The project’s code and algorithms are available on GitHub.
