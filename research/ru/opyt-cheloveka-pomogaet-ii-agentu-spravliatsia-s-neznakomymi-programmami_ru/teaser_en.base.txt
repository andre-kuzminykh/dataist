AI agents can click through unfamiliar software, but they still struggle when a task depends on knowing how people actually get work done.

The authors introduce Work2Skill, a way to turn human demonstrations into reusable guidance for AI agents. It records screen activity alongside mouse and keyboard actions, then helps an agent identify the workflow rules, the order of steps, and how to check that a task is complete. In tests, agents using these learned skills handled computer tasks more successfully than agents without them.

In this review we look at how Work2Skill captures human experience, turns it into practical instructions, and helps AI agents work with unfamiliar software.
