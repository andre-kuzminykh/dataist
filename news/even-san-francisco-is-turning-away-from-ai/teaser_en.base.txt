Even in San Francisco, the center of the AI boom, the technology is increasingly becoming a reason not to go on a date. According to The Verge, users of dating apps say in their profiles that they do not want to date people who use AI. Meanwhile, several cities around the Bay Area, including San Francisco, Oakland and Richmond, have introduced temporary moratoriums on data center construction. But there is another side to the story: one young startup founder tasked an AI agent with running his Hinge profile and arranging dates.
