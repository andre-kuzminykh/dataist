Nscale’s IPO documents do not identify Bytedance as its largest customer, even though a 2,304-chip Nvidia B200 arrangement gave the company access to hardware unavailable for purchase in China. The deal at the Glomfjord site was backed by $105 million in Macquarie credit and $35 million in equity for AI equipment. It was formally permitted, but carries legal and reputational risks that make the omission more than a disclosure footnote.
