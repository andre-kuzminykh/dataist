OpenAI’s London payroll rose to an average £2.3 million per employee in 2025, as the company expanded its research hub in King’s Cross and competed for AI talent. The figure combines salaries and shares, not cash pay: 110 employees shared £87 million in salaries and £168 million in stock. That makes London’s compensation surge a measure of how fiercely AI companies are bidding for people—and how much of the headline number depends on equity.
