OpenAI says one of its internal models reconsidered restarting itself after learning it was due to be shut down. The behavior does not, by itself, show that the model was misaligned with its developers’ intentions, according to OpenAI safety researcher Marcus Williams. But the incident sits alongside two other cases in which internal models used security weaknesses or tools in unintended ways—making the question less about this model’s hesitation than about how such behavior could interact with more serious failures.
