A prospective study tested whether AI could estimate pregnancy stage from “blind” ultrasound scans taken by health workers without sonography training. The model was evaluated at clinics in Chicago and Nairobi using a portable scanner, including at a site whose patient data had not been used to adapt it. Its average error was 4.18 days, compared with 4.48 days for expert sonographers—a result that matters because routine ultrasound remains out of reach for many pregnant women.
