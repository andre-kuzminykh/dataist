A book by journalist Paris Marx examines the impact of data centers and generative AI, from how these facilities work to their effects on surrounding communities. Marx links rising protests to the scale of construction and the strain on power grids and water resources. Data centers have become a prominent political issue: he says opposition to them may have influenced the results of recent US midterm elections. Resistance also extends to other physical manifestations of the tech industry, including Flock cameras, Meta smart glasses and Waymo self-driving cars.
