At this week’s All In conference, I moderated a panel on world models—an area with major backers, prominent founders and almost no clear product roadmaps. AMI Labs, founded by Yann LeCun, and World Labs, founded by Fei-Fei Li, have drawn significant attention and funding, yet neither has made a convincing case for where its technology will first earn money. That secrecy may reflect early-stage research. It may also be a deliberate strategy: in a field broad enough to cover robots, video, graphics and autonomous systems, revealing the destination could help competitors catch up.
