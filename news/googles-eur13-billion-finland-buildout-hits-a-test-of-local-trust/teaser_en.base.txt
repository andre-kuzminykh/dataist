Google’s €13 billion Finnish data-center expansion has run into a test of trust: regulators ordered work paused after the company cleared forest before completing required environmental assessments. In Muhos, about 250,000 trees were cut across 330 hectares; an ecologist estimates another 150,000 were felled at Otanmäki. The dispute now reaches beyond two sites, raising questions about who benefits from Finland’s AI infrastructure boom and who bears its costs.
