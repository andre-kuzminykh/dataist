Agility Robotics built Digit 5 for the shift, not the sprint. The company’s new humanoid is designed to work beside people for up to 20 hours a day, lift heavier loads and meet safety requirements that kept earlier Digit deployments behind physical barriers. Those changes make it Agility’s biggest, heaviest and strongest worker yet — and cost it the running, jumping and graceful movement that founder Jonathan Hurst once wanted the robot to embody.
