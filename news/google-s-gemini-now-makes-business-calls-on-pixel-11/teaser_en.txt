Google has introduced Call for Me, a beta feature that lets Gemini place calls to businesses from Pixel 11 smartphones. It is available only in the US, only in English, and only to Gemini subscribers enrolled in the open beta of Phone by Google. The feature revives an idea Google abandoned with Duplex: letting an assistant handle the tedious parts of a phone call. The difference now is that a large language model is expected to manage a less predictable conversation, not just book a restaurant table.
