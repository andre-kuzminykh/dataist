Amazon has introduced two new Bee devices for turning everyday conversations into notes, tasks and summaries. Bee Frontier starts at $74.99; Bee Eclipse costs $99.99. Both extend the same idea as the original Bee: capture useful details without asking users to take notes themselves. The pitch is familiar, but the pricing puts Eclipse below Plaud’s competing recorders, while Bee’s paid plan and Alexa integration make the devices part of a broader Amazon service.
