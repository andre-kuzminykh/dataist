U.S. banks are restricting AI agents that use customers’ credentials to access accounts, citing security risks and uncertainty over whether an agent is acting with the customer’s permission. Banks would prefer to let agents read account data without making transactions, but users can connect them through a browser, including with Computer Use or open-source software such as OpenClaw. That makes access difficult to block at the bank’s end. OpenAI has already launched a separate ChatGPT service for financial tasks in the U.S.; it can review financial data but cannot make transactions.
