Greek Prime Minister Kyriakos Mitsotakis used a San Francisco appearance to pitch Greece as a recovering economy and a practical base for technology companies. He pointed to digital infrastructure, easier conditions for startups and a plan to bring back specialists who left during the debt crisis. But the sharper message was less promotional: when the conversation turned to AI, Mitsotakis admitted that governments are not prepared for the speed of change, and that even a planned ban on social media for children may already be aimed at yesterday’s problem.
