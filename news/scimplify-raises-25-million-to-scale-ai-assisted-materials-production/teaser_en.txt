Scimplify has raised $25 million in an all-equity round to expand its AI-assisted production of critical materials and build a larger presence in the US and Japan. The company says it has raised $80 million in equity funding overall. A person familiar with the deal put its post-money valuation at $250 million, up from $150 million after its $40 million Series B in March 2025; Scimplify declined to disclose its valuation.
