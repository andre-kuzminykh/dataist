High-frequency trading firms are building AI infrastructure at a scale that would have looked out of place in finance just a few years ago. XTX Markets is building a data center in Finland, while Jane Street runs one in Texas with more than 4,000 Nvidia GPUs. The shift matters because these firms are not just buying access to computing: they are committing billions to providers and backing a chip startup, even as the cost and useful life of the hardware remain uncertain.
