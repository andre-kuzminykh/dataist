ElevenLabs has introduced ElevenAI, an experimental app for teams that combines meeting notes, dictation and work with AI agents. The service launched at the company’s 11/11 summit in New York; it is currently in preview, and users need to sign up for early access. ElevenAI can transcribe meetings, answer questions about past conversations, help participants in a chat in real time and draft emails based on notes. Dictation works in more than 90 languages. ElevenLabs is valued at $22 billion and is positioning the product as a solution for teams.
