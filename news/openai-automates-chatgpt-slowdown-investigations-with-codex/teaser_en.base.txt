OpenAI has built Performance Factory, an internal system based on Codex that investigates performance regressions and helps fix them. The company already uses it to keep ChatGPT fast. In just over three months, the system ran more than 1,700 investigations and identified the causes of more than 600 issues—an average of 17 investigations a day. It takes each issue from a slowdown alert through root-cause analysis and a proposed fix to verification, while leaving code review and the decision to deploy it to engineers.
