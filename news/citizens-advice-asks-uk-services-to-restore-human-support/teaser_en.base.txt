Citizens Advice is calling on UK service providers to give customers a guaranteed route to a human after finding that AI chatbots and online-only support are blocking access to essential help. The charity, which personally helped more than 2.7 million people in England and Wales last year, says at least 14 million people face digital exclusion. For them, a failed chatbot is not a minor inconvenience: it can delay corrections to bills, debt support and help with preventing homelessness.
