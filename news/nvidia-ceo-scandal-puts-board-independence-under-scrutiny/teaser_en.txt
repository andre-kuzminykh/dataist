Nvidia shares lost about 9.5% over two days, falling 5.47% on Tuesday after dropping 4% on Monday. The company had shared almost no news, but its board hired the law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to conduct an internal investigation after CEO Jensen Huang acknowledged an affair with Alice Huang, whom US intelligence agencies consider a Chinese spy. Corporate governance experts say there is no obvious precedent for a situation like this. The investigation must determine what Huang knew, whether Alice Huang received information from him, and whether any laws or Nvidia’s internal policies were violated.
