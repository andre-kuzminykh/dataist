Databricks has updated its Genie AI agent so it can independently handle multi-step tasks that require sustained work. It gets context from an ontology Databricks built using customer data stored on the company’s platform. Databricks CEO Ali Ghodsi showed how he uses Genie at work: the agent analyzes employee surveys, evaluates proposals from executives and reviews failed negotiations with prospective customers. The company says Genie can handle a wide range of tasks without supervision.
