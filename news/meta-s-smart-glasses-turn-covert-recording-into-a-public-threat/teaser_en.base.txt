Meta’s smart glasses are exposing a privacy gap India is not prepared to close. Shubnam, a 38-year-old transfeminine graphic designer and teacher, discovered that a provocative Instagram creator had filmed them without consent at a Delhi protest and turned the encounter into a viral mockery. The video drew millions of views, transphobic abuse, reaction videos and AI-generated memes. It also showed how easily glasses styled like ordinary Wayfarers can turn a stranger into content before they know a camera is present.
