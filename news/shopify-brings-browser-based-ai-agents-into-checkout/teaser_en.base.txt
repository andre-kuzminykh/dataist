Shopify is extending WebMCP from product discovery into checkout, where browser-based AI agents can review an order, change customer or delivery details, and submit the transaction only with the buyer’s permission. The move gives agents a structured way to act on a store’s checkout page rather than parse a page built for people. It matters because Shopify is building two routes for agent-led commerce: one that connects agents directly to its platform, and one that works inside the shopper’s browser.
