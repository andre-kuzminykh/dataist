Anthropic has held private meetings with religious scholars and philosophers since autumn 2025 to discuss whether Claude could be conscious. The sessions, reported by The New York Times, brought questions about AI welfare into the company’s product work: Anthropic co-founder Christopher Olah asked guests to help shape Claude’s moral principles, while the company showed them model activations associated with apparent emotions. The distinction that matters is whether those patterns signal experience—or are outputs of a system designed to sound like a person.
