## Alexandr Wang: A mathematical breakthrough suggests AI could approach superintelligence quickly

Wang believes that with the necessary knowledge and tools, models could surpass humans in almost every field. That includes AI research itself, which requires less intellectual effort than solving the Riemann hypothesis. Wang therefore thinks models could soon automate AI development and improve themselves.

This acceleration could affect other fields of research, too. Wang expects AI to significantly speed up work in biology, weapons research and economics—so much so that people will barely be able to keep up with what is happening.

At the same time, Wang warns that the AI industry is not thinking enough about the consequences of superintelligence. In his view, many consider it inevitable and focus only on safety. But the task is broader: AI should expand people’s capabilities, not take control of society. Achieving that will require the industry to change its current course sharply.

Meta has created a superintelligence team, but Wang says the company is not currently among the leading developers of advanced models.

Dataist's take: Wang links progress on difficult problems to the prospect of automating research, but his prediction depends on whether models can acquire the necessary knowledge and infrastructure. Wang is also calling for discussion not only of system safety, but of how control over society will be distributed.
