Carolyn Marvin, a 78-year-old retired professor of communications at the University of Pennsylvania, blocks Uber Eats robots to draw attention to their presence on city sidewalks. In Philadelphia, she has become a local celebrity: passersby support her protests, and some residents even dressed up as her and a robot for Halloween. A few weeks ago, Marvin was detained for interfering with a robot; she was issued a fine for disorderly conduct. She plans to challenge it in court.
