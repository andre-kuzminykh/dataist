The Internet Watch Foundation says it is finding more photorealistic child sexual abuse images made with AI, and that the volume of illegal material its analysts have examined this year has already surpassed the 2025 total. The latest figures cover still images, not video; most depicted girls and children aged seven to 13. The report puts pressure on a UK government that has not announced a separate AI bill, even as officials weigh new rules against a test of whether they make people safer.
