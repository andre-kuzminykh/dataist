## What Claude can do

Claude for Manufacturing helps prepare estimates based on real inventory data, compare suppliers, and create quality reports and design options. When equipment is idle, the system can analyze sensor readings and machine manuals.

With the additional Infor plugin, Claude can place orders with backup suppliers if deliveries are delayed and change production schedules. These actions require human approval. The results are linked to their source documents, so employees can check them before sending them to customers or suppliers.

## Limitations and work with equipment

The plugin is available to Team and Enterprise customers, but it does not yet control physical machines. Anthropic is separately developing the Model Hardware Standard, a research preview of a standard for connecting AI agents to devices—including robotic arms and microscopes—through unified drivers.

According to Anthropic, this approach could cut setup time from several weeks or months to a few hours or minutes. Human oversight remains necessary, however: AI still has a limited understanding of physical processes.

Dataist's take: Claude for Manufacturing automates work with industrial software, but does not remove employees’ responsibility for checking results and approving actions. Anthropic describes connections to physical equipment as a separate research project, not a feature of the new plugin.
