## The demand has to keep rising

For now, the equation works: cheaper tokens are associated with more demand for computing. But the relationship is a condition, not a guarantee. If AI use stops growing fast enough, lower prices would no longer be offset by greater consumption.

The consequences would reach beyond Nvidia:

- Chipmakers and memory suppliers could face weaker demand.
- Energy companies and cloud services could also be exposed.

## Markets are watching the chain

US stocks fell after reports that OpenAI’s annual revenue run rate may be lower than previously reported. The reaction suggests that markets are already sensitive to signs that AI growth may not match expectations.

I think the important question is not whether token prices fall, but whether usage expands quickly enough to keep the infrastructure behind AI in short supply. Nvidia’s upside depends on that second part holding.
