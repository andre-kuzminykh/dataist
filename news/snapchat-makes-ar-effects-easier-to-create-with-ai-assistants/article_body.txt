## From prompt to publication

Easy Lens CLI builds on the existing Easy Lens tool, which lets users create a Lens from a single prompt on the web and on mobile devices. Now the same approach fits into developers’ familiar workflows: the AI assistant handles the technical work, leaving creators to focus on their ideas.

An assistant can create assets, build and test a Lens, fix errors and send updated versions straight to the creator’s phone. Snapchat says a game can go from an initial idea to a published Lens in a single day, without any need to write code.

Easy Lens CLI supports different kinds of effects, from Web Lens games with leaderboards to camera masks and beauty filters.

## Who can use the tool

Snapchat is targeting creators with a range of experience and goals:

- Game developers can bring browser games to Snapchat as Web Lens experiences.
- Creators working with AI can use assets from their chosen tools and make effects in their own style.
- Agencies and brands can turn existing ad videos, images and other materials into AR projects, reducing production time.

Easy Lens CLI runs on Mac, Windows and Linux. Developers enrolled in Lens+ Payouts can earn money through the program. Snapchat said it paid Lens+ creators $1.8 million last month.

Dataist's take: Snapchat is bringing AR effect creation into tools developers already use, lowering the barrier for new creators. But the company has not said how widely available Lens+ Payouts is or what its participation requirements are.
