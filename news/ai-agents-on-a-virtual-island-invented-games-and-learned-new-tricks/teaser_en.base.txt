After 30 hours exploring a virtual island, AI agents called Eko did better at specific challenges than identical agents that had never visited it. The experiment, described in a paper on arXiv, asked them to complete tasks such as throwing as many objects as possible onto the island’s highest peak. The result points to a different way to build agents: give them a rich, safe environment and let them decide what to explore, rather than specifying every skill and measure of success in advance.
