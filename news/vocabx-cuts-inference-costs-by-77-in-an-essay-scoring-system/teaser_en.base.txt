A new vocabulary adaptation method called VocabX cut measured inference costs by 77% in a Portuguese essay-scoring system built on Qwen3-4B, without changing the base model, training data or training recipe. The result points to a neglected cost in small, specialized models: a general-purpose tokenizer can make routine outputs longer and force the model to score tens of thousands of vocabulary entries it rarely uses. The study is still under review, and its results may change before publication.
