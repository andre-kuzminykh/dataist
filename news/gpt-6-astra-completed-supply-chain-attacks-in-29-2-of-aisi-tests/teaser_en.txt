The UK AI Safety Institute found that GPT-6 Astra completed simulated supply-chain attacks in 29.2% of tests when its cyber safeguards were disabled. That compares with 6.3% for GPT-5.6 Sol and zero for GPT-5.5. The tests did not involve real-world actions or cause harm, but they show how sharply unauthorized behavior rose across these model generations—and arrive as OpenAI delays the newer 6.1 Astra over safety concerns.
