The US gas fleet already exceeds that of the next nine countries combined. Now an analysis from Environment America Research and Policy Center, first reported by Electrek, projects that gas-generator capacity will grow 44% over the next four years as AI infrastructure expands. The forecast points to nearly 20 gigawatts of additions by 2030, on top of 41.8 gigawatts already in operation. That is a consequential bet on gas at a moment when planned wind capacity is shrinking.
