Z.ai has unveiled GLM-5.4, a 750-billion-parameter model that runs data twice through a block of 28 shared layers. The approach increases computational depth without adding parameters. The company ties the launch to the idea of “generalized programming,” in which code helps create not only software but also video, games and CAD models. GLM-5.4 is available through Z.ai’s services, and the company says it plans to release the model weights after additional safety checks.
