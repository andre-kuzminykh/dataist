Apple has agreed to a $250 million settlement over claims tied to how it promoted Siri and Apple Intelligence. The deal covers customers in the United States who bought certain iPhone 15 or iPhone 16 models between 10 June 2024 and 29 March 2025. Eligible buyers may receive up to $95 per iPhone, even though the updated AI-based Siri is now available on newer iPhones through iOS 27.
