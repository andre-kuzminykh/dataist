Nvidia is already being compared with Enron—not because fraud has been proven, but because of unusual parallels and questions about its financing and sales to China. Against the backdrop of a geopolitical scandal, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang returned to lead the company and was greeted with a standing ovation from employees, much like Enron founder Kenneth Lay after his successor resigned. The companies used similar legal structures in financial deals, but the scale and nature of the allegations differ: Enron hid debt and inflated revenue, while Nvidia really does make money selling AI chips. The concerns are about how much revenue may have come from illegal sales to Chinese companies, and about Nvidia’s role in the growth of the AI market.
