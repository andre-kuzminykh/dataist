Tasmania’s Supreme Court has overturned a parole condition that barred Susan Neill-Fraser from speaking to the media about her murder conviction after the state’s parole board relied on a document containing court cases invented by AI. Neill-Fraser has spent 17 years denying that she killed Bob Chappell, whose body was never found. The episode is not only a failure of legal fact-checking: an AI-generated error helped restrict the speech of a person still trying to challenge a disputed conviction.
