A widely cited estimate says 95% of AI pilots fail to deliver the expected return or results, at least by the measures available. At The Next Endeavor in Mountain View, executives from GAI Insights, Jellyfish, Kloudfuse and Nasik discussed what it takes to turn a pilot into something a company can run in production. Their answers pointed less to a shortage of AI tools than to the work around them: changing roles and processes, measuring results and deciding who is responsible when systems fail.
