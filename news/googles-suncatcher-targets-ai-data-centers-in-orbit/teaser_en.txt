Google is pursuing Suncatcher, a project aimed at putting AI data centers in orbit and powering them with solar energy. The idea joins work by SpaceX and Blue Origin, but it remains far from an obvious business: matching one 1 GW terrestrial data center would require about 10,000 satellites, while cooling, radiation and launch costs all remain unresolved. That makes Suncatcher less a routine infrastructure expansion than a test of whether AI’s demand for power can justify moving computation off Earth.
