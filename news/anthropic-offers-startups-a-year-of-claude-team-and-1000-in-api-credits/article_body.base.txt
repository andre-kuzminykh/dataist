## Who can apply

Applications are open through the Claude for Startups program page. Claude Team is Anthropic’s paid plan for teams.

Anthropic says supporting companies that build on its models is part of its mission, alongside working with startup founders and developers.

## The bet behind the offer

I think the more interesting question is what startups can build with a year of team access and $1,000 in API credits once the offer ends. The announcement makes the support concrete, but says nothing about what happens to participating companies after those benefits run out.
