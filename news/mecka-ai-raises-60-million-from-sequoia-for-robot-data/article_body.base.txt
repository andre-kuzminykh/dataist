## A market taking shape

Mecka is not alone in targeting real-world data for robotics. XDOF, another startup in the space, was previously reported by TechCrunch to be in talks for a Series B at a $1.2 billion valuation.

Companies that built data-collection platforms for language models are also moving into robotics. Scale AI and Micro1 are among them.

## The harder question is what the recordings teach

I think the interesting test is not whether Mecka can recruit people to film routine tasks, but whether those recordings give robots useful training data. The announcement describes how the data is captured; it does not say how much has been collected, which robot systems will use it, or whether the footage has already improved their performance.

That gap matters. A person’s video of making coffee may show what happened, but the announcement offers no detail on how Mecka turns recordings into data a robot can learn from. The $60 million signals investor confidence in the category; it does not yet show that the data pipeline works at scale.
