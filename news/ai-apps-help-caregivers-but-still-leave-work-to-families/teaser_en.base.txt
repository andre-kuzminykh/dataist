Apps for people caring for loved ones collect information about medications, symptoms, doctor visits and everyday tasks. Some already use AI: Ari, for example, helps track a patient’s condition and prepare for appointments. AARP estimates that in 2025, about 63 million Americans cared for adults or children with illnesses or disabilities, spending an average of up to 27 hours a week on care. Digital tools can make coordination easier, but some of the work — entering information and keeping it up to date — still falls to caregivers.
