A former Meta employee has urged Andy Burnham to broaden restrictions on non-disclosure agreements (NDAs). Former Meta director of global public policy Sarah Wynn-Williams says these contracts prevent tech workers from speaking out about the risks of AI development. Burnham previously announced plans to change the rules so victims of crime and workplace harassment can speak to people of their choice, even if they have signed an NDA. Wynn-Williams wants the changes to cover existing agreements and, ultimately, to ban NDAs.
