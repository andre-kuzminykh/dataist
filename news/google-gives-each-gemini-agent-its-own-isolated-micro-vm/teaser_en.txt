Google has built Agent Compute Engine, giving each Gemini agent its own hardware-isolated micro-virtual machine. The design also underpins Agent Substrate, Google’s open-source runtime based on Kubernetes. Google says it can fit ten times as many agents on a server; in a test with 200,000 agents, pausing and resuming work took less than 500 milliseconds. The architecture matters because it makes isolation and fast recovery part of the infrastructure for running agents, not just properties of the model.
