Nous Research fine-tuned Alibaba’s open Qwen3.8-27B model to remove Chinese state propaganda from its answers. The new model, Arete-27B, was trained on 53 million tokens: the share of answers containing propaganda fell from 22% to 0.9%, while results on internal tests of general capabilities barely changed. Nous argues that the political bias in Chinese open models is a superficial layer that fine-tuning can remove. The company released Arete-27B’s weights, test set and training data on Hugging Face under the Apache 2.0 license.
