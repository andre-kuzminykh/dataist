In VentureBeat’s pilot test, Claude Fable 5.2 fixed code errors in 10 of 15 attempts, while Claude Opus 5.5 did so in 9 of 15. The team paid about $89 for Fable’s API calls and about $29 for Opus’s: the difference in results was just one successful attempt, while Fable’s costs were roughly three times higher. Neither model solved the hardest task. The test measured the cost of getting a working fix in real code, rather than simply comparing token prices.
