Huawei is preparing to launch its Ascend 960DT AI chip early next year, saying it will be twice as powerful as its current chips. But the company cannot get enough high-bandwidth memory, or HBM, from Chinese suppliers, while US export controls block direct purchases from Samsung and SK Hynix, which together account for more than 80% of the global market. According to The Information, Huawei is asking customers to source the memory themselves—a workaround that pushes procurement toward a costly, unreliable black market.
