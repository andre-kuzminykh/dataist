PayPal says the accuracy of its internal data assistant rose from 43% to 95% after the company added its own business knowledge without changing the underlying large language model. Ren Zhang, PayPal’s vice president and head of global AI products, explained that the assistant needs more than verified data: it also needs definitions, query templates and employees’ analytical procedures. The company aims to keep this knowledge separate from models so it can carry it over when models are updated. In parallel, PayPal is setting rules for AI agents: when to consult verified sources and when to seek human approval.
