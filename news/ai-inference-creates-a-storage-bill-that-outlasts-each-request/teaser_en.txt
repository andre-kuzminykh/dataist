AI inference has a storage bill that keeps growing after each request is finished. In WD’s internal measurements, an eight-second 720p video delivered to a user as a 3.97 MB file leaves more than 500 MB of associated data on the server, including logs, cached context, intermediate versions, backups and security records. That is 130 times the delivered file’s size. As inference scales, the cost of keeping its by-products may become a larger infrastructure problem than many AI budgets anticipate.
