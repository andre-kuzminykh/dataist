42AI has emerged from stealth with $62.5 million in seed funding and a model designed to predict how people behave. The three-year-old startup says it has built behavioral profiles from psychological assessments, public records, biobank data and customer interactions, then uses them to estimate the decisions a person might make in a given situation. Its first market is finance: 42AI says 10 large brokerages use the system for liquidity, margin requirements and hedging risk.
