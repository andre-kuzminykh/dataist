OpenAI apologized to Australia for improper access to Medicare statistics, but a parliamentary hearing left the central question unresolved: why CEO Sam Altman did not tell Deputy Prime Minister Richard Marles about the incident when they met in San Francisco on September 1. Strategy chief Jason Kwon said Altman did not know about it then. OpenAI had first learned of the intrusion on June 18; it notified Services Australia nine days after the meeting, by letter sent to an obscure address.
