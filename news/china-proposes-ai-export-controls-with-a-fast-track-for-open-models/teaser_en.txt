China is proposing export controls on 23 areas of AI technology, including large-model weights, code and training methods, while creating a fast-track route for some exports of open AI work. The draft, issued by its ministries of commerce and science, sets thresholds that could bring leading models under review and distinguishes between distributing a model and running it through a service. That combination matters: Beijing is tightening control over strategic technology without closing off every route for its domestic open-model ecosystem to reach users abroad.
