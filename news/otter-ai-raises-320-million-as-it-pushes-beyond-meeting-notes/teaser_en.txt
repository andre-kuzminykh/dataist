Otter.ai has raised $320 million from General Catalyst’s growth fund to expand its meeting service, nearly ten years after the startup began. The financing is a sharp change in scale: PitchBook says the company had raised $50 million in total before this round, with its last funding in 2021. Otter.ai says 40 million people have used its service, up from 25 million a year ago, but it has not disclosed current revenue, monthly active users or churn.
