## Anthropic heads to the stock market after the largest IPO in history

Ninety minutes before trading began, market participants were expecting the customary jump in a newly public company’s share price: investors who had not taken part in the offering could try to buy shares once trading started. Anthropic shares rose 20% on the over-the-counter “grey market” that morning.

Chris Beauchamp, chief market analyst at investment and trading platform IG, said client interest in Anthropic shares in the premarket had risen noticeably after Nvidia’s results. In his assessment, sentiment was also affected by the controversy around Jensen dying down. At the start of the week, IG clients expected the stock to easily top $100 when trading began; by the time he commented, shares were already trading at $105.

At $105 per share, Anthropic’s market value would be $1.9 trillion — slightly below the $2 trillion figure cited when IPO plans were widely discussed. Beauchamp also said markets needed confirmation that interest in AI companies remained strong. In his view, Anthropic’s offering should provide that signal.
