Google has begun a global rollout of a collapsible sidebar for AI Mode on the desktop search homepage. It helps users return to recent queries and continue searching: from the sidebar, they can reopen past conversations, start new ones, create notebooks and go to settings. Google Search vice president Robby Stein announced the update on X. The company is also expanding temporary AI Mode conversations worldwide. They do not use previous context, are not saved in history and do not affect future recommendations.
