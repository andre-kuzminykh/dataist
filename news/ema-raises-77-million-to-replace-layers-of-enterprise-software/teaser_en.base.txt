Ema has raised $77 million in all-primary funding to automate enterprise work with systems that coordinate multiple AI agents across a company’s existing applications. Founded in 2023 by former Google and Coinbase executive Surojit Chatterjee and former Okta executive Suvik Sen, the Mountain View startup is targeting budgets traditionally split between enterprise software and IT services. Its larger claim is that AI will first sit on top of SaaS products, then make some of them—and some of the human work around them—less necessary.
