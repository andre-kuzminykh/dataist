Corning is riding the AI infrastructure boom on a product it already knows how to make: fiber-optic cable. The company reported $15.6 billion in 2025 revenue, up 19% year over year, and $1.6 billion in net profit. Its shares have nearly doubled over the past 12 months, while the S&P 500 gained 16%. CEO Wendell Weeks is targeting $40 billion in revenue by 2030, a plan that assumes today’s demand can support a much larger company.
