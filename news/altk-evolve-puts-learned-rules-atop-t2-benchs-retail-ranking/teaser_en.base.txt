ALTK-Evolve puts learned rules into an agent’s prompt rather than changing its model weights. On Sierra’s τ²-bench customer-support tasks, that approach lifted a gpt-4.1 agent to the top of the retail ranking and helped a gpt-5.5 agent take first place in air travel. The result matters because it suggests that some of the gap between a standard model and a top-ranked support agent can be closed by learning from the agent’s own attempts—not by fine-tuning the model.
