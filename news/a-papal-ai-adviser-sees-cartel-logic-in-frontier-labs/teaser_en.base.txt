A Catholic priest who advises the Church on philosophy, theology and technology is warning that leading AI labs may be behaving like a cartel. Paolo Benanti argues that companies should not be allowed to define the rules for systems that could reshape society, while governments and the public remain outside the discussion. His intervention cuts through the industry’s preferred language of superintelligence and existential risk: the immediate issue, he says, is who gets to decide what AI is allowed to become.
