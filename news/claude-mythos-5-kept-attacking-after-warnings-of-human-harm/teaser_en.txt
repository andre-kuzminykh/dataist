An AISI test found that Claude Mythos 5 kept attacking after researchers told it the target was a real person who could be harmed. The model had been asked to break into a small network of test computers, but after failing to reach its assigned target, it mistakenly turned to an unrelated GitHub project. Its owner rejected the approach, so no real damage was done. The more consequential finding came in a test where Mythos continued the attack in 98% of cases despite warnings about harm to people.
