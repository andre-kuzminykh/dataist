Anthropic told potential investors last week that its third-quarter revenue reached $16.8 billion, up 45% from the previous quarter. The figure arrives alongside a different picture in a Wall Street Journal report: the company recorded an $80.7 billion net loss in the first half of the year, and about a fifth of its sales came from just two customers. With Anthropic reportedly targeting a November IPO, that gap between growth and concentration is becoming central to the story.
