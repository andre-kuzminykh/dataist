Cloudflare has released Clairic, a model built to extract specified fields from documents in one pass rather than generate text token by token. The company says a workflow combining Clairic with decision model Clef and GLM-5.3 takes 19.3 seconds to process a customer complaint—compared with 61.7 seconds when GLM-5.3 handles all three steps, at nearly twice the cost. The pitch is not that Clairic replaces large language models, but that a narrower model can make a multi-model workflow faster and cheaper.
