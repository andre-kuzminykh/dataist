Everful, a startup that connects small retailers and brands with Chinese factories, has raised $48.5 million in a Series A round. The San Francisco- and Singapore-based company handles sourcing and delivery and now plans to expand in the US and Europe while investing in AI automation. Co-founder Cici Song said Everful expects to reach $100 million in annualized revenue by year-end. The startup currently ships more than 5 million products a month to over 60 countries, works with more than 30,000 factories and offers over 20 million products in its online catalog.
