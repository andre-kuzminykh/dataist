Alan Turing is remembered for a test of whether a machine can pass for human, but his wartime work focused on breaking Enigma. Now two cryptanalysts say they have used OpenAI’s Astra and Anthropic’s Claude Opus 5 to read two Enigma messages that had resisted previous attempts. One model searched archives, built an Enigma simulator and recovered a message researchers had struggled with since 2005. The other used a known clue: an officer’s name.
