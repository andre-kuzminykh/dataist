In American stores, AI is used to schedule shifts, monitor workers, hire staff and track shoppers. A Walmart cashier in Oregon said she receives 10–15 safety alerts per shift; until she deals with each one, the app will not let her continue working. Unions and worker advocates are calling for limits on AI use and for its terms to be set in collective bargaining agreements. A National Retail Federation survey published in December 2025 found that companies were already adopting AI across different areas and planned to increase investment.
