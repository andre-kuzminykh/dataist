AlphaGo’s move 37 was not a flash of intuition. The program found it by combining a neural network that suggested promising moves with a search process that tested their consequences. That distinction matters now: today’s language models can produce convincing explanations, but they still generate them by predicting one token after another. I think the gap is not a matter of making that process longer. It is whether a system can keep track of what it knows, test what follows and revise its beliefs when evidence changes.
