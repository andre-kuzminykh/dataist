Burnham will speak at the United Nations in New York before his first meeting with Trump, with artificial intelligence high on the agenda. The UK says it wants to use AI “for good,” lower the cost of living and make the country safer. It will also announce a new partnership with the US on AI and autonomous systems, aimed at protecting critical infrastructure from sea lanes to airspace. But Britain’s public position arrives just after it declined to support Finland’s call for mandatory pre-release testing of frontier AI models.
