## What police found

Emergency services called police to the scene. Officers found the teenager and his companion a block away, with the robot in a car. Police say the teenager had put it there.

A photo released by police shows the robot wearing a white tank top, a gold chain and black moustache.

Police also seized two mobile phones belonging to the suspects. Investigators are trying to establish whether someone intentionally directed the device during the incident.

## The unanswered question

The reported livestream makes the phones potentially important evidence, but the police statement does not say whether footage exists or what it shows. I think that is the key distinction in this case: a robot’s presence does not establish who, if anyone, controlled its movements.

Police also warned in a subsequent Facebook post that some online comments about the case “could cross a line and, in the worst case, constitute a criminal offence.” That warning is a separate part of the story from the investigation into the incident itself.
