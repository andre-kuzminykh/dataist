Discovered Materials, a San Francisco startup in Y Combinator’s spring 2026 cohort, has launched a contest for AI models, offering a $1 million prize pool for solutions to five open problems in materials science. The startup uses AI to find new materials for advanced chips. Before launching the contest, its organizers tested whether leading models could solve the problems autonomously: agents worked for several days with access to the internet and other resources, but made no notable progress.
