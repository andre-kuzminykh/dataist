Anthropic says it moved Claude to AMD GPUs in just three days, with its Mythos model writing or adapting almost all the compute kernels for the new platform itself. This kind of work usually takes six to twelve months. The company also says it trains and runs Claude across four major chip families, combining them into a shared pool of computing resources. If the figures hold up, AI could make it easier to switch providers and weaken Nvidia’s advantage, which is tied to its CUDA ecosystem.
