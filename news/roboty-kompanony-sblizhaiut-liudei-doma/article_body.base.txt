# Companion Robots Help People Stay Closer in Modern Homes

The development of multimodal AI and robotics is making companion robots more personal and attentive to people’s emotional states. The OlloNi SS1 home companion robot from Ollobot is designed for use in an ordinary home: it can greet family members when they return, escort an elderly person from the living room to the kitchen, remind a child to take a break from studying, or notice that someone is unusually quiet and gently ask whether everything is all right.
