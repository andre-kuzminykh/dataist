Max Justicz, CTO and co-founder of startup Charge Robotics, used an OpenAI model to find a serious vulnerability in code related to Ubuntu. If exploited, it could have allowed hackers to take over servers running the popular Linux-based operating system. Justicz found the bug in two hours using GPT 5.6 Sol through the Daybreak Blue program. OpenAI is now offering vulnerability researchers a new model, ChatGPT Cyber, but the program still has only thousands of participants, and defenders may not have enough resources.
