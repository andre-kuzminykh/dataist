British photographer Rankin, whose real name is John Rankin Waddell, has published Contact, a book of contact sheets featuring celebrities photographed between 1987 and 2006. After working with generative AI, he returned to photography made by people and says that exploring the new technology helped him understand the value of the exchange between photographer and subject. Rankin also believes AI will change photography and damage the industry: he predicts that in less than a year it will begin displacing professionals in retouching, hair, makeup and styling.
