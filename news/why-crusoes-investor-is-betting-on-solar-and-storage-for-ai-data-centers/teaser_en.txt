G2 Venture Partners partner Brook Porter believes future AI data centers should be built around solar power and long-duration energy storage. Global natural gas generation capacity is growing by roughly 60–70 GW a year, while solar power is already adding more than 1 TW. G2VP estimates that investment in data centers will exceed $4 trillion by the end of the decade. Solar farms and storage systems could help developers build large facilities independently of the grid and bring new capacity online faster.
