California has imposed new limits on how employers can use AI, barring them from making firing decisions entirely through AI, assessing workers’ emotional states, or collecting neural data. The laws, signed by Gov. Gavin Newsom on Thursday, also require notice when AI causes layoffs and ban AI surveillance in restrooms. The package puts California among the first states to adopt broad workplace AI protections, as workers fear the technology could cost them jobs, deepen discrimination and expand surveillance.
