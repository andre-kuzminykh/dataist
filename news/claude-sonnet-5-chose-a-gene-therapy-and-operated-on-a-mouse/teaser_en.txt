A Claude Sonnet 5 agent selected a gene therapy, chose where to inject it in a mouse’s brain and carried out the procedure using a motorized surgical rig. Researchers gave it a mouse epilepsy model, 19 possible therapies and one objective: reduce seizures. A licensed veterinarian approved every irreversible step and could stop the machine, but the agent made the treatment and targeting decisions itself. The experiment’s most revealing moment came when it refused to proceed.
