A 15-person team from ITESO, the Jesuit University of Guadalajara, has built RoboMeshA, a portable autonomous platform for teaching robotics and AI in Mexican classrooms. The project targets schools in Guadalajara where motivated teachers and talented students often lack access to robotics labs and other advanced tools. Instead of asking schools to build computer rooms or install software, RoboMeshA brings a small, browser-accessible learning network to them.
