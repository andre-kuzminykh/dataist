Hugging Face presented Microduck — a compact open-source duck-shaped robot. The 25-centimeter-tall device costs $399 and is expected to reach buyers by Christmas. The robot can waddle, lift objects weighing up to 800 grams with its beak, get up after falling, squat and roller-skate. It can be taught new actions using reinforcement learning: developers have access to a development kit, simulation and a full set of tools for training on GitHub.
