A three-month randomized trial across 11 US intellectual-property law firms found that Google Labs’ unreleased InFlow assistant helped patent lawyers produce stronger documents, especially those with less experience. But when lawyers later had to edit a patent draft without AI, junior lawyers did not retain a comparable advantage. The result puts a practical tension at the center of AI adoption: tools can raise the quality of work now without ensuring that the people using them build the judgment to do it unaided.
