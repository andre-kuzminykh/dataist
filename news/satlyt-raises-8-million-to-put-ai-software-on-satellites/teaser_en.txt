Satlyt raised $8 million to run AI software on satellites, betting that orbital computing needs a shared software layer as well as more powerful spacecraft. Founded by Rama Afullo, a former Google cloud employee who briefly worked at SpaceX’s Starlink in 2024, the Sunnyvale and Nairobi startup does not plan to build satellites. Its software has already flown twice, and a new mission this week will test it on a spacecraft built by India’s TakeMe2Space.
