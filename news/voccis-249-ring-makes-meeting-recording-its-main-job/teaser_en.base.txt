Vocci has released a ring built primarily to record meetings, adding a less conspicuous form factor to a market that already includes pendants, badges and card-shaped devices. The $249 Vocci ring weighs less than 6 grams, records for up to eight hours on a charge, and turns conversations into transcripts and AI-generated summaries. That makes the hardware convenient, but its discreet appearance also puts privacy at the center of the product.
