## What Microsoft says Maia 300 will deliver

Microsoft says Maia 300 will produce 40% more tokens per megawatt than leading GPU systems. A rack containing 96 Maia 300 chips, the company says, should deliver up to seven times as many tokens as a Maia 200 rack.

The details are in a technical report. Microsoft says it will present more material at Ignite 2026 in November.

## The claim that needs context

Tokens per megawatt is a useful measure of throughput against power use, but it does not tell us how Maia 300 compares on price, availability or performance across the workloads Microsoft names. I think the headline figures are compelling, but without those details they are not enough to judge whether the chips will be a practical alternative to leading GPU systems.

The other notable gap is what “several gigawatts” means in deployment terms. The announcement gives a target for Microsoft’s own Maia capacity by 2027, not a breakdown of how much will be Maia 300 or where that capacity will be used. Until the technical report and further details arrive, the scale is clearer than the economics.
