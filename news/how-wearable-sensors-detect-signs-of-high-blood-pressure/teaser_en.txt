Google Research has introduced an AI system that uses sensors in ordinary smartwatches to passively detect signs of high blood pressure, without cuff calibration. The AI-PPG-ACC-HTN system analyzes several days of signals and, in a study of 196 participants, produced results comparable to traditional measurements taken as part of a clinical assessment. The work was published in Nature; its findings also helped inform a blood pressure trend-tracking feature in the Google Health app.
