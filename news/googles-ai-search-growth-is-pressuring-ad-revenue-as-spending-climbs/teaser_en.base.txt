Google’s search business is showing the cost of shifting queries toward AI: search revenue fell short of expectations as AI-mode queries grew faster but generated less money than traditional search. On an investor call, Google executive Philipp Schindler said the lower monetization of those queries had affected results. The company says it is pleased with overall momentum, and search queries grew at their fastest annual rate in five years. But the gap between usage and revenue is becoming harder to ignore as Google expands its AI products and spending.
