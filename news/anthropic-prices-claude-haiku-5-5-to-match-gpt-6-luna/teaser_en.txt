Anthropic has released Claude Haiku 5.5 with API prices 90% below Haiku 4.5 for shorter requests, putting it at the same low-end rates as OpenAI’s GPT-6 Luna. The launch is aimed less at replacing larger models than at making a small one cheap enough to call often: Anthropic positions Haiku as a helper for Opus and Sonnet, while reserving harder coding work for those larger systems. That makes the pricing threshold—and what happens to real task costs beyond it—central to the pitch.
