What if an AI agent could do some of its thinking without putting every thought into words?

The authors introduce a system that lets an agent reason in a compact internal format, then switch to plain text when it needs to use tools or communicate with the outside world. This approach aims to keep the agent’s thought process inspectable while making its work faster and less costly.

In this review we look at how the system blends silent reasoning with tool use, and what its results suggest about building more capable AI agents.
