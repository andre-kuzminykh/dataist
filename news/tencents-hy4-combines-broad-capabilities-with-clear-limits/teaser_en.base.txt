Tencent has released Hy4, a language model that can analyze images and work with software independently. The company calls it one of the strongest open models, but has not yet published its weights. Hy4 is available through Tencent Cloud, OpenRouter and Tencent apps, including the Yuanbao chatbot. It scored 35 on the Artificial Analysis index, placing second among models from Chinese labs. But it trails leading commercial models on difficult math and programming tasks, and often makes up answers on factual accuracy tests when it cannot search the web.
