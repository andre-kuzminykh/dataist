Global stock markets are falling despite Anthropic’s record IPO: investors continue to sell shares in AI-related companies. Asian markets are heading for their worst week since 2020, while exchanges in Europe and the US are also declining. On Wall Street, the main indexes fell by more than 2% on Thursday. After a sharp rise, Anthropic shares reversed course and fell 6% on Friday to $91.30, approaching their $83 IPO price.
