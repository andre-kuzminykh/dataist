Last week, Bernie Sanders and Steve Bannon appeared together before a Washington group opposing the expansion of AI and the construction of data centers. The pairing was unusual, but the politics were increasingly familiar: both attacked the corporate oligarchs behind the industry. AI is becoming a visible symbol of a broader bargain that many Americans believe is failing them—corporations accumulate wealth, workers lose ground, and politicians appear willing to protect the people financing them. That makes AI more than a technology issue. It is becoming a test of which party can claim the language of economic anger.
