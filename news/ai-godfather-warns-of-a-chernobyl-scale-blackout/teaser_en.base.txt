Geoffrey Hinton, the Nobel Prize laureate and one of AI’s pioneers, told The Guardian that an AI-powered attack could disable the energy system and cause a large-scale blackout as soon as this year or next. In his scenario, the fallout could affect the global financial system or power grids and force world leaders to take the technology’s dangers more seriously. Hinton clarified that he was not necessarily predicting a nuclear accident: the comparison with Chernobyl refers to the scale of the potential disaster.
