17 scientists from the natural, applied and social sciences told Ai2 researchers which tasks can be delegated to AI agents and which risks need to be considered. They discussed checking results, protecting data, the effect of automation on research skills and responsibility for conclusions. The authors propose evaluating agents against the rules of specific disciplines and involving scientists in designing those evaluations. The study is preliminary: it consists of interviews with scientists in the US, not a representative survey or an evaluation of specific systems.
