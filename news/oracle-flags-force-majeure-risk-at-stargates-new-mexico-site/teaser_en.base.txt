Oracle has sent a force majeure notice concerning the Stargate data-center site in New Mexico, creating a contractual path to defer payments if the project misses its planned 2028 launch. The notice does not mean Oracle is leaving Project Jupiter: the company says it expects no delay and remains committed to New Mexico. But the filing puts the project’s unresolved power and permitting problems into a financial frame, at a time when AI infrastructure builders are taking on extraordinary costs to secure capacity for customers such as OpenAI.
