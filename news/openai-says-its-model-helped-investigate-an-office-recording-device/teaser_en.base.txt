OpenAI says it used one of its models to investigate a “sophisticated recording device” found in an office, identify the employee who had installed it days earlier, and help authorities make an arrest. The employee was fired and arrested. The company says it has found no sign so far that he improperly accessed or removed model weights, source code or customer data. The incident adds a new test of OpenAI’s security claims: the company has offered few details about how its model helped or what the device was recording.
