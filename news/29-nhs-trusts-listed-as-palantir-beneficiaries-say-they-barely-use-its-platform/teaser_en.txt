The gap is between NHS England’s list of trusts benefiting from Palantir’s health data platform and what some of those trusts say they actually use. A Guardian investigation, based on more than 100 freedom of information requests, found that 29 trusts listed as beneficiaries have not deployed the system or are making little use of it. The discrepancy matters because NHS England has cited the platform’s reported gains to defend its 2024 contract with Palantir, while campaigners and MPs are urging the government to end the deal.
