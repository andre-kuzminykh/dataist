OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra has become a target for Chinese AI labs DeepSeek and Moonshot AI, according to The Information. Sources familiar with the matter said the labs used Astra’s answers to train their latest models, including Kimi K3.1 Pro and V4.1-Flash 1017. The reported shift follows Anthropic’s claim that new safeguards cut by 90–99% the data attackers can extract from Claude, which had previously been the main target.
