Google DeepMind is speeding up Gemini 4 while its new leader moves the lab away from AGI as its defining mission. Koray Kavukcuoglu said the model is in early post-training and that he hopes to release it “much earlier” than the end of the year. The timing matters: Google’s promised Gemini 3.5 Pro never arrived, while Anthropic and OpenAI have moved ahead with newer model families. The leadership change therefore looks like more than a change of pace. DeepMind, once organized around long-term research, is being run increasingly as Google’s product engine.
