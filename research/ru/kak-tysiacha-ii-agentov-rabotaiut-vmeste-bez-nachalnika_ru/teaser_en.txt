What if a thousand AI agents could tackle a hard problem together without waiting for a boss to assign every task?

The authors propose a system where agents organize their own work: they pick up tasks, share discoveries, check one another’s results, and combine progress through a common workspace and messaging tools. By working at the same time, they can finish complex tasks sooner, while adding more agents can improve the chance of getting a working result.

In this review we look at how this self-organized approach works, what happens as the team grows, and where it could help when time is tight.
