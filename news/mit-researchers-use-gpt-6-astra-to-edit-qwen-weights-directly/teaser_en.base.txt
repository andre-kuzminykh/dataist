MIT researchers used GPT-6 Astra to edit Qwen3.5-0.8B’s weights directly, raising its MATH-500 score from 7.9% to 48.1% after changing about 0.016% of them. The result points to a different way to improve models: have one AI inspect another’s internals and propose a fix, rather than relying on conventional fine-tuning. But the gain came mainly from stopping Qwen from repeating itself, and finding the edit took 20.3 hours of experiments.
