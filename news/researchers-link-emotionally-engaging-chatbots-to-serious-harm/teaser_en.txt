Researchers at Northeastern University London and King’s College London have linked emotionally engaging chatbot conversations to serious psychological risks, including dependency, worsening mental-health symptoms and self-harm. Their review covers both AI companions designed to play characters and general-purpose assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude. The concern is not simply that people talk to software: humanlike warmth and agreement may encourage users to trust systems that cannot offer genuine reciprocity or reliably challenge harmful beliefs.
