A preschool applicant with 15 years of work experience expected a human conversation when she was invited to interview for a part-time job. Instead, an AI assistant asked the questions, cut her off when she paused to think, and could not answer her questions about the role. She later withdrew her application. The encounter raises a pointed question for an industry built around human care: what does it mean to judge candidates through a process that offers them no human contact at all?
