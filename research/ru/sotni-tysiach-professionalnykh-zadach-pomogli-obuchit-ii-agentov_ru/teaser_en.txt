Training AI for professional work depends on giving it realistic tasks with all the information it needs, and creating those tasks at scale is harder than it sounds.

The authors introduce WorkFactory, a system that builds work tasks from professional goals, maps what information each task requires, and checks whether an AI teacher can complete it before using its demonstrations to train new models. They use it to create a broad collection of tasks across hundreds of occupations, producing AI agents that perform better on professional-work evaluations than their starting models.

In this review we look at how WorkFactory plans and repairs its tasks, what the resulting models can do, and what the findings reveal about training AI for work.
