Meta has put three different bets on the face at once: a $1,300 VR glasses device, a $349 Ray-Ban model with no cameras, and a more capable Gen 3 camera platform starting at $449. The VR Glasses are meant to make Quest computing less like wearing a helmet, while the Audio model responds to privacy criticism by removing imaging altogether. The lineup shows Meta narrowing the gap between virtual displays and ordinary eyewear, but also exposing how much of the experience still depends on compromises in immersion, battery life and price.
