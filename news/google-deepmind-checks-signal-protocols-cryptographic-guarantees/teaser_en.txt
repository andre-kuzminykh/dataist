Google DeepMind researchers used a new framework, AlphaProof Code, to formally verify several key cryptographic guarantees of the Signal protocol. The work took four weeks and uncovered no new vulnerabilities in the parts of the protocol they examined. But when they tested older versions of Signal, the system detected known vulnerabilities that developers had fixed long ago. The analysis did not cover the entire Signal mobile app. The researchers published their specification and proof to support further work on formal software verification.
