Palantir’s revenue rose 96% year over year in the third quarter to $2.31 billion, but its shares edged down on Tuesday. Investors were unsettled by signs of a slowdown in corporate sales: US companies signed contracts worth a total of $1.46 billion, down from a record $2.13 billion in the previous quarter. At the same time, US corporate revenue surpassed revenue from US government customers for the first time. Management raised its full-year forecast and expects revenue from US commercial customers to exceed $3.559 billion, representing growth of at least 143%.
