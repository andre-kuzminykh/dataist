CATL and Tencent are backing a growing funding round for Deepseek as the Chinese AI startup prepares to reorganize for a planned IPO in early 2027. The company is building a data center that will house at least 160,000 Huawei chips, while founder Liang Wenfeng wants to keep developing open models with freely available weights. The financing comes after Deepseek raised about $7.4 billion at a valuation above $50 billion in its first external round, completed in June.
