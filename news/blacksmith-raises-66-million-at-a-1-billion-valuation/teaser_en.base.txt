Blacksmith has raised $66 million in a Series C led by Sequoia, valuing the company at $1 billion. The funding comes just two months after its $45 million Series B, which valued it at $550 million, and a year after a $10 million Series A put its valuation at $60 million. Blacksmith builds continuous integration tools for assembling, testing, merging and validating code changes—a task its co-founder and CEO, Aditya Jayaprakash, says is becoming harder as AI writes more of the code.
