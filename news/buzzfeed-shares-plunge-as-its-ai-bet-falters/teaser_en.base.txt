BuzzFeed shares fell 38% in a single morning after the company reported weak third-quarter results, dropping to $0.57. At the start of the year, they had reached $2.09. Earnings per share came in at minus $0.22, 13 cents below analysts’ already-low expectations. BuzzFeed had previously reported a net loss of $57.3 million for 2025 and acknowledged “substantial doubt” about its ability to continue operating. Against that backdrop, former CEO Jonah Peretti’s bet on AI-generated content looks increasingly risky.
