A video can pass every code check and still look or sound unmistakably wrong.

The authors introduce a way for AI coding agents to inspect video drafts while they are being made, rather than waiting until the full render is finished. The system checks short previews and targeted details such as whether text is readable, scenes match the brief, objects behave as intended, and speech stays in sync with the visuals. By catching problems early and returning concise evidence to the agent, it helps avoid expensive rewrites and improves the finished video.

In this review we look at how the system examines a draft across images, sound, and timing, and how those checks help an AI agent make better edits as it works.
