More than 600 flights were delayed at Newark and Philadelphia as disruptions spread across the US East Coast. Hundreds more were delayed at four other airports, while dozens of aircraft waiting to depart could not take off. The disruption coincided with the Trump administration’s limited rollout of an AI system for air traffic control near Washington, but early reports do not connect the two events. The overlap matters because SMART is being introduced into a system that already appears difficult to isolate when something breaks.
