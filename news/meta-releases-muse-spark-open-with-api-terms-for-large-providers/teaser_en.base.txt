Meta has released Muse Spark Open, a model for coding and AI agents whose weights are available on Hugging Face. The company says it is optimized for Apple’s M5 Ultra and can run locally across two Mac Studio computers, each with 512 GB of unified memory. The weights are free to use, but commercial distribution through an API comes with conditions: providers earning more than $20 million a year need a separate agreement with Meta, and users must display the label “Built with Muse Spark Open.” The release puts Meta into a crowded open-weights contest with Qwen, GLM and Kimi.
