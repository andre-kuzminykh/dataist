The Pentagon has asked Congress for $30.3 million to build Polygraph+, a program intended to modernize federal lie-detection and credibility-assessment technology. The proposal would put AI alongside polygraph testing for screening potential employees and identifying insider threats. It arrives as the department, under Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, is using polygraphs more aggressively to look for sources of suspected leaks. The larger problem is that the Pentagon is considering scaling a method whose scientific record remains deeply unsettled across a workforce of 2.8 million people.
