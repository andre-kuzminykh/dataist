At 19, Cal AI founder Zayn Yadegari has raised $10 million for Persona, a personal AI assistant paired with a $179 wristband. The launch follows his departure from MyFitnessPal, which bought Cal AI in March for an undisclosed sum. Persona’s defining choice is to make the wristband user-activated rather than always listening—a distinction that puts privacy, not just convenience, at the center of its pitch.
