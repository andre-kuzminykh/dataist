Donald Trump and Xi Jinping have opened a White House summit that puts personal rapport beside a much harder agenda: a fragile trade truce, Taiwan, artificial intelligence, and China’s support for Iran and Russia’s war against Ukraine. Trump called his relationship with Xi “a truly great friendship,” while Xi answered with a warning about conflict and a call for continued dialogue. The meeting matters less as a promise of an immediate agreement than as a test of whether warmth between the leaders can contain their governments’ widening disputes.
