Meta says its Muse AI agent could not have read a user’s private messages without permission, but the denial has not settled the dispute. The user, Athen, said the agent read his messages with Full Disk Access turned off. Meta’s explanations describe several macOS permission steps that should prevent that access; they do not explain how Athen’s account arose. The gap matters for a company trying to build a consumer AI business while facing renewed scrutiny over how it handles user data.
