NVIDIA has released the open-source SOMA Retargeter v0.2, a tool that adapts human movements to the proportions and joints of humanoid robots. It takes animation on a SOMA skeleton, calculates robot poses, and saves trajectories as CSV files for training control systems. The release includes examples and configurations for five robots: Unitree G1, Unitree H2, Booster T1, AgiBot X2 Ultra, and AgiBot A3T3. Movements can come from motion-capture recordings, be reconstructed from video, or be generated from text and pose constraints.
