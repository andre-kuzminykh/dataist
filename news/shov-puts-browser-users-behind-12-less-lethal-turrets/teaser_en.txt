Shov has turned a browser tab into a firing position. The site lets users spend $5 to control one of 12 pneumatic turrets at a real shooting range, using specially made hardened-rubber projectiles against green targets. The setup is presented as a game, complete with 90-second rounds and a video-game-style announcer. But the interface also makes a more uncomfortable idea ordinary: remote access to a weapon platform that, in another setting, could carry a lethal rifle.
