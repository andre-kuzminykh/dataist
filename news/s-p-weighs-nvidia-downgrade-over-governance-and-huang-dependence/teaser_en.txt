S&P Global Ratings is considering lowering Nvidia’s credit rating from AA to AA-. The agency is concerned about weak oversight of management, the lack of a succession plan and the company’s dependence on its CEO, Jensen Huang, for its future. It plans to complete its review within 90 days. Nvidia is likely to retain its investment-grade rating, but a downgrade could raise borrowing costs for both the company and the infrastructure firms whose debt it supports.
