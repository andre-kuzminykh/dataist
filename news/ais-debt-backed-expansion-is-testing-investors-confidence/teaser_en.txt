The financial risk in AI is no longer confined to questions about whether the technology will pay off. Alphabet lost more than half a trillion dollars in market value last week, its largest decline in history, as investors reassessed the sector. With data-center expansion still accelerating and companies’ fortunes tied together through debt and circular financing, a downturn could reach beyond tech. South Korea is adding to the same wager even as its central bank warns of a risk to the broader economy.
