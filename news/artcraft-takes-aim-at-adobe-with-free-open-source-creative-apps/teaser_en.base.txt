A four-person team in Atlanta has released ArtCraft, a free, open-source suite of creative apps built through reverse engineering Adobe software. Its tools aim to offer familiar alternatives to Photoshop and Lightroom without subscriptions or cloud dependence. That is a pointed challenge to Adobe’s business model, but ArtCraft is still in active development, with some features at an early alpha stage. For now, the suite looks more like an opening bid for users who want out of Creative Cloud than a ready replacement for professionals.
