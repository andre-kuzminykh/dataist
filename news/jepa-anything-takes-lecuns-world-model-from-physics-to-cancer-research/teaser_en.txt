JEPA-Anything asks whether one world model can handle tasks from physics and robotics to biology. PhAI Labs researchers and colleagues at Stanford, Oxford and Princeton built the model around Yann LeCun’s JEPA approach, then tested it against a standard JEPA across ten tasks. Its strongest results came in dynamic systems, while its most consequential claim is biological: the model identified a potential combination for attacking liver cancer, then researchers tested it in cells, organoids, tissue samples and mice. That is evidence of a promising research lead, not a treatment.
