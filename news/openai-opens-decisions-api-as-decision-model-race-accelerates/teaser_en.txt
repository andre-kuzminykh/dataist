OpenAI has opened its Decisions API to all customers, putting a faster, lower-cost alternative to large language models into developers’ hands as competition grows around models built to choose among options. The API adapts ChatGPT Luna rather than using a model trained specifically for decision-making. OpenAI says it can cut task times from one to three seconds to 100, 120 or 180 milliseconds; early tests at Speak showed the time between actions falling from 1.4 seconds to 0.13 seconds.
