Andreessen Horowitz’s seventh ranking of consumer AI products adds a measure of what Americans actually spend, using consumer card data from YipitData. The data is a sample, not a picture of company revenue, but it changes the story behind a crowded field: nearly half of US residents use AI, while just 4.5% had an active personal subscription to ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude in August. For now, consumer AI looks less like a mass subscription market than a small group of heavy spenders.
