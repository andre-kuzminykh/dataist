AI-generated charts can answer a question, but they often stop at the view their author chose. Flint-interactive is a language for building visualizations that readers can explore: select data, change a view and connect a chart to other parts of an application. Built on Flint, it lets developers and AI agents describe interactions at a higher level instead of managing low-level display behavior themselves. The pitch is practical: make interactive charts easier to generate and more dependable, without asking an agent to repeatedly write and debug custom code.
