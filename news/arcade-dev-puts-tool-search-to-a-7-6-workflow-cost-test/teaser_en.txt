Arcade.dev says its new Tool Recommendation system can cut the input-token cost of finding tools by as much as 37%. The result that matters more to enterprise buyers is smaller: in a test of complete workflows, total input and output token costs fell 7.6%, with no change in task completion. The feature is due to reach Arcade Cloud customers on October 15 at no separate charge, though platform usage fees remain. Those figures make this less a simple cost-saving story than a test of whether tool search can pay for itself in real work.
