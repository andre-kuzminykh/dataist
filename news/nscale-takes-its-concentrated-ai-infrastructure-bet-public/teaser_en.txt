Nscale is preparing to list on the New York Stock Exchange at a reported $35 billion valuation, with Bloomberg saying it plans to raise $3 billion. The AI infrastructure company has assembled more than $103 billion in contracts since separating from Arkon Energy two years ago, but two customers account for about 85% of that total. The IPO will therefore test not just investor appetite for AI infrastructure, but how much concentration Wall Street is willing to underwrite.
