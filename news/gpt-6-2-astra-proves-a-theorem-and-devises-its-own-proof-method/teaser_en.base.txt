OpenAI said GPT-6.2 Astra built a formal proof of the 2006 Smoktunowicz theorem without human assistance during the run. A machine can verify it; according to Vals AI, it is the first known proof of the theorem written in Lean. The model chose its own approach because Mathlib, a mathematical library, lacked key foundations of the original proof. On the Autoform-Hard test, Astra solved 49 of 56 problems, taking first place ahead of GPT-6.2 Sol and Claude Fable 5.2, which each solved 48.
