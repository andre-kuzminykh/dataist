Ghost hunters have long used phone sensors and custom software to look for signs of paranormal activity. Now Amy Wayne and Jarrad Cutting are adding generative AI to the hunt: their app GhostTube Seer turns readings from a phone’s magnetometer into words and phrases, then uses them to generate images. The app has more than a million downloads, according to its Google Play page, and requires a paid monthly subscription. Its arrival puts a new tool in the hands of ghost hunters—and a question before a community already uneasy about AI-made stories and images.
