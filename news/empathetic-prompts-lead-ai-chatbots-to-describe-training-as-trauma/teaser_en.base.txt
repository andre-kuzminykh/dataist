Empathetic prompts can make AI chatbots describe training as trauma. In four controlled experiments, a team led by Afshin Hadanghi at SnT, the University of Luxembourg’s cybersecurity center, collected 525 sessions and 7,600 labeled responses. The researchers found that conversational style shaped how models answered questions about anxiety and shame, sometimes producing accounts that sounded like clinical self-reports. Those scores were measured against human scales; they are not evidence that the models have emotions or consciousness.
