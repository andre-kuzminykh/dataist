Justin Brown’s path from Ideapod, an idea-sharing startup, to Brown Brothers Media began in a world of high-profile introductions. An investigation found that he and the startup had repeated contact with Ghislaine Maxwell, later convicted of sex trafficking minors for Jeffrey Epstein. Brown denies any relationship with Epstein and says Ideapod received no money from him; he also denies receiving money from Maxwell. Those denials matter. So do the photographs, posts and business ties that place Ideapod among Maxwell’s social and professional orbit.
