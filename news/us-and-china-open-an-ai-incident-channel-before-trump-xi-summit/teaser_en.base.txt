The United States and China have agreed to open an official channel on artificial intelligence ahead of Donald Trump’s meeting with Xi Jinping. Scott Bessent announced the deal after talks with Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng in New York, according to the Financial Times and The Information. The proposed mechanism would notify both sides about AI incidents affecting national security, turning a vague concern about frontier systems into a diplomatic process. A second meeting is expected to settle how those messages would be exchanged.
