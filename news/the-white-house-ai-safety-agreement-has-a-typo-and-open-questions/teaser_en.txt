The White House’s voluntary AI safety agreement, signed by President Donald Trump and six technology executives, promises companies will monitor their systems and submit them to audits. But the document’s own signature line misspells the country it represents: “President of the Unites States.” Trump posted the typo on Truth Social, and the White House emailed the agreement soon after it was signed. Whether the error will reach the Federal Register, the official record of presidential orders and other government documents, is unknown.
