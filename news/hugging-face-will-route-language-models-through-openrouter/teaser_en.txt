Hugging Face and OpenRouter are combining their services to make it easier to run open-weight models. Requests to language models through Providers Inference will now be routed through OpenRouter, which selects a provider based on current price, speed and reliability, then retries with another provider if the first one fails. There is no need to change your Hugging Face code or token. OpenRouter handles more than 800 trillion tokens a month for over 10 million users, routing requests across more than 600 models and 80 providers.
