Microduck has sold 20,000 units through pre-orders after Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang appeared with the small open-source robot in a video. The toys are promised for delivery by Christmas, and Pollen Robotics’ product has become part of Nvidia through a chain of acquisitions: Hugging Face bought Pollen in 2025, then Nvidia bought Hugging Face in September. The sales figure makes Microduck an unlikely holiday hit, but it does not yet show whether buyers want a working robot or a charming toy.
