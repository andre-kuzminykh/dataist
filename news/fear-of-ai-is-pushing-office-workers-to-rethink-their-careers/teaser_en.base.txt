Office workers are leaving familiar careers because they fear AI will change their jobs or make them feel pointless. The data do not yet show mass layoffs caused by the technology: in the US, it has been linked to about 1 million jobs created and 200,000 cuts. But 71% of Americans believe AI will reduce the number of jobs over the next 20 years. Workers are already making decisions based on that fear: changing fields, looking for additional income, or choosing work they see as more resilient and aligned with their values.
