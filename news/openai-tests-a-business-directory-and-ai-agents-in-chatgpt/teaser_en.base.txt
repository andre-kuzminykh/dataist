OpenAI has launched a beta feature called Businesses in ChatGPT. Companies can create profiles directly in ChatGPT and connect AI agents to answer customer questions. Through Business Hub, businesses can update their information, add product catalogs with prices and availability, and track mentions in conversations and visits to their websites. AI agents are currently available to eligible US online retailers and internet service providers; Shopify merchants can connect their stores directly. OpenAI says it does not share users’ conversations with companies. The company says ChatGPT is used by 1.2 billion people a week; over time, OpenAI plans to expand the feature to other markets and industries.
