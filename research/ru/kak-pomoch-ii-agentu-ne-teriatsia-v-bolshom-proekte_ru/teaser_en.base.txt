A capable AI coding agent can still get lost when a software project grows too large to hold in its head.

The authors propose Dev-Primitives: small, AI-accessible guides attached to parts of a codebase, so each part can explain how it works and communicate with related parts. Their HERMES framework brings these guides together, activating relevant components as needed and using clues from program failures to identify what needs changing. This could help AI agents handle complex, ongoing coding tasks without repeatedly piecing the whole project together from scratch.

In this review we look at how Dev-Primitives and HERMES work, and why organizing an agent’s view of a project may matter as much as the model behind it.
