OpenAI and Anthropic are running exercises for scenarios including cyberattacks aided by their products and a political uprising after a catastrophe caused by their models, Axios reports. OpenAI says its teams discuss possible situations to prepare for a range of circumstances, not because they consider those scenarios inevitable. Anthropic declined to comment. The exercises suggest that both companies are treating public unrest as a contingency to plan for, even as the likelihood and scale of any such crisis remain unclear.
