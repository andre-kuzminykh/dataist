Three hikers in their early 20s called for help from a rocky slope on Mount Shasta in Northern California after a series of mistakes. They had brought too little food and overestimated their fitness. They reached the summit at 7:30 p.m., then started down in the dark instead of turning back when they failed to make the top by evening. By morning, they were lost on difficult terrain, and one had injured a knee. The hikers later said they had planned poorly in part because they trusted Google Gemini. The episode is a warning about using a chatbot as a guide in places where bad advice can become a physical emergency.
