## Meta moves Muse AI assistant into Instagram direct messages

[FIGURE:0]

## Muse in chats

The integration removes the need to switch between Instagram and the Muse app. Conversations with the assistant in Instagram will appear in the main Muse app as a separate chat.

Users can send an Instagram post to Muse and ask it to take action. Meta’s examples include adding a coffee shop to an itinerary in Mexico City or looking for a discount on a pair of shoes for Black Friday.

The feature will begin rolling out on Tuesday to iOS and Android users in the United States, Canada, Mexico, Japan and Singapore.

## Working with saved posts

Meta has also added new ways to use saved posts and short videos. Muse will be able to sort saved items into users’ folders, called Collections. Users can also ask it to:

- turn a recipe video into a shopping list;
- put together an itinerary for a future trip using content from their favourite travel creators;
- suggest outfits based on saved fashion videos.

Starting Tuesday, when users save a post, they will be prompted to ask Muse to organise it. The feature is launching in the United States and Canada.

Meta says users remain in control of how Muse works in Instagram: the assistant will not publish, send or make anything public without their approval. Users can ask it to forget information or undo an action at any time.

**Dataist’s take:** Moving Muse into direct messages and letting it work with saved posts gives the assistant access to context users are already collecting on Instagram. The assistant’s actions still require user approval, and the new features are launching only in the countries listed.
