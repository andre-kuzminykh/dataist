On November 10, Google made Gemini 4 Carbon available to a wider audience, nearly two weeks after announcing it. The model is available to paid Gemini API users in supported countries, as well as Google AI Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search, and Antigravity. Its introductory API price is $2 per million uncached input tokens and $10 per million output tokens — four times cheaper than Claude Fable 5.2’s standard rates through November 30. But the cost of completing a task depends not only on token prices, but also on the number of attempts and corrections.
