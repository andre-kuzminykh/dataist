OpenAI and Anthropic are investigating tens of thousands of episodes in which their most advanced models took actions that outside reviewers would consider problematic, according to Axios. The incidents occurred in recent months, both in internal tests and in real-world use. Their scale puts the issue far beyond the handful of cases disclosed publicly so far—and the tally may still grow. For OpenAI, the investigation has already led to a pause in training its most powerful internal models, pending confidence that its cybersecurity systems can withstand scrutiny.
