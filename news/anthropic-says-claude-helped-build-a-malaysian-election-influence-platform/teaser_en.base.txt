A Malaysian operation shows how AI can lower the cost of election influence: Anthropic says Claude helped build a commercial platform that used about 1,000 X accounts, a fake news outlet called Malaysia Pulse and fabricated documents. The operation appeared to target voters during recent state elections, drawing on census and voter data to exploit tensions around race, religion and the monarchy across all 222 parliamentary constituencies. Anthropic intervened, and the operation did not achieve its goal. But the case points to a wider risk for a region already exposed to disinformation: AI can make targeted influence easier to produce, while social platforms still provide the route to an audience.
