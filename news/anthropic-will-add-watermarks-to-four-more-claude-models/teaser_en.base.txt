Anthropic is expanding watermarking to text generated by Claude models. In a new email to API customers, the company said watermarks will appear in Claude Opus 4.6, Claude Opus 4.5, Claude Sonnet 4.5 and Claude Haiku 4.5 starting November 16, 2026. The rollout on Amazon Bedrock may take a few extra days. On two older models—Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6—the watermark works only with the default generation settings: changing just one parameter is enough to bypass it.
