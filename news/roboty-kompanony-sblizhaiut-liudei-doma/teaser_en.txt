The development of multimodal AI and robotics is making companion robots more personalized and attentive to people’s emotional state. The OlloNi SS1 home companion robot from Ollobot is designed for use in an ordinary home: it can greet family members when they return, accompany an elderly person from the living room to the kitchen, remind a child to take a break from studying, or notice that someone is unusually quiet and gently ask whether everything is all right.
