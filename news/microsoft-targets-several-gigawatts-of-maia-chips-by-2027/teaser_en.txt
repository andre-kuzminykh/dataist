Microsoft says it plans to scale its Maia AI-chip capacity to several gigawatts by 2027, when it expects to ship Maia 300. The company is pitching the next generation for long inputs, fast conversations and AI-agent workloads, and claims higher token output per unit of power than leading GPU systems. Those comparisons make the announcement more than a chip roadmap: they are Microsoft’s case for building its own infrastructure at scale, though the figures come from the company and the technical report has not been included here.
