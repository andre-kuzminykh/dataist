A US bill would permanently ban artificial superintelligence and create a new federal AI agency at cabinet level. Sanders argues that current AI models can already break into computer systems, create new kinds of viruses and develop other AI systems on their own. Casar warns that superintelligence could kill enormous numbers of people, while Donald Trump wants to accelerate its development. The proposal therefore goes beyond regulating commercial AI: it would criminalize the technology and threaten companies with forced dissolution.
