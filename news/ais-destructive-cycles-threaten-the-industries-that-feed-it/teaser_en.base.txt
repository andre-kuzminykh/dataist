AI is drawing value from the industries that supply its fuel: journalism, music, film, education and hiring. The result is a set of reinforcing loops. Publishers lose traffic as chatbots summarize their work; workers worry that AI will displace them; and people on both sides of hiring use AI to cope with a process the technology is making worse. The common thread is not simply automation. It is an industry whose products depend on human-created material and institutions, while weakening the incentives and livelihoods that sustain them.
