An AI agent may be able to improve its work even when nobody can tell it the right answer.

The authors introduce a framework that lets an agent revise the instructions and tools it uses as it tackles a task, then carry lessons from those revisions into future work. Instead of relying on answer keys, the agent tests different approaches against each other; this helps it handle unfamiliar tasks and build better habits over time.

In this review we look at how the framework guides those self-improvements, what its tests reveal, and how far the idea could take AI agents in changing real-world settings.
