The New York Times and other publishers have sued Brave, alleging that the browser company built an index of more than 30 billion web pages using an unidentified crawler that publishers could not block. The Times also challenges Brave’s handling of paywalls and its ad-blocking practices. The case extends a fight the newspaper has already taken to OpenAI and Perplexity, and puts the browser’s role as a gatekeeper between publishers and readers under scrutiny.
