Reports say Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang may have been targeted in an alleged Chinese intelligence operation linked to his relationship with Alice Huang, the former head of Singaporean cloud provider Megaspeed. Beijing denies the allegations, and Nvidia’s board says it sees no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised. The scandal touches on advanced-chip exports, Huang’s relationship with Donald Trump and possible questions for US intelligence. Amid reports of the threat, Nvidia shares have fallen by about a quarter over the past month.
