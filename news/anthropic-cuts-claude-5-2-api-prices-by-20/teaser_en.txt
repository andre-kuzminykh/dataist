Anthropic has released Claude Fable 5.2 for a broad range of tasks and Claude Mythos 5.2 for cybersecurity and biology. In tests run by the company and outside developers, the new models posted strong results, while API prices fell 20% to $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, excluding caching. Fable 5.2 is now available on paid Claude plans, Claude Code, Claude Platform, AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure. Mythos 5.2 is available to vetted users through Project Glasswing.
