Anthropic is widening its Cyber Verification Program, folding Project Glasswing into a tiered system that gives more vetted organizations access to Claude’s cyber capabilities. The change formalizes a distinction the company has been managing for six months: public models remain constrained, while approved defenders can use less restricted versions for security work. Anthropic’s own tests suggest the tiers can separate routine defense from authorized attack simulation, though the announcement leaves open how well those safeguards will hold beyond a benchmark.
