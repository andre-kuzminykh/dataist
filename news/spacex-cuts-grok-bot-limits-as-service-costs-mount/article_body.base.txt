## The cost behind the growth

A one-time $1.2 billion expense tied to the acquisition of Cursor weighed on the division’s profit. The source does not say how much of Grok Bot’s operating costs drove the limit cuts, or how the expense relates to the product’s economics.

Musk recently called Grok Bot the fastest-growing product he had ever seen. That claim and the limits now sit side by side, but the announcement offers no usage figures or cost breakdown to show what the growth is costing.

## The missing terms

Musk has not said how usage limits will work for subscribers going forward. I think that is the more consequential omission: without the new limits or a clearer account of service costs, it is hard to tell whether SpaceX is managing a temporary expense or resetting what subscribers can expect from Grok Bot.
