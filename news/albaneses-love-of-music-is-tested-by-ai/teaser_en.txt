Australia’s prime minister, Anthony Albanese, is trying to reconcile copyright rules with plans to build an AI industry in the country. Anthropic, valued at $1.5 trillion, has proposed building data centers in Australia powered by renewable energy and training models there to export tokens around the world. The government sees this as the foundation of a new “green” industry and a way to integrate the country into the global AI supply chain. But content creators fear Australia will weaken copyright protections to attract such investment and become a venue for legitimizing data collected without authors’ consent.
