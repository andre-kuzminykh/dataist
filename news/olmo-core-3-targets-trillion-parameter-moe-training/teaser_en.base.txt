Olmo-core 3 is a new open training system from the Olmo team for scaling mixture-of-experts models. In one benchmark, it increased the expert pool from 8 to 128 while keeping only four experts active per token: total model capacity rose from 4.6 billion to 47 billion parameters, with training speed falling by less than 5%. The team also tested the infrastructure on configurations above a trillion parameters, making the release a bid to open up not just model weights but the machinery needed to train larger models.
