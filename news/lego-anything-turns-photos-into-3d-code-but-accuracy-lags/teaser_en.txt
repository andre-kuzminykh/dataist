A photo-to-3D agent can produce a scene that runs in Blender and still get the geometry wrong. That gap is the point of LEGO-Anything, a system developed by researchers at the University of Maryland and AWS: it turns an image into editable code, then tests how faithfully the resulting scene represents what was pictured. The work suggests that code makes a reconstruction easier to inspect and improve—but does not give the agent a reliable sense of whether it has improved.
