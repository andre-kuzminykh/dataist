Perceptron, a company founded by two former Meta researchers, has introduced the Isaac 0.5 computer vision model. The company was established in November 2024 and develops advanced vision models that help machines perceive their surroundings, reason and act in industrial environments. Isaac 0.5 is intended for robots working in warehouses and factories: it helps them navigate complex spaces, and companies extract visual intelligence from recordings made by robot cameras. The model is publicly available: anyone can study its parameters and training materials.
