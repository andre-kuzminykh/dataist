Anthropic lost $80 billion in the first half of 2026, according to figures in a draft IPO prospectus reviewed by The Wall Street Journal. Revenue reached $16.3 billion, up $1.1 billion from the same period a year earlier. The gap matters beyond one AI lab: Anthropic has also committed to $450 billion in cloud infrastructure spending over the next seven to ten years, leaving its financial trajectory tied to whether revenue can catch up with costs.
