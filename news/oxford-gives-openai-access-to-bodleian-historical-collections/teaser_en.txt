Oxford has allowed OpenAI to use material from the Bodleian Libraries to train AI models, in a deal centered on digitising historical collections. By June 2025, OpenAI had received 125,000 images scanned from historical dissertations, along with material from a rare collection of 16th-century ballads. The university says the digitisation is limited to works outside copyright and that the Bodleian retains the rights to the scans. The agreement also leaves open the possibility of digitising more of the library’s 23 million items.
