Tesla has extended its Austin robotaxi service by one hour, to 11 p.m., and Elon Musk says the company is trying to avoid hitting pets that are hard to see at night. His example was gray kittens on gray asphalt. The explanation draws attention to a weakness in Tesla’s camera-only approach: competitors’ lidar and radar can help detect objects in darkness, though adding sensors would challenge Musk’s long-standing argument that they make autonomous cars less safe.
