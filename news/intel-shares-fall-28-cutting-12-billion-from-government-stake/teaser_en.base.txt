Intel shares fell 28% after the company forecast fourth-quarter revenue of $15 billion to $16 billion, below analysts’ expectations of about $17 billion. The selloff cut $12 billion from the value of the US government’s stake. That loss has not erased the gains: Intel shares are still up more than 70% this year, and the government’s investment, along with Nvidia’s, remains profitable. But the forecast shows how quickly the summer rally can give way to questions about Intel’s ability to turn its AI-related opportunity into cash.
