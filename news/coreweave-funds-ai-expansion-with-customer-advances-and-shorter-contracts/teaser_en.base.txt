CoreWeave, a cloud provider that rents out computing capacity for AI, is asking customers to pay upfront, taking out loans tied to specific contracts and signing shorter agreements that allow it to charge higher prices. In the third quarter, the company reported revenue of $3.66 billion, up 168% from a year earlier, and a net loss of $709 million; interest expense was $846 million. The new approach is intended to help fund data center construction and fulfill $132.5 billion in orders without resorting to unsecured borrowing.
