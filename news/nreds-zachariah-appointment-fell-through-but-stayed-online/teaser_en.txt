A June announcement put Katie Zachariah on the advisory board of NovaRed Mining alongside former US homeland security secretary Kristi Noem. Zachariah had left her job as a Department of Homeland Security spokesperson two months earlier. But when WIRED asked her on Friday whether she was still on the board, she said the appointment “was fake.” People associated with the company say they discussed a possible role with her, but it never materialized. The episode raises a more basic question about NRED Intelligent Mining: why did a small mining company announce prominent advisers before their involvement was settled?
