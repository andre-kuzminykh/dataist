Meta exposed hidden instructions for WhatsApp’s AI support chatbot in the code of roughly 80 help and FAQ pages, according to an analysis by Redact. The text included guidance on legal questions, data-protection rules and user complaints, and was not intended for readers. WhatsApp removed it after WIRED reported the issue, calling the exposure a bug. The episode matters less as a glimpse of chatbot prompts than as a reminder that support systems can reveal a company’s internal boundaries in places built to explain its policies.
