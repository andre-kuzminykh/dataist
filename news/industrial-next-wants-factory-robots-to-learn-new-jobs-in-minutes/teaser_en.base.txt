Industrial Next wants factory robots to move between jobs in minutes, not weeks. Founded in 2021 by former Tesla employees Lukasz Pankau and Allen Pan, the startup is building robotic stations that learn new tasks through simulation and demonstrations rather than fixed programs. Its systems are already doing delicate work in smartphone manufacturing, including tasks manufacturers had struggled to automate. The larger claim is that robots can adapt when a product or production line changes—a capability that could make factories less dependent on equipment built for one job.
