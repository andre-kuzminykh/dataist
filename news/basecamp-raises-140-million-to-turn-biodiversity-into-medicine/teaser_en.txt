Basecamp Research has raised $140 million in a round led by S32, with participation from Nvidia, Anthropic’s Anthology Fund, NATO Innovation Fund and Redalpine. The 2020-founded company wants to use genetic material from poorly studied ecosystems to train its EDEN biological models, then turn the resulting designs into therapies. Its immediate focus is cell therapy delivered inside the body. The bet is that evolution contains a far larger library of useful biological solutions than current genomic databases capture.
