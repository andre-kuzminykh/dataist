At MAX in Miami, Adobe announced new AI features for Photoshop, Premiere, Illustrator and InDesign, and expanded Firefly’s capabilities. The company released a local MCP protocol that will let users access Creative Cloud app features through third-party chatbots, including ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. Firefly gained tools for prototyping, image editing and video assembly, while Adobe’s apps are getting new ways to work with lighting, color and objects.
