Reform UK’s Gareth O’Connell drew scrutiny after speaking against 20mph speed limits in the Senedd without looking at his notes. Small flashes from his glasses prompted suspicions that they were smart glasses, whose lenses can act as miniature teleprompters. No one claimed the glasses were recording, but the episode has pushed Welsh politicians to demand clearer rules—and renewed calls to ban smart glasses in Westminster, where lawmakers say discreet recording could put private discussions at risk.
