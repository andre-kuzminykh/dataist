# Model ML raises $250 million to automate bankers’ work

The three-year-old company was founded by brothers Chaz and Arnie Englander. They had previously built two other businesses together, including Fat Llama, a peer-to-peer rental marketplace. In 2022, Swedish competitor Hygglo bought it for $41.5 million.

## How Model ML’s agents work

Model ML’s agents connect to clients’ systems, data and tools, including Excel and PowerPoint. The company says the agents account for the context of the deals bankers and consultants are working on, and can automatically update financial models and presentations when new developments change the analysis.

Other startups automating repetitive tasks for investment bankers and management consultants include Hebbia and Rogo. In April, Rogo raised $160 million at a $2 billion valuation.

## Betting on open models

Anthropic and OpenAI have also built tools for finance professionals. Model ML says it uses open models, which, according to the company, lets it operate at about 2% of the cost of frontier models and deliver results faster.

The funds raised point to investor interest in automating professional tasks involving documents, models and presentations. However, the cost and speed comparison comes from Model ML, so it does not provide an independent assessment of how much more effective its agents are than competitors’ products.
