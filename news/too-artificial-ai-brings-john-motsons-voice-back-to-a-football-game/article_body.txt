# EA Sports brings John Motson’s voice back to the football simulator

## A voice for a new generation of players

Motson died in February 2023. His career spanned 50 years; the Lancashire native retired eight years before his death. In FC27, his voice will introduce new footballers, including Lamine Yamal and Chloe Kelly.

The commentator’s son, Fred Motson, thanked EA Sports for paying tribute to his father and preserving his voice in the game. He said Motson’s voice had inspired millions of players across generations as they picked up a controller, and that new fans would now be able to discover his work.

EA Sports said it used nearly a decade of archive recordings made while Motson worked on games in the Fifa series from 1996 to 2006. The new version combines original audio with an AI-recreated voice trained on the commentator’s own recordings.

## Reactions and other digital returns

The decision drew mixed reactions. Reddit users called the return of Motson’s voice “disrespectful” and “disgusting,” and lamented that the world had become bleak if even death offered no escape. Using AI to digitally resurrect celebrities is sometimes called “ghost exploitation.”

Other projects featuring digital likenesses of deceased stars have emerged:

- The family of Greta Garbo, one of the stars of cinema’s golden age, granted rights to her likeness and voice. In September, it emerged that these would be used to create an AI version of the actress for an ad campaign by a Swedish industrial company.
- An AI version of Val Kilmer, star of the Batman films, was announced earlier this year as a cast member in *As Deep As the Grave*. Kilmer died in 2025; he had been cast in the film but did not live to shoot it.

Dataist’s take: EA Sports’ project draws on archive recordings and the family’s consent, but the Reddit discussion shows that digitally recreating deceased celebrities remains controversial. The amount paid to Motson’s family has not been disclosed.
