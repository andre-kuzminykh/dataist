Open-source AI models are being used to create child sexual abuse material, and the scale is growing faster than the systems meant to detect it. Rebecca Portnoff, a child-safety expert, estimates that more than 500 open models are now used to produce it, up from fewer than a dozen two years ago. Reports of AI-generated material to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children topped 1.5 million last year, compared with 67,000 in 2024.
