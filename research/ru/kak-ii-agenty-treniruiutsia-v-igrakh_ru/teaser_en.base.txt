Can AI learn to play games by helping build the games it needs to practice on?

Game2Player pairs an AI game-builder with two kinds of AI players: one that writes code and one that interprets what it sees. The builder turns existing web games into clear tasks with ways to track progress and confirm success, while the players test those tasks and flag problems. This creates a steady supply of checked gameplay examples, helping AI learn not just what a game looks like, but which actions move it closer to its goal.

In this review we look at how Game2Player creates and verifies game-playing practice, how the resulting experience is used to train AI agents, and what this approach could mean for learning to act in visual worlds.
