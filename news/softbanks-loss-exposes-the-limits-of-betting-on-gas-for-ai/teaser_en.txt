SoftBank reported a quarterly loss of $7.1 billion, more than half of it tied to power-generation equipment. The company made nonrefundable deposits on gas turbines and ordered more than it needed — over 13 GW, nearly 50% extra. SoftBank now considers a significant portion of those orders useless. As demand for electricity from data centers grows, the episode suggests gas generation may expand more slowly than SoftBank expected, even as AI’s appetite for power continues to support the market.
