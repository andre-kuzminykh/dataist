Laplace Intelligence is testing robots and self-driving cars for failures before they enter the real world. The startup uses AI agents to search simulated environments for rare situations that could expose weaknesses in physical AI systems. It says the goal is to help developers find problems before deployment, then build an evidence base that customers, insurers and regulators can trust. That ambition matters as AI moves beyond screens: a failure that is manageable in software could have more serious consequences on a road or around people.
