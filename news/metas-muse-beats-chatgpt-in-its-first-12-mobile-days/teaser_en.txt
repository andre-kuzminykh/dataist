Meta’s Muse drew more downloads and daily users than ChatGPT did at the same early stage of its mobile launch, according to Apptopia estimates. The comparison is not perfectly even: ChatGPT launched worldwide on iOS, while Muse launched on both iOS and Android but only in the US and Canada. Even after narrowing the data to comparable markets and devices, Muse came out ahead — an early sign that Meta’s distribution network may matter as much as the app itself.
