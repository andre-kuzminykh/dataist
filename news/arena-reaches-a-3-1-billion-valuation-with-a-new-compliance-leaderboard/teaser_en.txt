Arena has raised a new round led by Lightspeed Venture Partners and Khosla Ventures, bringing its valuation to $3.1 billion, nearly double what it was 10 months ago. The platform lets users compare AI models by judging their responses to prompts and projects, and says tens of millions visit each month. The raise comes as companies look beyond static benchmarks—and Arena is expanding its leaderboard to score whether models follow requirements in use.
