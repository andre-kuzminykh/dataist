Can AI agents write game-playing programs good enough to beat people—or even themselves?

The researchers introduce a test of whether coding agents can learn from playing modern games and turn that experience into programs that run on their own, without asking an AI model for help at every move. The programs often outperform existing AI players, but human players still win in most of the games examined, showing how hard it is to handle changing scenes, controls, and decisions that unfold over time.

In this review we look at how the test works, what the results reveal about AI’s ability to learn through interaction, and where human skill still has the edge.
