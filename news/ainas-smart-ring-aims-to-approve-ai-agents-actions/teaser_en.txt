Aina has introduced a titanium smart ring with a small screen and a joystick button. It lets users approve or reject AI agents’ actions without taking out their phone. The device connects to Mac and iPhone, uses location, calendar and email data, and works with third-party services such as Uber and Spotify. Preorders are open at a temporary price of $399, rising to $499 later. Shipments are due to begin in January 2027.
