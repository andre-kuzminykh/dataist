Rivian is adding its voice AI assistant to the technology it licenses to Volkswagen through their joint venture, extending a partnership that began with Rivian’s software and electrical architecture. The deal’s price, payment terms and eligible Volkswagen models remain undisclosed. The assistant only arrived in Rivian vehicles in September 2026, after two years of development, so the announcement offers an early test of whether the platform Volkswagen backed can extend beyond the car’s underlying electronics.
