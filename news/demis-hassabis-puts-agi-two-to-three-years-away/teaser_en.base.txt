Demis Hassabis now puts the arrival of artificial general intelligence two to three years away, closer than his earlier estimate this year of three to five. In an interview with The Wall Street Journal, the Google DeepMind co-founder said his forecast keeps moving toward the present. His proposed test is not whether a model can reproduce existing knowledge, but whether it can make a scientific leap: could a system trained only on knowledge available before 1901 independently recreate Einstein’s 1905 papers?
