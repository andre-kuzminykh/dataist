Instinct says more than half of its platform’s transactions involve travel, according to founder Shinn in an interview with investor Patrick O’Shaughnessy. He sees a case for AI interfaces in urgent bookings, such as finding a way to reach New York that same evening. But Instinct has also drawn criticism for how Shinn talks about restaurant reservations: an agent that checks for cancellations every five seconds could help users get a table, while potentially giving them an advantage over other diners.
