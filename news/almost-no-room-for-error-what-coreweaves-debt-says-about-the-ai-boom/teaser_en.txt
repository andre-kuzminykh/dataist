CoreWeave, a former cryptocurrency miner turned AI computing provider, is building a network of 52 data centers and financing its expansion with large loans. The company owes $47 billion, including more than $10 billion due next year; last quarter, it lost $709 million on revenue of $3.6 billion. Its model depends on Nvidia, customers such as OpenAI and Anthropic, and bringing new facilities online quickly. If revenue, financing or construction is delayed, servicing its debt will become harder.
