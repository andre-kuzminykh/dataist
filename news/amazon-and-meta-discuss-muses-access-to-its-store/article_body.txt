# Amazon and Meta discuss Muse’s access to its store

## Why Amazon restricted access

Amazon CEO Andy Jassy said that shopping through third-party agents has not yet become more convenient or better than buying directly from a seller. At the same time, he did not rule out the possibility that the company could open access to them.

Amazon has its own shopping features that use AI agents. In some cases, they have brought in sellers who do not sell on Amazon. The company has also tried to block Perplexity, but without success.

## Muse is gaining users

Muse is Meta’s most successful consumer AI product to date. More than 10 million people in the US and Canada use it. According to CNBC, the agent helps people organize saved products on Instagram, boosting advertisers’ conversion rates and already benefiting Meta’s social network ad business.

According to Forter Intelligence, Muse recently accounted for 51% of all orders placed through AI agents. SpaceX’s Grok had 24%, and OpenAI’s ChatGPT had 22%.

Dataist’s take: The talks show that AI agents’ access to major retail platforms is about more than shopping convenience; it also touches on control over search and advertising. Amazon may open access to Muse, but no final decision has been made: executives at both companies must approve it.
