Paul Christiano, a member of the OpenAI Foundation board, said at the Hinton Lectures 2026 conference that under rapid, weakly controlled AI development, the odds of a takeover could be comparable to a coin toss. He estimated at around 20% the risk that, within a year, AI alignment problems could prevent people from living normally, through death, enslavement or collateral damage. Christiano also called an AI accident the most likely cause of death for many people attending the conference.
