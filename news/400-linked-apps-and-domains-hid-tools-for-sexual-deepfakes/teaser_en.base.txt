A pet-image app can look harmless until it decides what to show you. Viefur sells coins for turning animal photos into dressed-up portraits; about $4 buys 130 coins. But WIRED found it among a network of more than 400 linked domains and apps that appear to conceal tools for making sexual deepfakes. The network’s apps can show ordinary content or, under certain conditions, load a separate interface for creating explicit images and videos from people’s photos.
