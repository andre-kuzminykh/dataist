A planned AI data center in West Virginia could erase a rare grove of 200 blight-resistant American chestnut trees. Nscale’s Monarch Compute Campus is planned to cover more than 2,250 acres and provide 8 gigawatts of computing capacity for AI companies, including Anthropic. The trees are more than a patch of woodland: the grove is a source of seeds and genetic material for efforts to restore a species devastated by disease.
