PitPro, a startup building tire-changing robots, has put its first system to work at a tire service in Calgary, Canada. The installation places two sensor-equipped robots in an existing service bay, where they remove wheels and fit new or repositioned tires in less than 15 minutes. It matters less as a flashy robot demo than as a test of whether a machine built for fleet depots can survive the dirt, weather and labor economics of ordinary tire shops.
