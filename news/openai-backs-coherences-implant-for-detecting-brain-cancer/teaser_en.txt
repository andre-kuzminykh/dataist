OpenAI has invested in Coherence, a San Francisco startup developing an implant that detects electrical signals associated with brain tumors. The company says it has identified those signals in animals and is now adapting its work for human research, with clinical trials as a longer-term goal. The investment connects OpenAI to a second brain-computer interface company, but Coherence’s proposed device is aimed not just at reading neural activity: it is also designed to stimulate tissue and potentially slow tumor growth.
