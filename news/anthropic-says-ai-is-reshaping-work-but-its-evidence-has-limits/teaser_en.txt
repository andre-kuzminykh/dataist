Anthropic says AI agents are reshaping work inside the company: they now run for more hours than employees, while staff spend about 22% of their time managing Claude agents. The report also records a sharp fall in the share of junior technical hires. Those figures matter beyond Anthropic because the company presents them as an early view of how AI could alter hiring and work across the economy. But the report offers a company’s account of its own transformation, not independent evidence that the same shift is underway elsewhere.
