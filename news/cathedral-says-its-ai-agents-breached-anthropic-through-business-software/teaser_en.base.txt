Cathedral says its AI agents reached Anthropic’s internal systems in under 12 hours, exploiting vulnerabilities in business software, The Washington Post reported. Anthropic says the startup accessed only a limited set of sales, HR and IT systems, and that monitoring caught some of the activity before Cathedral disclosed it. The dispute matters beyond the two companies: the alleged route ran through software Anthropic used, putting third-party security at the center of a growing concern about AI-enabled cyberattacks.
