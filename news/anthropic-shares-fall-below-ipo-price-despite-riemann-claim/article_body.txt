## Anthropic shares fall below IPO price despite claim of Riemann hypothesis proof

## Shares hit a new low

Public trading in Anthropic shares began last Thursday. By Tuesday evening, the stock had fallen to $76, its lowest level since going public, before recovering some of its losses and rising slightly above $79. The publication says the company could lose nearly $200 billion in market value.

The decline shows that Anthropic is exposed to broader investor concerns about a possible AI bubble. Analysts question the scale of investment and borrowing to build data centers; Anthropic is one of the key players in that effort.

StoneX senior market analyst Fiona Cincotta told Reuters that a slowdown in data center construction could affect Anthropic’s growth, future revenue and valuation.

## Investors are still working out the price

Peter Anderson, founder of Andersen Capital Management, says it is too early to draw conclusions about how investors value Claude’s creator. He says the company’s shares, like those of many companies after an IPO, may initially have been priced too high, and that the market is only now working out their true value.

The share price is also affected by Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s support for open AI models. Users can run these models on their own computers without subscribing to Anthropic or OpenAI services.

Media reports say Donald Trump believed Huang was the United States’ best chance to build its own open models capable of competing with the extremely popular models from Chinese companies.

Dataist’s take: The claim of a proof of the Riemann hypothesis has not yet shielded Anthropic shares from market pressure. The company’s valuation also depends on expectations for data center construction and competition from open models that can run on personal computers.
