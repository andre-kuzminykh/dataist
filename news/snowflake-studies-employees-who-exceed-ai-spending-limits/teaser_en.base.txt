Snowflake sets AI spending limits for employees, but it does more than restrict those who exceed them: the company investigates which workflows drive the high costs and whether they can be extended to others. According to Will Allen, head of products for CoCo and CoWork AI at Snowflake, overspending can sometimes point to a useful way of working. The approach differs both from the internal token-consumption rankings used by Amazon, Meta and OpenAI in 2026 and from simply imposing strict limits.
