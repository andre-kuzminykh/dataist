Cody Wilson has built GatGPT, an AI tool that answers questions about firearms and recommends parts for assembling them. Trained on DEFCAD, Wilson’s online library of millions of digital gun and gun-part models, it is meant to help users design weapons through natural-language prompts. Wilson says he used GatGPT to create the WASP, a working pistol with no serial number. The project matters less as a single unusual gun than as a possible way to lower the barrier to designing untraceable firearms.
