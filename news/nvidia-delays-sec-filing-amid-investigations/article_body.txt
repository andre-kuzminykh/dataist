# Nvidia delays SEC filing amid investigations

## What is known about the delay

A 10-Q contains more information about a company’s operations than a financial results press release. Among other things, it describes legal proceedings that could affect the business and other risk factors.

Nvidia usually filed this report on the same day it announced its quarterly results. The delay therefore drew attention: economic and investment analyst Edward Dowd called it a troubling sign in a comment on X.

After Nvidia’s public earnings call with investors, UBS analyst Timothy Arcuri asked the company about the delay. In a research note, he said management attributed it to the ongoing investigation and the possible need to add new risk factors to the report.

Arcuri maintained his buy rating on Nvidia shares but lowered his price target from $240 to $220. At the time of publication, the shares were trading at about $150.

## What investigations are linked to Nvidia

On the same day Nvidia reported its quarterly results, The Wall Street Journal reported that the SEC was examining whether the company’s disclosures had misled investors.

The company is also facing several federal investigations over allegations that its partners sold Nvidia chips subject to export restrictions to China. Several congressional committees are conducting inquiries, while the Department of Justice is investigating Megaspeed. The company is run by a woman who had an affair with Huang; she is also suspected of spying for China.

Nvidia is separately conducting an internal investigation into Huang. He admitted to having an affair with Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive. Allegations have also emerged about his relationships with other Nvidia employees.

The filing delay could mean Nvidia plans to provide more detail about its legal risks. When the 10-Q is released, Wall Street will scrutinize it closely.

Dataist's take: the key question is exactly what information the company will add to the risk section. Nvidia has not yet filed the report or publicly explained the delay; the SEC and other investigations remain separate circumstances against which the market will read the document.
