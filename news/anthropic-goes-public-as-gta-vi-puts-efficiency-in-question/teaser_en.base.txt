After 13 years of waiting, Rockstar Games released Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA VI). The game debuted with the highest score in Metacritic history and brought in $3.79 billion on its first day. Around 5,000 people worked on it for more than a decade, and the game itself was made without generative AI. Its release coincided with Anthropic’s $81.3 billion IPO, the largest in history. The contrast raised the question again of what is lost when efficiency becomes the main priority in creative work.
