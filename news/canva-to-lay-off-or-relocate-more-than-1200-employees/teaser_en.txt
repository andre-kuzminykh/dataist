Canva will lay off or relocate 1,271 employees, representing 22% of its global workforce. The reorganisation will affect people who helped build the Australian design platform: some will be asked to move to central offices, while others will leave the company. Those leaving will receive at least six months’ salary and, in countries where health insurance is provided, another 12 months of coverage. They will also be allowed to keep their work laptops and apply for a $10,000 grant (A$14,000) to start their own business.
