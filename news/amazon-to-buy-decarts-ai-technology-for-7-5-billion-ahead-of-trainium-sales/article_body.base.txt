# Amazon to buy Decart technology for $7.5 billion to advance Trainium chips

[FIGURE:0]

## What the deal includes

Decart is known for real-time interactive world models — the latest is called Oasis 3 — and tools for AI video generation. But the company’s key technology for Amazon is DOS, a software stack designed to make models run more efficiently across different hardware platforms.

Amazon said it will acquire the Decart founders, as well as the company’s AI researchers and engineers, along with DOS technology.

## Why Amazon wants Decart’s technology

Until now, Amazon has used Trainium chips in its own data centers. The company is now preparing to offer them to customers, competing with Nvidia, whose chips remain the industry standard. Decart’s optimization could help customers run their workloads on Amazon hardware instead of Nvidia’s.

Peter DeSantis, Amazon’s senior vice president of AI foundational models, custom chips and quantum computing, linked more efficient computing with better training and reinforcement learning, faster inference, and lower costs for customers using more advanced models.

Decart and Amazon did not respond to TechCrunch’s requests for comment.

Dataist’s take: The acquisition links Amazon’s plans to sell Trainium with software already designed to work across hardware from several manufacturers. The deal could make it easier for customers to switch to Amazon chips, but the source does not say when they will go on sale or on what terms.
