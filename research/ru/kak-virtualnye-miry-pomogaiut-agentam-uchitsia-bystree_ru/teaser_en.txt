An AI agent can only learn from the worlds it gets to explore, so what happens when those worlds are both realistic and easy to expand?

The authors introduce AgentGarten, a system that combines interactive simulators with a shared visual engine, giving agents worlds with consistent rules and lifelike scenes. Agents learn by acting, then pass useful experience to later agents through reusable playbooks, helping them improve with far fewer rounds of practice than conventional training.

In this review we look at how AgentGarten builds its virtual worlds, turns each agent’s experience into a foundation for the next, and could make ongoing learning more practical.
