Meta has introduced Muse Ember, its smallest and most cost-efficient model. It is designed for routine AI-agent work: calling tools, handling small coding tasks, routing requests, extracting data and processing long documents. Meta says Muse Ember delivers more capability per dollar than Claude Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna at every reasoning level. In an Artificial Analysis test, a task costs about $0.14: the larger Muse Spark 1.3 scored nearly as much — 37 versus 36 — but costs $2.39. In Meta’s own tests, Muse Ember leads in most cases. But on real-world coding tasks, Haiku outperformed it on the FrontierCode 2.0-AA benchmark: 34.9% versus 27.2%.
