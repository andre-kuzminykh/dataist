Nearly 55,000 people have signed a petition opposing Google’s plan to build a data center in Thurrock, 15 miles east of London. Representatives of conservation group Buglife, local environmental groups, nonprofit Foxglove and motorcycle club Thurrock Hammers gathered outside the company’s office in London’s King’s Cross. They say the project could harm rare plants and animals, increase emissions and deprive local residents of a racetrack. Google promises to protect biodiversity and offset emissions, but opponents intend to challenge the project at every stage.
