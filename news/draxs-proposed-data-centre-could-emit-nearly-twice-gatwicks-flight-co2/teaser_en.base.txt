Drax is considering a 1.2 GW data centre at its North Yorkshire biomass power station, with most of its electricity supplied by burning imported wood. An NRDC estimate puts the site’s annual emissions at 8.6 million tonnes of CO₂ if it runs at full power around the clock — nearly twice the emissions from all flights out of Gatwick in 2024. The project has not entered formal planning, but the estimate puts a specific question behind the UK’s data-centre boom: what counts as clean power when demand grows faster than the grid?
