OpenAI says a team of thousands of AI agents analyzed data from millions of mouse-brain cells in 32 minutes, a task the researchers estimate would take people several months to a year. The project, built with PerturbAI and Scripps Research, used GPT-Rosalind to study a CRISPR-based atlas of the whole brain. Its results include differences between two disease-linked genes and a possible connection between cholesterol regulation and neurodegeneration.
