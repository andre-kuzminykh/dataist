Microsoft has launched two Windows 11 PCs built around Nvidia chips and local AI workloads: a Surface laptop starting at $2,600 and a developer workstation starting at $6,000. The machines pair upgraded processors, graphics, unified memory and cooling with a new Windows feature for isolating AI agents. The strategy matters beyond the hardware: Microsoft says the feature will reach all Windows 11 users, making the operating system—not just the new PCs—part of its push to support agent-based software.
