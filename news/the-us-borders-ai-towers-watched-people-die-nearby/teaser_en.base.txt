The US border’s “virtual wall” was built to spot people in remote terrain and get help to them before they disappeared into the desert. An investigation by MIT Technology Review and the Times of San Diego found that more than 1,050 people died from 2015 to early 2026 within the stated coverage areas of Border Patrol surveillance towers. Many were in terrain the cameras should have been able to see. The finding turns a showcase for AI-enabled enforcement into a test of whether detection without dependable response is protection at all.
