Goodfire is putting its interpretability research to work as a safety layer for AI agents: small probes read a model’s internal signals during inference, and a second model investigates when they flag suspicious behavior. The monitors are now available to Baseten customers, who can choose which risks to track and what the system should do when it detects one. Goodfire says the approach can be substantially cheaper than having another model reread every step—an important claim as agents increasingly operate beyond test environments.
