Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei has opposed handing political power to AI. In a post on X, he responded to an Axios article citing people familiar with internal discussions who said some Anthropic employees believe humanity can safely hand power to superintelligent systems and often should, rather than fight to retain control. Amodei called it absurd to suggest that he supports people giving up their free will. In his view, AI should be subject to lawful democratic processes, and political decisions should remain in human hands.
