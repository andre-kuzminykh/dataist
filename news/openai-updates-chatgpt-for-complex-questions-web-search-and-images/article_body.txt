OpenAI has begun rolling out GPT-6.2 Sol, a version of ChatGPT tuned for conversations. It is available worldwide to Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise users. The company says the model handles complex requests and web searches better than GPT-6, and asks more relevant follow-up questions. In internal tests, GPT-6.2 interpreted images more accurately — 71.7% versus 47.4% — and followed complex instructions more often: 78.1% versus 62.5%.

# OpenAI releases GPT-6.2 Sol for complex requests and web search

## Test results

On an internal benchmark of expert-level tasks, GPT-6.2 outperformed GPT-6 at all four reasoning levels: Instant, Medium, High and Extra high. The new model scored roughly 65% to just over 71%, while GPT-6 scored about 52–66%.

OpenAI also uses an internal version of BrowseComp to test how models find answers to difficult questions on the web. GPT-6.2 led at every level and, at the highest level, outperformed GPT-6 by about 10 percentage points.

## Safety and access

According to the system card, GPT-6.2 follows safety rules in 91–94% of difficult conversations. GPT-6 scored 77–80%. The most notable progress was in teen safety, covering emotional dependence, eating disorders and dangerous activities.

The results apply only to ChatGPT’s chat mode. The Work and Codex models have not changed. Enterprise administrators can decide whether their users get access to GPT-6.2.

Dataist’s take: GPT-6.2 improves several aspects of ChatGPT at once, but the results cited come from OpenAI’s internal tests. The update also does not extend to Work and Codex, and Enterprise access depends on administrators’ decisions.
