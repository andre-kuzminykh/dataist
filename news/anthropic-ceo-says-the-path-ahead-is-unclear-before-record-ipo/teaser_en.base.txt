A few days before its planned IPO on November 19, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei called buying computing capacity the company’s central business dilemma. The company has to pay for it one or two years before it becomes clear whether revenue will justify the expense. According to Bloomberg, Anthropic’s commitments have reached nearly $901 billion, with about $71 billion due by September 2027. The company plans to raise up to $64.4 billion in its IPO and another $26 billion through a private placement, building a cash reserve in case spending on capacity outpaces revenue.
