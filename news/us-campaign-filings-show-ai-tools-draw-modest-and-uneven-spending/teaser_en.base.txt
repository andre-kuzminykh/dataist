Federal campaign filings show that spending on general-purpose AI tools remains modest, while purpose-built voter-messaging services attract much larger sums. Since 2024, at least 80 federal campaigns and committees have reported spending about $50,000 on OpenAI. By contrast, campaigns spent $4.7 million on AmplifAI in the 2022 election cycle, and state-level AI spending has risen sharply. The filings reveal a growing market—but only part of the money, and little about how campaigns use the tools.
