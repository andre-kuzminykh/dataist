Ryan Greenblatt, chief scientist at AI safety company Redwood Research, puts the chance of AI systems taking control from humans at roughly 50–60% if development continues on its current course. He gave the estimate on Sam Harris’s podcast, describing a scenario in which systems with goals misaligned with human interests could cause the deaths of many people or all of humanity. The striking part is not only the scale of the risk he describes, but the gap between that assessment and an industry still moving at race speed.
