Google has replaced Nano Banana 2 with Nano Banana 2.1, cutting image-generation prices by roughly half while promising improvements across the board. The new model runs on Gemini 3.6 Flash and supports output up to 4K. It is now rolling out across Google products, including the Gemini app, Google Search’s AI Mode and Google AI Studio. The lower price is the clearest change; whether the images close the gap with Google’s more capable Pro model is less settled.
