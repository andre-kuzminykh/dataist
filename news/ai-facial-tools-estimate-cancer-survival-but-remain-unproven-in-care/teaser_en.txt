An AI system built from facial photographs estimates biological age and cancer survival through two related tools, FAHR-FaceAge and FAHR-FaceSurvival. In US patients receiving radiation therapy, those judged to look at least five years older than their actual age had a 21% higher risk of death over a median four-year follow-up; those judged at least five years younger had a 17% lower risk. The findings suggest a possible way to assess patients’ physical reserve before intensive treatment, but the researchers say more study is needed before broad use.
