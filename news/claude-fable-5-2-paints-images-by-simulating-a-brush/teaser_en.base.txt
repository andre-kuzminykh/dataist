Anthropic has introduced Claude Fable 5.2, a language model that creates images by laying down brushstrokes one by one on a virtual canvas using code. It simulates bristle texture, paint application and error correction. In one demo, the model built a parking lot scene from about 700,000 strokes; in another, it painted a bird at dawn in 111 coordinate sequences. On Anthropic’s internal RoboDojo Sim benchmark, Fable 5.2 scored 64.9%, compared with 29.8% for OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra. But in a test with a physical robotic arm, it was slower and less precise than its competitor.
