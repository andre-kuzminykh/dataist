Flow Engineering has raised $50 million at a $750 million valuation to build AI agents for hardware design. The three-year-old San Francisco startup says its software matches CAD drawings against product requirements, simulation results and other tests. The round brings in investors with ties to Elon Musk-backed companies, while Sequoia Capital, which led Flow’s previous round, invested again. The valuation makes Flow’s next challenge clear: showing that its agents can become part of the engineering process at companies building complex physical products.
