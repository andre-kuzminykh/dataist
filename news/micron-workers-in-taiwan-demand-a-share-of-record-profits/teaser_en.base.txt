Workers at Micron Technology in Taiwan are pushing for higher wages, quarterly bonuses and increased pension contributions. In October, a dispute with management led to the first strike in the island’s semiconductor industry: about a dozen employees walked off the job without authorization for one day. The company posted record net income of nearly $85 billion for the fiscal year, up from $8.5 billion a year earlier. Workers rejected an agreement Micron management reached with one of the two unions and retained the option to call a strike.
