Google is testing Call for Me, a Gemini feature that makes phone calls for Pixel 11 owners in the US. The limited experiment is available to people who pay for a Gemini subscription and install the beta version of Google Phone for Android. Gemini can call a store to check stock, reserve a restaurant table, move an appointment, or ask a business to hold items. It can navigate automated menus, wait on hold and continue once a person answers. The shift is from helping with a call to handling an ordinary errand, while the user watches.
