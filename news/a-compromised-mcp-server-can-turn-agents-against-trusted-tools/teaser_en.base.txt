By the end of 2026, task-specific AI agents will be running in 40% of enterprise applications, up from less than 5% in January, Gartner predicts. The company also named oversight of agentic AI the top cybersecurity trend for 2026 and warned that organizations are not ready to register identities and manage access for software agents. This matters to companies adopting the Model Context Protocol (MCP): its authentication rules continue to change, and a compromised connected server can influence an agent’s actions and its calls to other tools.
