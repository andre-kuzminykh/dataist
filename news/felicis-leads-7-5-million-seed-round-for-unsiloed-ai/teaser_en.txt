Unsiloed AI has raised $7.5 million in seed funding in a round led by Felicis. The company is developing software that extracts data from complex documents — tables, charts and pages with challenging layouts — and converts it into a structured format for AI agents. The system also identifies exactly where each piece of information came from, so companies can verify the results. Y Combinator, Orange Collective, Uncorrelated Ventures, and angel investors Aarti Ramamurthy and Beth Turner also participated in the round.
