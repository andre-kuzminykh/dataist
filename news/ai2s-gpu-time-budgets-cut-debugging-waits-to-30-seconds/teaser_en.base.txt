Ai2 has replaced GPU priority scheduling with time budgets, fair-share allocation and a minimum runtime agreement across clusters containing thousands of NVIDIA H100, B200 and B300 GPUs. The change is meant to make scarce compute follow research priorities without leaving hardware idle. It also cut the 90th-percentile wait for debugging jobs on the new scheduler from two hours to 30 seconds—a result that makes this more than a budgeting exercise.
