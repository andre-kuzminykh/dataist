Hugging Face’s Irene Solaiman says AI benchmarks still fall short of the scientific consistency needed to compare systems reliably. At TechCrunch Disrupt last week, the company’s chief policy officer pointed to unresolved questions about reproducibility, access to models and how to compare tests whose reliability is assessed separately. Those gaps matter as labs look to outside evaluators for safety checks: scrutiny is shifting from models alone to the agents and tools people actually use.
