On November 5, ten teams gathered at the Disaster City training facility in Texas to test how people and robots handle finding and triaging casualties in simulated disasters. The nine-day final of the DARPA Triage Challenge took place across a 52-acre site in College Station. Teams searched for mannequins and actors, assessed their condition, and decided who needed help first. The winner was set to receive $1.5 million, with a total prize pool of $2.5 million.
